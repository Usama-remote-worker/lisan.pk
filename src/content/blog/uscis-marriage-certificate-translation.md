# Nikah Nama English Translation for NVC and USCIS (2026): Pakistan Marriage Documents Explained

**For a Pakistani marriage-based U.S. immigration case, prepare your official Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC) and, when the application or consular instructions call for it, your Urdu Nikah Nama with a complete certified English translation.** USCIS, the National Visa Center (NVC), and the U.S. Embassy in Islamabad handle documents at different stages, so there is **no single three-document rule that automatically applies to every case**. The safest approach is to check your filing stage and bring the marriage records requested for that stage. > **Key distinction:** The **Nikah Nama** is the marriage contract/registration record; the **MRC** is the government-issued marriage registration certificate described in the U.S. Department of State's Pakistan reciprocity guidance. A translator prepares an English version of an existing document; a translator cannot issue or replace an official MRC.

## Do NVC and USCIS require both the Nikah Nama and NADRA MRC?

**Not necessarily at every stage.** The Department of State's Pakistan reciprocity schedule identifies the **Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC)** as the marriage certificate issued through competent local government authorities. It also explains that the underlying handwritten Urdu Nikah Nama is used to obtain an MRC. The schedule does **not** itself announce a blanket policy that every NVC applicant must upload three separate marriage files. [1]

The right answer depends on what is being submitted:

| Stage or authority | Core document requirement | How to handle Urdu translation | Practical preparation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **USCIS Form I-130 spouse petition** | Marriage evidence under the current I-130 instructions; consult the specific evidence checklist and any USCIS notice | Any submitted foreign-language information requires a **full certified English translation** | Review your MRC, Nikah Nama and other marriage evidence; translate any Urdu record you submit |
| **NVC / CEAC immigrant-visa document review** | Civil documents matching the applicable reciprocity schedule and the exact CEAC document slot | NVC's general rule has a language-of-interview-country exception; read your NVC instructions and post-specific rules | Keep the official MRC ready, and a complete translated Nikah Nama if requested or useful to resolve ambiguity |
| **U.S. Embassy Islamabad immigrant-visa interview** | Interview instructions expressly list the **original marriage certificate**, English translation and photocopy for married applicants | **English translation is expressly listed** for the marriage certificate in Islamabad's interview checklist | Bring original/certified copies and any additional marriage record requested in your interview instructions |

**What people call the “three-document bundle”** is a practical preparation set: **(1) Urdu Nikah Nama, (2) certified English translation of that Nikah Nama, and (3) official MRC.** Keeping these records available can make follow-up requests easier, but do not label this a universally mandatory “NVC three-document rule.” If NVC or the embassy asks for a specific document, follow that instruction rather than a generic checklist. [1–4]

## Nikah Nama vs NADRA Marriage Registration Certificate: what is the difference?

The terms are often confused, particularly in online visa discussions.

| Point | Urdu Nikah Nama | Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The underlying marriage contract/registration form | A government-issued marriage registration certificate |
| **Typical contents** | Spouses' identities, marriage particulars, witnesses, dower (mehr) and other contractual entries | Registered particulars of the marriage in a certificate format |
| **Common language** | Often Urdu, including handwriting | May be bilingual or contain English text, depending on issuing authority |
| **Issuing or registering body** | Nikah Registrar, with registration through the relevant local authority | Relevant local government authority, such as a Union Council or other authorized body |
| **Can the other replace it?** | A translated Nikah Nama does not generate an official MRC | An MRC does not automatically reproduce every contractual detail on the Nikah Nama |
| **U.S. immigration use** | Useful where original contract details or a specific request matter | Specifically named as the Pakistani marriage certificate in Department of State reciprocity guidance |

Although people commonly say “NADRA marriage certificate,” the State Department describes the issuing authorities as **Union Councils, Municipal Corporations, the Capital Development Authority and Cantonment Boards**, as applicable. The digital certificate may be associated with NADRA systems; that does not make a translation agency the issuing authority. [1]

If the MRC is missing, obtain it from the appropriate registration authority. Translating only the Nikah Nama should not be presented as a substitute for an obtainable civil record where that record is requested.

## I-130 marriage certificate translation: what does USCIS actually require?

**USCIS Form I-130 instructions require a full English translation for any document submitted with foreign-language information.** The translator must certify that the translation is complete and accurate and that they are competent to translate into English. The instructions also specify the translator's **signature, printed name, date of signature and contact information**. [2]

For an Urdu Nikah Nama, this means the translation should address the meaningful text throughout the original—not only a typed summary of names and dates. A complete translation should cover legible printed and handwritten entries, relevant conditions, dower/mehr amounts, and visible official annotations.

A useful Nikah Nama translation checklist:

1. **Names:** Transcribe both spouses' names faithfully; identify discrepancies with passports separately rather than silently changing the source.
2. **Dates:** Preserve the exact source date and calendar; add a documented conversion only if a reviewer requests one.
3. **Mehr/dower:** Include amounts, property or conditions shown on the form.
4. **Witnesses and registrar:** Translate legible roles, names and official details.
5. **Official marks:** Identify stamps, seals and signatures in brackets where appropriate; do not invent unreadable text.
6. **Blank or illegible entries:** Mark them honestly rather than guessing their contents.
7. **All pages:** Include the complete document set, including any reverse side containing text or official markings.
8. **Certification:** Attach the signed statement covering completeness, accuracy and translator competence.

**Does a marriage translation have to look identical to the original?** USCIS requires a **full English translation**, not a pixel-for-pixel copy of the source design. A clear, traceable field-by-field layout is helpful for reviewers. A decorative replica is less important than completeness and accuracy. [2]

## What must a USCIS-certified Nikah Nama translation certificate say?

The certification needs to establish two core facts: **the translation is complete and accurate**, and **the translator is competent to translate from Urdu into English**. The current Form I-130 instructions also ask for the translator's identifying and contact details. [2]

The following is an **illustrative wording template**, not a completed or government-issued certificate:

> **TRANSLATOR'S CERTIFICATION OF ACCURACY**  
> I, **[translator's full name]**, certify that I am competent to translate from **Urdu into English** and that the attached English translation of **[document identification]** is complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge and ability.  
> **Printed name:** [name]  
> **Signature:** [signature]  
> **Date signed:** [date]  
> **Contact information:** [email, phone and/or mailing address]

The final certificate must reflect the **real translator and actual work completed**. Do not publish a filled-in sample using an invented translator identity, professional license, or signature. A genuine redacted sample can be added later only with permission and adequate removal of personal data.

### Do I need a notarized translation for USCIS?

**Notarization is not part of the general translation-certification requirement quoted in Form I-130 instructions.** A separate authority, particular case notice or requested use may impose additional formalities, so do not buy notarization or apostille solely because a translation agency advertises it as universally mandatory. For the governing filing requirement, consult the [current I-130 instructions](https://www.uscis.gov/i-130) and any case-specific request. [2]

### Can I translate my own Nikah Nama?

The regulation and Form I-130 instructions focus on a complete, accurate translation and a competent translator's certification; they do not themselves declare that every self-translation is automatically unlawful or that only a government-licensed translation agency qualifies. However, a genuinely independent translator can provide an easier-to-evaluate record and reduce disputes about competence or impartiality. If you have a special case, seek qualified immigration advice rather than relying on an absolute marketing claim.

## NVC translation rules are not identical to USCIS translation rules

**Do not treat “USCIS” and “NVC” as interchangeable.** USCIS receives petitions such as Form I-130, while NVC collects immigrant-visa documents after petition approval in applicable cases. The Department of State's general NVC instructions say translations are required for documents that are neither in English nor in the official language of the country where the applicant is applying. It also explains that translations are not required for documents in the language of the interview country. [3, 4]

That general exception matters for Urdu-language documents in Pakistan. **But Islamabad's current immigrant-visa interview instructions separately list an English translation for a married applicant's marriage certificate.** Applicants should therefore read both sets of instructions and follow the more specific instruction applying to their case; they should not assume a translation is either always unnecessary or always required for every CEAC slot. [4, 5]

For couples who want documents ready before an interview or possible additional request, obtaining a correctly certified English Nikah Nama translation can be practical even when a particular NVC upload step does not explicitly demand it.

## Is a bilingual Nikah Nama or bilingual MRC enough without translation?

**Sometimes an English-language certificate may be usable as issued; do not assume that every bilingual document automatically needs a second translation.** The relevant question is whether the document submitted contains untranslated foreign-language information and whether your specific authority requires the original contract, the civil certificate or both.

For **USCIS**, the I-130 translation instruction applies when a submitted document contains information in a foreign language. For **NVC**, check the general language exception and CEAC document instructions. For an **Islamabad interview**, the post-specific checklist asks for an English translation of the marriage certificate. [2–5]

## Step-by-step: prepare your marriage documents for I-130, NVC or an Islamabad interview

### Step 1 — Identify the current stage of your case

Are you filing **Form I-130 with USCIS**, uploading **civil documents through CEAC**, or attending an **interview at the U.S. Embassy Islamabad**? Each stage has its own instructions. Read the exact checklist or government message applicable to you.

### Step 2 — Locate the original Nikah Nama

Find every page, including any reverse side containing stamps or writing. Check whether the registrar's entries and marriage date are legible. Do not erase or edit names, witnesses, dates or dower figures to make them match a passport.

### Step 3 — Obtain or check the official MRC

Verify the certificate against the original marriage details. If an MRC has not been issued, contact the competent local government registration authority. The Department of State's reciprocity page explains the authority and obtaining procedure for Pakistani marriage certificates. [1]

### Step 4 — Resolve identity discrepancies appropriately

Compare spellings, dates of birth and other identifiers with the spouses' passports and relevant records. A translator may note a variant or explain transliteration, but **cannot legally amend an original civil record**. Ask the issuing authority or an immigration professional about material discrepancies.

### Step 5 — Obtain a complete certified translation where needed

Ask for **Urdu-to-English** translation for the U.S. document use, not Arabic translation for a Gulf authority. Make sure the signed certification includes the I-130 elements where your use is a USCIS petition. [2]

### Step 6 — Prepare the correct CEAC files

NVC's scanning guidance currently states **PDF (preferred), JPG and JPEG** are accepted, with a **4 MB maximum for an individual scanned document**. Scans should be in colour, readable, correctly oriented, and include the reverse side when it has material content. When a translation accompanies a foreign-language document, NVC says to scan the source document first and the translation second **in one file**. Always follow any newer CEAC limits shown when uploading. [6]

**Important:** This **single-file rule concerns the source document and its translation**. An MRC that is separately requested in a CEAC slot is not necessarily the same document as the underlying Urdu Nikah Nama; follow the portal's actual document categories. Do not upload a random collection of unrelated records into one slot merely because someone calls them the “three-document bundle.”

### Step 7 — Submit the documents and monitor messages

An upload may remain saved but unsubmitted. Confirm that the relevant NVC document entries have been **submitted**, then monitor **CEAC messages and response notes**. “Uploaded” and “Accepted” are not the same status. [7]

### Step 8 — Bring interview originals

For the Islamabad immigrant-visa interview, review the latest embassy checklist. Married applicants are instructed to bring the **original marriage certificate, English translation and photocopy**. Originals are not replaced by a photo on your phone. If the embassy letter asks for additional Nikah Nama pages, bring them too. [5]

## NVC rejected my Nikah Nama translation: what should I do?

First, look for the actual **CEAC response note**. A rejected document can reflect the wrong record, unclear scan, missing page, incorrect category, translation issue or another case-specific reason. The rejection status alone does **not** establish that the translator was at fault. [7]

| What the response note indicates | First corrective action |
|---|---|
| Incorrect civil document | Check the Pakistan reciprocity guidance and upload the right issuing-authority record |
| Translation incomplete or missing | Obtain a complete certified English translation for the submitted Urdu text |
| Illegible or cut-off scan | Rescan every page and relevant reverse-side marking, then verify readability |
| Incorrect document category | Select the appropriate CEAC slot and follow the exact note |
| Names or dates appear inconsistent | Compare source documents; seek corrections from issuing authority where needed |
| No clear explanation | Review CEAC messages and contact the official NVC support channel for case-specific guidance |

NVC explains that a **Rejected** document must be corrected and resubmitted; its feedback appears in the **Response Note** column. Do not respond by uploading unrelated documents or ordering every possible certification. [7]

### Does a rejected document mean the visa has been denied?

**No.** NVC document rejection means the specific submission needs attention. It is not itself a final immigrant-visa decision. Still, correct the issue as early as possible because missing or rejected documents can delay the case from becoming documentarily complete.

### Can translation errors cause a USCIS Request for Evidence (RFE)?

Incomplete or unclear evidence can lead USCIS to request more information, but no translation agency can truthfully claim that one typo automatically causes an RFE or that its work guarantees a petition will be approved. Follow the exact notice if USCIS requests additional material.

## Frequently asked questions: NVC, USCIS, Urdu Nikah Nama and MRC

### 1. Does NVC require the Urdu Nikah Nama and NADRA MRC together?

There is no universal three-document requirement in the general NVC instructions. The Department of State identifies the MRC as Pakistan's marriage certificate. Prepare the Nikah Nama and its English translation if requested by your immigration authority or useful for the case, and follow the exact CEAC slot and embassy instructions. [1, 3–5]

### 2. What is the “three-document rule” people discuss for Pakistani marriages?

It is a **practical bundle** of the original Urdu Nikah Nama, its certified English translation and the official MRC—not a universally stated NVC regulation. Different stages may request different items.

### 3. Is a certified English translation required for Form I-130?

If a document submitted to USCIS contains foreign-language information, the Form I-130 instructions require a full English translation with signed certification of accuracy, completeness and translator competence. [2]

### 4. Is the NADRA MRC the same as the Nikah Nama?

No. The MRC is the official marriage registration certificate; the Nikah Nama is the underlying marriage contract/registration record. An MRC does not necessarily contain all contract details. [1]

### 5. Can I file only the bilingual MRC?

The MRC is the document identified by the U.S. State Department's Pakistan reciprocity guidance. Whether it is sufficient for **your specific USCIS petition or NVC/consular case** depends on the request, its contents and other evidence requirements. Do not assume a general answer overrides a case-specific notice.

### 6. Does the U.S. Embassy Islamabad require an English marriage-certificate translation?

Its current immigrant-visa interview checklist states that married applicants should bring the original marriage certificate, English translation and photocopy. Recheck the version attached to your own interview appointment. [5]

### 7. Is Urdu acceptable without translation at NVC?

NVC's general rule includes an exception for documents in the official language of the country where the interview is held. But USCIS has a different translation rule, and Islamabad's interview checklist separately requests English translation of marriage certificates. Apply the instruction for the stage and post involved. [2–5]

### 8. Does a USCIS translator need to be a U.S. government-approved translator?

Form I-130 instructions require certification of competence, completeness and accuracy. They do not prescribe a universal USCIS-issued translator license. The agency or translator must still make a truthful signed certification. [2]

### 9. Does a USCIS translation need notarization or apostille?

The general I-130 translation instruction does not call for either as a routine translation requirement. Specific requests and other legal uses may differ. [2]

### 10. What if a Nikah Nama is handwritten and difficult to read?

Provide a high-quality scan of all pages. The translator should identify illegible entries rather than inventing text. A corrected or clearer record may be needed from the proper authority if material details cannot be verified.

### 11. Can the translator change a surname to match the passport?

No. The translation should faithfully represent the source. A documented transliteration note may clarify a difference, but a translation is not a substitute for correcting an official civil record.

### 12. Can I upload my Nikah Nama and English translation in one PDF to CEAC?

Yes, NVC instructs applicants with certified translations to scan the foreign-language source first, then the translation in a single file. Follow current file-size rules and place other documents in their assigned slots. [4, 6]

### 13. My CEAC document status says “Rejected.” Is my visa refused?

Not by that status alone. Read the **Response Note**, correct the requested item and resubmit. The NVC website explains what “Rejected” means for document review. [7]

### 14. How much is Nikah Nama translation for NVC in Pakistan?

It depends on document length, handwriting, language, certification and delivery. Request a quote after the full pages are reviewed. Check [LISAN.PK pricing](/pricing) for published standard-service categories; complex marriage contracts may be priced separately.

### 15. Can I request Nikah Nama translation while living in the United States?

Yes. You can request an assessment online using clear scans of the Pakistani record. Confirm whether the receiving authority needs a digital certification, additional original records or any particular delivery format.

### 16. Can LISAN.PK guarantee NVC acceptance or a successful visa outcome?

No. LISAN.PK can provide a certified translation and address documented translation issues, but NVC, USCIS and consular officers decide document sufficiency and immigration outcomes. No independent translation provider can guarantee a visa or eliminate every possible request for additional evidence.
