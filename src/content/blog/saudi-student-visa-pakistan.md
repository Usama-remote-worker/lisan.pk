# Saudi Student Visa for Pakistani Students 2026/27: From Scholarship Acceptance to Visa & Travel

A Pakistani student normally begins the Saudi educational-visa stage after receiving final acceptance from the Saudi educational institution. For Study in Saudi applicants, the official student manual says the educational-visa information is completed upon final acceptance, while Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs states that the study-visa request is initiated by the Saudi educational institution for a person outside the Kingdom.

In simple terms:

# **Scholarship Application**
↓
# **University Review**
↓
# **University Approval**
↓
# **Ministry / Final Processing**
↓
# **Final Acceptance**
↓
# **Study in Saudi Visa Information**
↓
# **Saudi Institutional / Visa Processing**
↓
# **Medical, Insurance & Other Requirements**
↓
# **Visa Issuance**
↓
# **Travel to Saudi Arabia**

Do not treat:

> **University approval**

as the same thing as:

> **Saudi student visa approval.**

They are separate stages.

This guide explains the complete process for a Pakistani student from scholarship acceptance until departure for Saudi Arabia.

# What Type of Visa Does a Saudi Scholarship Student Need?

## Direct Answer

**Bachelor's, Master's and PhD students normally fall under Saudi Arabia's long-term educational visa because these are long-duration academic programs.**

Study in Saudi currently distinguishes between:

### Short-Term Educational Visa
For study, training or short programs lasting up to six months.  
**Current validity:** 6 months, with the possibility of extension for another six months.

### Long-Term Educational Visa
For long-duration academic study or research, including degrees such as:
- Bachelor's
- Master's
- PhD  
**Current validity:** 1 year, and Study in Saudi states that it can be renewed annually.

---

# Saudi Long-Term Educational Visa Requirements

## Direct Answer

**Study in Saudi currently lists several core conditions for the long-term educational visa, including a valid passport, university acceptance, financial support or guarantee, medical report, medical insurance and language proficiency.**

The official requirements currently include:
- applicant generally 16 years or older,
- passport valid for at least 6 months from the intended date of entry,
- acceptance from a licensed and accredited Saudi educational institution,
- proof of ability to cover study/living expenses or a financial guarantee,
- written guardian consent for applicants under 18,
- medical report confirming freedom from infectious diseases,
- medical insurance for the student and accompanying dependants,
- proof of proficiency in the language of study.

Applicants studying the Arabic language are exempt from the language-proficiency condition specified for the visa.

# Does a Fully Funded Scholarship Satisfy the Financial Requirement?

## Direct Answer

**A scholarship or financial guarantee may satisfy the educational visa's financial-support requirement, depending on the official award and instructions issued for the student.**

Study in Saudi allows either:
- proof of financial ability,
- or a financial guarantee.

A fully funded scholarship can therefore be relevant evidence of financial support.

However:

> **Do not prepare a random bank statement simply because an unofficial website says every scholarship student needs one. Follow the documents requested for your specific scholarship and visa case.**

# Is University Approval the Same as Final Acceptance?

## Direct Answer

**Not necessarily.**

The Study in Saudi FAQ explains that after a student submits the academic application:
1. the selected university reviews it,
2. after university approval,
3. the application is processed by the Saudi Ministry of Education.

Therefore:

> **University Approval ≠ Visa Issued**  
> and  
> **University Approval may not yet equal final completion of every Saudi approval stage.**

Do not buy a flight ticket merely because someone tells you: "Your university approved you." Wait for the next official instruction.

# What Happens After I Submit the Study in Saudi Visa Information?

## Direct Answer

**The visa information is sent to the university where you have been accepted.**

The official Study in Saudi manual shows a confirmation message stating that the educational-visa data has been submitted for study at the relevant university.

This is an important point:

> **Submitting your visa data does not mean the visa has already been issued. It means the educational-visa information has entered the next processing stage.**

# Step 3: The Saudi Educational Institution Handles the Institutional Visa Request

## Direct Answer

**Saudi MOFA defines the study-visa request as a service used by a Saudi educational institution to request a person from outside Saudi Arabia for study.**

This means the study visa is institution-linked. It is not simply:  
*Student visits a visa website → chooses "student visa" → receives visa independently.*

The Saudi educational institution is involved in the official request/processing chain.

# Is Wafid Used in Pakistan?

## Direct Answer

**Pakistan is currently included in the Wafid program administered under the Gulf Health Council for people travelling to GCC states for work or residence.**

Wafid currently lists Pakistan among its participating countries and allows applicants to book medical examinations through approved centers.

However:

> **A Saudi scholarship student should use Wafid only when the official visa instructions for the student's case direct them to the applicable medical route. Do not book a Wafid medical merely because another student or agent says so.**

---

# How Does Wafid Appointment Booking Work?

Where Wafid applies, its official process currently involves:
1. Visit Wafid.
2. Select Book an Appointment.
3. Choose the country.
4. Choose the city.
5. Choose the destination country.
6. Enter the required personal/passport information.
7. Pay the applicable booking fee.
8. Receive the medical examination slip.
9. Attend the assigned medical center.

Wafid states that the medical center is assigned automatically based on the selected city and country. Applicants cannot simply choose any clinic they prefer.

---

# Do Not Book the Medical Too Early

Wafid's regulations state that the medical certificate issued by accredited centers has a limited validity period for visa purposes. That is another reason to wait for the correct visa stage. A medical completed too early can expire before visa processing finishes.

---

# What Does the Saudi Educational Visa Require Medically?

Study in Saudi requires a **medical report showing freedom from infectious diseases** for long-term educational visas.

The exact tests and approved medical process should be taken from your visa instructions, the official medical provider, or the Saudi authority handling your case. Avoid using an old medical checklist from a previous intake as your only source.
