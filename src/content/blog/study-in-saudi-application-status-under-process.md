You submitted your Saudi scholarship application. Now the portal shows: **Under Process**, **Returned**, **Rejected**, or **Accepted**.

What does your **Study in Saudi application status** actually mean, and should you do anything?

This is one of the most important stages of the Saudi scholarship process because the answer is not always *"just wait."*

* Some statuses mean your application is still being reviewed.
* Some may require immediate action.
* Some indicate that the application was not approved at a particular stage.

And if your application has already been submitted, you should also be careful about trying to change information because the official Study in Saudi FAQ states that **address and contact details can be edited, while other submitted application data cannot normally be changed**.

The official Study in Saudi process also involves more than one stage: after submission, the application goes through the university selected by the applicant, and following university approval it is processed by the Saudi Ministry of Education.

This guide explains the main **Study in Saudi status meanings in 2026**, how long Under Process may take, what to do if your application is returned or rejected, and when you should contact official support.

# Study in Saudi Application Status: Quick Guide

| Status | What It Generally Means | Can You Edit? | What Should You Do? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | Application has been submitted into the review process | Limited | Monitor your account |
| **Under Process** | Application is being reviewed / processed by the relevant institution | Limited | Wait and monitor |
| **Returned** | Application has been sent back and may require action | Depends on what portal allows | Read reason and correct what is requested |
| **Rejected** | Application was not approved at a review stage | Usually not | Check reason and assess alternatives |
| **Accepted / Approved** | Application has received approval at the displayed stage | Usually no need to edit | Follow next portal instructions |
| **Complete Visa Data** | Further educational-visa information is required | Follow portal instructions | Complete requested visa information |

The portal's **My Applications / My Requests** section shows applications and their statuses, and applicants can click the application number to view details. The exact status and instructions inside **your own account** should always take priority over generic descriptions online.

# How to Check Your Study in Saudi Application Status

Applicants can track submitted requests through the **My Applications / My Requests** area following these steps:

1. **Log in** to your Study in Saudi account.
2. Open **My Services → My Applications / My Requests**.
3. Find your submitted application (displays application number, application type, program, status, and creation date).
4. **Click the application number** to open full details.

Do not rely only on email—check the portal itself regularly while your application is active.

---

# What Does “Submitted” Mean on Study in Saudi?

**Submitted** means you have completed the application and sent it into the official review process.

It does not mean:
* Your scholarship has been approved
* The university has accepted you
* The Ministry has approved you
* Your visa has been issued

The official Study in Saudi FAQ explains that after submission, the application first passes through the university selected by the student. If the university approves it, the application then moves to Ministry of Education processing.

> **Submitted = application received into the process, not scholarship awarded.**

---

# Can I Edit My Application After It Is Submitted?

This is where applicants should be particularly careful.

The current official Study in Saudi FAQ states that **address and contact details may be modified, while other submitted application information cannot normally be edited.**

That means you should not assume you can later freely change passport details, qualification, degree, marks, CGPA, or academic data. If you experience technical errors or status lockouts, refer to our [Study in Saudi Portal Problems & Application Error Fixes Guide](/blog/study-in-saudi-portal-problems-errors).

# What Does Study in Saudi “Under Process” Mean?

**Under Process means your application is still within the official review/processing workflow.**

On the Study in Saudi system, this status indicates the application is being handled by the relevant educational institution. The workflow is:

**Application submitted → Selected university reviews it → University approval → Ministry of Education processes it.**

Seeing **Under Process** does not mean your application has been rejected. It is a **neutral processing status** indicating that review is ongoing.

---

# How Long Does Study in Saudi Stay Under Process?

There is **no single official number of days** published in the Study in Saudi FAQ for scholarship applications to remain Under Process.

Because there are multiple stages (university review followed by Ministry processing), avoid believing unverified claims like *"Under Process means an answer in 3 days"* or *"After 30 days you are rejected."*

---

# What Should I Do If My Application Has Been Under Process for a Long Time?

A long Under Process period by itself does not prove a problem. First check:
* Has the status changed?
* Is there a note inside the application?
* Has the university requested anything?
* Did you receive an email or portal prompt?

If nothing has changed and no action is requested, continue monitoring your account.

---

# What Does “Returned” Mean on Study in Saudi?

If your application status shows **Returned**, do not assume you have been permanently rejected. A returned application should first be treated as an application that requires you to **check the portal for further action**.

### What to do:
1. **Open Application Details:** Read the actual return reason or comment.
2. **Identify What is Requested:** Missing information, corrected document, or specific action.
3. **Correct Only What Is Requested:** Do not alter unrelated information.
4. **Review Before Resubmitting:** Check the corrected field or scan carefully.
5. **If Field is Locked:** Contact official Study in Saudi technical support.

> **Returned = investigate what action the portal requests.**  
> **Rejected = investigate why the application was not approved.**

---

# What Does Study in Saudi “Rejected” Mean?

If your status is **Rejected**, the application has not been approved at the stage reflected in your portal. Rejection can relate to academic eligibility, degree relevance, program requirements, university decision, document issues, or scholarship availability.

### If your application is rejected:
1. **Read the Portal Reason:** Check for any specific remarks.
2. **Determine Cause:** Check whether it is an academic criteria issue or a document error.
3. **Explore Alternatives:** Look for other fully funded options in our catalog of [Saudi Arabia Fully Funded Scholarship Programs 2026/27](/blog/saudi-fully-funded-scholarship-programs-2026).
4. **Beware of Fraud:** Never pay private agents claiming they can "reverse" an official university decision.

# What Does “Accepted” Mean on Study in Saudi?

An **Accepted / Approved** status indicates approval at the stage displayed in your account. However, because Study in Saudi involves multiple stages (university approval followed by Ministry processing), follow every subsequent prompt carefully.

Look for portal prompts regarding:
* Educational visa data completion
* Admission confirmation
* Passport & medical verification
