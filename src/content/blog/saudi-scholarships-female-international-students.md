# Saudi Scholarships for Female International Students 2026/27: Universities, Programs & Eligibility

Yes, **female international students can receive scholarships to study in Saudi Arabia**.

But there is an important detail that many scholarship websites miss:

> **Female eligibility must be checked at the university and program level.**

A Saudi university may accept international women generally while some of its individual programs remain:

- male only,
- female only,
- or open to both genders.

Saudi Arabia's official Study in Saudi scholarship guidance itself defines scholarships as study seats available to **male or female non-Saudi students** studying in Saudi higher-education institutions.

At the same time, individual universities apply their own admission rules.

For example:

- **Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University** has a dedicated external scholarship route specifically for non-Saudi women.
- **King Saud University** currently lists several scholarship programs available to both genders and at least one program specifically marked female.
- **Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University** officially describes internal and external scholarship routes for both male and female students.
- Some universities may enroll female international students internally but have different historical patterns for external scholarships.

Therefore, the correct question is not:

> **"Can women get Saudi scholarships?"**

The answer to that is yes.

The better question is:

> **"Which university, degree and program currently accepts female international scholarship applicants?"**

This guide answers that question using current Saudi government and university sources.

As of **3 November 2026**, the official Study in Saudi program-search page currently states:

That means this guide identifies **verified female scholarship routes and historically/currently documented eligible programs**, but it does **not** claim that every program discussed below is open for application today.

Always verify the live Study in Saudi program listing before submitting.

# Can Female International Students Study in Saudi Arabia on Scholarship?

Yes.

Saudi Arabia's official Study in Saudi full-scholarship page explicitly defines the scholarship beneficiary as a **male or female non-Saudi student** admitted to a Saudi higher-education institution.

Saudi Ministry of Education guidance also describes international scholarship opportunities for students from countries around the world and distinguishes between:

- **external scholarships** for non-Saudis applying from outside Saudi Arabia,
- and **internal scholarships** for non-Saudis already residing legally inside the Kingdom.

For a Pakistani woman living in Pakistan, the relevant route will generally be an:

# **External Scholarship**

provided she satisfies the university and program requirements.

# 1. Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University

## The Most Important Saudi University for Female International Scholarship Applicants

**Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University (PNU)** is a women-focused university in Riyadh and has a dedicated official external-scholarship page for **non-Saudi female students residing outside Saudi Arabia**.

This is one of the clearest official female scholarship routes in Saudi Arabia.

The university's current scholarship page explicitly states that Saudi Arabia offers scholarships for non-Saudi women from outside the Kingdom to study at PNU.

---

# Princess Nourah External Scholarship Age Requirement

For the current Bachelor's and Arabic Language Institute route, PNU states that the applicant must generally be:

# **17–25 years old**

for:

- Bachelor's programs,
- and the Arabic Language Teaching Institute diploma.

The university also requires a high-school diploma or equivalent.

---

# Princess Nourah Academic Requirements

PNU currently distinguishes between applicants from:

### Arab countries

and

### Non-Arab countries.

This distinction is particularly important for Pakistani applicants.

---

# Female Applicants From Arab Countries

PNU's current official page states that applicants from Arab countries applying to:

### Science and Business Administration colleges

must pass the approved tests and satisfy the admission criteria of the program.

For:

### Humanities programs

the university currently states a high-school average requirement of at least:

# **95/100**

for the route described on the scholarship page.

---

# Female Applicants From Non-Arab Countries

This is especially relevant to Pakistani applicants.

PNU currently states that applicants from non-Arab countries must achieve:

# **B2 level in the Hamza Academic Arabic Language Proficiency Test**

for the route described on its scholarship page.

The university provides an exemption where the applicant:

- completed high school in English,
- **in an English-speaking country**,
- and applies to a program taught in English.

### What This Means for Pakistani Applicants

Pakistan is not an Arab country.

Therefore, a Pakistani female applicant should **not assume that studying in an English-medium Pakistani school automatically removes PNU's Arabic-language requirement**.

The exemption published by PNU specifically refers to completing high school in an **English-speaking country**.

Pakistani applicants should therefore check the exact current language rule before applying.

---

# What If a Female Applicant Does Not Meet PNU's Arabic Requirement?

PNU states that applicants who do not satisfy the required Arabic proficiency may potentially be admitted to the:

# **Diploma Program at the Institute of Arabic Language Teaching**

but importantly:

> admission to the Arabic-language diploma does **not guarantee progression to a Bachelor's degree**.

That distinction should not be ignored.

---

# Which PNU Programs Are Available to Female International Students?

PNU's current external-scholarship page states that, for the route described there, admission is available to programs except **health programs**, subject to the university's published criteria.

Students should still check the live program list because:

**university eligibility ≠ every program being open in every intake.**

---

# Princess Nourah Scholarship Benefits

PNU currently confirms a substantial external-scholarship package including:

- full healthcare during study,
- university housing,
- subsidized meals,
- monthly stipend equivalent to Saudi students,
- annual round-trip ticket,
- reimbursement for initial travel to Saudi Arabia,
- two-month preparation allowance,
- three-month graduation allowance for shipping books.

For students admitted for the **1448 AH academic year**, PNU also states that the university covers the educational-visa fee.

This makes PNU one of the most clearly documented female scholarship routes in Saudi Arabia.

---

# Princess Nourah Required Documents

PNU's current external-scholarship page lists documents including:

- high-school diploma,
- transcript,
- valid passport,
- certificate of good conduct or eligible recommendations,
- criminal-record clearance,
- medical report.

The university also currently requires academic documents to be authenticated and translated into Arabic according to the published instructions.

Applicants should follow the exact current PNU instructions rather than applying a general Saudi document checklist.

---

# Is PNU Acceptance Immediately Final?

No.

PNU explicitly states:

> **Online approval represents preliminary admission. Final admission is not complete until Ministry of Education approval is issued.**

This is important because students sometimes interpret an initial university approval as:

- final scholarship approval,
- or visa approval.

Those are not the same thing.

# Important Note About Guardian / Mahram Requirements

This issue must be handled carefully because different official pages do not always present the rule in the same way.

IMSIU's current international-student admission page still states that a **female scholarship student must have a mahram according to the applicable regulations**, with specified conditions regarding that person's scholarship or legal residence.

Saudi Ministry's general public-university scholarship guidance also contains similar wording.

However, current PNU's female external-scholarship page does not list this as one of the admission conditions shown on that page.

Therefore LISAN.PK does **not** make a blanket statement such as:

> "Every female international student must have a mahram."

Nor should we state:

> "No Saudi university requires a mahram anymore."

The accurate advice is:

> **Female applicants should check the current university-specific and visa-stage instructions applicable to their own scholarship route.**

Where an official university currently lists the requirement, follow that university's rules.

# 5. University of Tabuk: An Important Cautionary Example

The University of Tabuk illustrates why female scholarship information must be checked carefully.

Its current external-scholarship page confirms that the university serves international students and offers internal and external scholarships.

The same page provides actual historical enrollment statistics for **1445 AH**.

For internal scholarship students, it reports female students at both:
- Bachelor's,
- and Master's level.

But in the external scholarship statistics published for that year, the university reports:
- **139 male external scholarship students**
- **0 female external scholarship students**.

That does **not necessarily prove** that women can never obtain an external scholarship at the University of Tabuk.

But it means LISAN.PK should not currently publish:

> "University of Tabuk definitely offers external scholarships for women"

without stronger current intake-level evidence.

This is the type of distinction that keeps scholarship information trustworthy.
