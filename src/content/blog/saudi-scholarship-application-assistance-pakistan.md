# Saudi Scholarship Application Assistance Pakistan: Study in Saudi Complete Support 2026

Applying for a Saudi scholarship from Pakistan does not always mean you need someone to manage your complete application.

Some students have already selected a university, chosen a program, checked their eligibility, and only need help preparing documents or translations. Other applicants are still trying to figure out which programs match their profile, whether they meet age/CGPA limits, if IELTS is required, or how to navigate the portal.

For this reason, LISAN.PK separates its support into two transparent service tiers:

## Package 1 — Documents & Translation
For students who already know where they want to apply and mainly need help preparing, checking, or translating their academic documents.

## Package 2 — Complete Application Support
For applicants who need end-to-end guidance including profile assessment, program selection, eligibility review, language requirements, document checks, portal submission guidance, and final application review.
