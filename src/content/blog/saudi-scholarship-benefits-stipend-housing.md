# Saudi Scholarship Benefits 2026/27: Stipend, Housing, Flights, Healthcare & Tuition

What does a **fully funded Saudi scholarship** actually cover?

Students often see claims such as:

> "Free tuition + SAR 1,000 monthly stipend + free accommodation + free flights."

The problem is that scholarship websites frequently combine benefits from different universities and present them as if every international student in Saudi Arabia receives exactly the same package.

That is not accurate.

Saudi Arabia's official scholarship system distinguishes among:

- **full/free scholarships,**
- **partial scholarships,**
- and **paid study opportunities.**

A full scholarship can include study costs, financial assistance and additional student benefits, but the exact package can depend on:

- the university,
- degree level,
- scholarship route,
- student category,
- and the regulations applied by the institution.

The official Study in Saudi platform states that full scholarships cover **study costs and the services provided by the university or educational institution**, together with financial assistance for international students.

Saudi Ministry of Education guidance also identifies benefits that external scholarship students may receive, including:

- healthcare,
- an arrival financial allowance,
- housing,
- university student benefits,
- and annual travel tickets,

subject to the applicable regulations.

The key phrase is:

> **may receive / according to applicable regulations.**

Therefore, this guide does not promise every student the same monthly stipend or identical housing arrangement.

Instead, we separate:

1. **Saudi government-level scholarship benefits**
2. **University-specific confirmed benefits**
3. **Benefits that vary**
4. **Benefits students often incorrectly assume are guaranteed**

---

For a **full external scholarship**, official Saudi sources identify or support benefits that can include:

The safest principle is:

# **Full scholarship does not mean every university provides every benefit in exactly the same form.**

# 1. Does a Saudi Full Scholarship Cover Tuition?

For a genuine **full scholarship**, yes—the official Study in Saudi description states that full scholarships cover the **costs of study**, including services provided by the university or educational institution.

This is the most fundamental benefit.

A student receiving a full scholarship should therefore distinguish it from:

### Partial Scholarship

The institution may provide only some benefits.

For example:

- study seat only,
- or study seat plus housing.

### Paid Study

The student is responsible for tuition or other applicable program fees.

Saudi Ministry guidance officially distinguishes these three categories.

---

# 2. Do Saudi Scholarship Students Receive a Monthly Stipend?

Many full scholarship students do receive a **monthly financial allowance**, but there is an important warning:

> **There is no single monthly stipend amount that should be advertised as the universal Saudi scholarship stipend for every university and every student.**

Study in Saudi describes full scholarships as including financial assistance but does not publish one universal Saudi-wide monthly figure on its full-scholarship page.

Individual universities publish their own stipend information.

---

# **It depends on the university and scholarship program.**

# What Does "Fully Funded" Actually Mean in Saudi Arabia?

Based on Study in Saudi's official description:

> A full scholarship covers the costs of study and university/institutional services and provides financial assistance to international students.

But "fully funded" should **not** be interpreted as:

- unlimited personal spending money,
- guaranteed private accommodation,
- unlimited flights,
- family travel expenses,
- every visa-related expense,
- every document cost,
- or every student's living costs being identical.

The actual scholarship package is governed by the institution and applicable regulations.

# Full Scholarship vs Partial Scholarship

This distinction can completely change the student's financial planning.

Saudi Ministry guidance says:

### Full / Free Scholarship

The student receives the full scholarship benefits under the applicable framework.

### Partial Scholarship

The institution provides only some benefits.

The Ministry gives examples such as:

- **study seat only**
- or **study seat + accommodation**

### Paid Study

The student pays the applicable fees.

Therefore, before accepting an offer, check exactly what is written under:

**Scholarship Type / Study Type / Funding**

> **International student admission does not automatically mean free study.**

**USD 20,000 per year**

### PhD

**USD 25,000–30,000 per year**

These are:

# **KAUST Fellowship figures**

They are **not Saudi Arabia's universal scholarship stipend rates**.

KAUST operates its own graduate admissions and funding model.

## Scholarship Program
A standard external scholarship may differ from a research fellowship.

## Additional Research Funding
Some graduate students may receive extra research-related support.

## University-Specific Programs
Special initiatives may have their own funding.

## Old Information
Some websites repeat stipend figures that may refer to older rules or different programs.

Therefore:

> **Never compare stipend figures without identifying which university, degree and scholarship route the number belongs to.**

# Can a Saudi Scholarship Cover Family Members?

Do not assume your scholarship automatically covers all dependents.

Saudi Ministry guidance states that healthcare may be provided to family members brought to reside with the student **where applicable regulations require/provide for it**.

That does not establish universal coverage of:

- family airfare,
- housing upgrades,
- school fees,
- spouse living expenses,
- or all dependent costs.

If you plan to bring dependents, ask your university what your specific scholarship and visa route covers.

---

# Is Medical Insurance the Same as Scholarship Healthcare?

Not always.

For long-term educational visas, Study in Saudi separately lists medical insurance among the visa requirements.

Meanwhile, universities may provide scholarship students access to university healthcare services.

Therefore:

**visa insurance requirement**

and

**university healthcare benefit**

should not automatically be treated as identical.

Follow the instructions issued for your visa and scholarship.

---

# Does the Scholarship Pay for Document Translation?

Do not assume so.

Document translation is normally part of the **application preparation process**, which happens before the applicant becomes a scholarship student.

Unless the exact university or scholarship explicitly states otherwise, applicants should expect to arrange required:

- document preparation,
- translation,
- authentication,
- passport,
- and application-related expenses

themselves.

Scholarship benefits generally begin after acceptance/enrollment according to the applicable program rules.

---

# Does the Scholarship Pay for IELTS or TOEFL?

There is no Saudi-wide official rule stating that every scholarship reimburses applicants for their pre-admission English test.

IELTS/TOEFL can be an **application requirement**, not a scholarship benefit.

Do not assume your test fee will be refunded.

---

# Does the Scholarship Pay for Attestation?

Again, do not assume it.

Saudi guidance may require authenticated certificates, but that does not automatically mean the university pays your country's document-authentication fees.

Applicants should treat pre-application and pre-visa document costs separately unless the scholarship explicitly confirms reimbursement.

# Should You Choose a Saudi University Based on the Highest Stipend?

Usually not.

A larger stipend is attractive, but university selection should primarily consider:

- program fit,
- academic quality,
- research compatibility,
- admission requirements,
- scholarship type,
- language,
- long-term career value.

## Does University of Tabuk provide accommodation?

## Does a Saudi scholarship include healthcare?

## Does a Saudi scholarship include flight tickets?

Saudi Ministry guidance lists annual travel tickets subject to applicable regulations among external scholarship benefits.

## Do I receive a flight every year?

Several universities explicitly confirm annual or academic-year round-trip tickets, but students must follow each institution's rules.

## Does the scholarship include the first flight to Saudi Arabia?

This varies. PNU, for example, currently states that it reimburses the initial ticket for eligible external scholarship students. Other universities may follow different arrangements.

## Do scholarship students receive an arrival allowance?

Saudi Ministry guidance identifies an arrival financial reward as a possible scholarship benefit. Some universities, including PNU and UQU, specify a two-month preparation allowance.

## Are meals free?

Not universally. University of Tabuk states that scholarship students receive free meals, while KAU, PNU and UQU describe subsidized or discounted meal arrangements.

## Do Master's and PhD students get extra benefits?

Some institutions offer additional postgraduate support. KAU, for example, lists thesis-printing and graduation-related allowances.

## Does the scholarship cover my family?

Do not assume complete dependent coverage. Saudi Ministry guidance states that healthcare can extend to family members in applicable circumstances, but other family benefits depend on regulations and the university.

## Does the scholarship pay for IELTS?

There is no universal official rule saying Saudi scholarships reimburse applicants for IELTS or TOEFL.

## Does the scholarship pay for document translation?

Do not assume so. Translation is generally part of pre-admission application preparation unless the specific scholarship says otherwise.

## Does the scholarship pay for attestation?

Not automatically. Authentication may be required, but applicants should check who bears the cost.

## Does fully funded mean I need no personal money?

No. Students can still have personal and pre-departure expenses.

## Is KAUST funding different?

Yes. KAUST operates a separate graduate fellowship. Its current funding page lists full tuition, stipend, accommodation, health insurance and relocation support, with an estimated fellowship value of USD 70,000–80,000 annually depending on circumstances.
