# What Is a Fully Funded Scholarship in Saudi Arabia?

A fully funded scholarship generally means that the student receives financial support covering the main cost of studying at the institution.

Depending on the university and scholarship regulations, support may include benefits such as:

* Tuition fee coverage
* University services
* Financial support or monthly allowance
* Accommodation or housing support
* Healthcare
* Travel-related benefits
* Arrival or settlement support
* Other university benefits available under the scholarship

The exact package can vary between universities and programs.

Therefore, students should never assume that every Saudi scholarship provides exactly the same financial benefits.

There are also **partial scholarships and paid study opportunities**, so applicants specifically searching for full funding should confirm that the selected opportunity is classified as a full scholarship.
