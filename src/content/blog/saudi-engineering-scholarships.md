# Saudi Engineering Scholarships 2026/27: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Petroleum & More

**Yes. International students can study engineering in Saudi Arabia through fully funded and other scholarship routes at Bachelor's, Master's and PhD level.** Verified options include Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Petroleum, Aerospace, Industrial, Materials, Environmental, Architectural and other engineering disciplines.

The strongest currently verified routes include **KFUPM, King Saud University, King Abdulaziz University and KAUST**, but the funding model differs: some are fully funded scholarships, some are university scholarship routes, and some programs may only be tuition-free or partially funded.

The most important rule is:

> **Engineering program exists ≠ international scholarship exists ≠ fully funded ≠ currently open.**

Always verify all four.

# Fully Funded vs Tuition-Free vs Partial Engineering Scholarship

These terms are not interchangeable:

## Fully Funded
May include: tuition, stipend, housing, healthcare, travel, other benefits. Examples: **KAUST Fellowship**, qualifying **KFUPM full scholarship**, or **KAU external scholarship**.

## Tuition-Free
Means tuition may be waived. It does **not automatically prove** that the student also receives housing, stipend, or flights.

## Partial Scholarship
Only part of the cost is funded. Do not describe **50% tuition support** as a **fully funded scholarship.**
