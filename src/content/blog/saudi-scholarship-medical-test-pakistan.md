# Is a Medical Test Required for a Saudi Student Visa?

## Direct Answer

**Yes, for the long-term educational visa.**

Study in Saudi currently states that applicants for long-term educational visas must provide:

> a medical report showing freedom from infectious diseases.

The same official conditions also include:

- applicant generally aged **16 or older**,

- passport valid for at least six months from intended entry,

- acceptance from an accredited Saudi educational institution,

- financial ability or financial guarantee,

- guardian consent for applicants under 18,

- medical insurance,

- language proficiency for the language of study, except Arabic-language study.

---

# Which Saudi Students Need the Long-Term Educational Visa?

The long-term educational visa is intended for longer academic study such as:

- Bachelor's

- Master's

- PhD

- long-term research programs.

Therefore, most international scholarship students studying a full university degree will fall under the long-term educational-visa route.

---

# Does a Short-Term Educational Visa Require the Same Medical?

## Direct Answer

**The Study in Saudi page does not list the same medical-report requirement under its short-term visa conditions.**

The short-term visa is intended for programs such as:

- short study,

- training,

- language programs,

- exchange programs,

generally lasting no more than six months.

Do not automatically apply long-term degree requirements to every short educational program.

---

# When Should a Saudi Scholarship Student Do the Medical?

## Direct Answer

**Do the medical when your scholarship process reaches the appropriate visa stage and you receive the applicable instructions.**

The medical is part of the:

# Visa / post-acceptance stage

not the:

# Initial scholarship application stage.

Doing it too early can create unnecessary problems because:

- the required provider may differ,

- the required format may differ,

- the medical may expire,

- your case may not yet have reached visa processing.

---

# Why You Should Not Do the Medical Too Early

If your eventual process uses Wafid, its regulations state that the medical certificate issued by an accredited center is valid for:

# **2 months**

for visa issuance or approval purposes.

This is one of the strongest reasons not to rush.

If you complete a medical months before your visa case is ready, the certificate may no longer be valid when required.

# What Is Wafid?

## Direct Answer

**Wafid is an official Gulf Health Council program for medical examinations of people travelling to GCC countries for work or residence. Pakistan is currently included in the Wafid program.**

The GCC countries include Saudi Arabia.

Wafid provides:

- appointment booking,

- accredited medical-center information,

- medical examination records,

- online medical-result checking.

---

# Is GAMCA the Same as Wafid?

## Direct Answer

**Wafid is the current Gulf Health Council system students should refer to when the official process requires the GCC medical route.**

Many Pakistani applicants still use the older term:

# **GAMCA medical**

when discussing Gulf medical examinations.

Today, the official online platform is:

# **Wafid**

Therefore, old guides referring only to “GAMCA” should be checked against the current Wafid system.

---

# Is Wafid Mandatory for Every Pakistani Saudi Scholarship Student?

## Direct Answer

**Do not state that it is universally mandatory unless your current visa instructions specifically require it.**

Study in Saudi's long-term educational visa rules state that the student must provide:

# **a medical report showing freedom from infectious diseases**

but that page does not itself specify:

> “Every Pakistani student must use Wafid.”

Meanwhile, Wafid officially covers Pakistan and Saudi-bound people entering GCC residence-related processes.

Therefore, the correct advice is:

> **Use Wafid when your Saudi university, visa process or authorized visa instructions direct you to Wafid/Gulf Health Council medical processing.**

Do not book merely because another student used it.

If your visa instructions require Wafid, the official Wafid process is straightforward.

## Step 1

Go to the official Wafid appointment system.

## Step 2

Choose:

- examination country: **Pakistan**

- city

- destination: **Saudi Arabia**

## Step 3

Enter your personal information carefully, including:

- first name,

- last name,

- nationality,

- gender,

- marital status,

- passport number,

- passport issue date,

- passport issue place,

- passport expiry date,

- visa type,

- email,

- phone number.

## Step 4

Select the appointment type.

## Step 5

Pay the appointment fee.

## Step 6

Download/generate your medical examination slip.

## Step 7

Attend the assigned or selected accredited medical center.

Wafid's official FAQ warns applicants to carefully check all entered passport and personal data before submission.

# What Happens at the Medical Examination?

## Direct Answer

**The purpose of the medical is to determine whether the applicant meets the health requirements applicable to the visa/residence route.**

Study in Saudi specifically requires a report confirming freedom from infectious diseases for long-term educational visas.

Where the Wafid process applies, its regulations address medical fitness through:

- clinical examination,

- laboratory testing,

- radiology/chest imaging,

- medical history and health assessment.

The exact testing process should be completed by the approved medical center.

Students should not self-order tests based only on an online checklist.

---

# What Diseases Does Wafid Screen For?

## Direct Answer

Wafid's medical-fitness regulations list several infectious conditions that can affect medical fitness, including:

- HIV,

- Hepatitis B,

- Hepatitis C,

- malaria / microfilaria,

- leprosy,

- tuberculosis-related or other specified abnormal chest X-ray findings.

Its regulations also contain criteria related to certain serious non-infectious medical conditions.

### Important

These are Wafid program medical-fitness rules.

Do not automatically interpret every Wafid work/residence rule as a separate Study in Saudi scholarship admission rule.

The scholarship requirement and the visa medical requirement are separate from academic admission.

# Does Saudi Scholarship Admission Require You to Be Medically Fit?

Saudi universities may include medical-fitness requirements in scholarship/admission rules.

Separately, Study in Saudi requires the long-term educational-visa applicant to submit a medical report showing freedom from infectious diseases.

That means medical fitness can appear at:

- university admission,

- visa,

- or both stages,

depending on the route.

## Is a chest X-ray part of Wafid medical assessment?

Wafid's fitness regulations specifically include chest-X-ray findings among its medical-fitness criteria.

## How long is Wafid medical valid?

Its regulations state **two months** for visa issuance/approval purposes.

## Can I check my medical online?

Yes. Wafid provides an online medical-result search using passport/nationality or slip number.

## What does “Fit” mean on Wafid?

It indicates that the candidate has been assessed as medically fit under the Wafid process shown in the official result system.

## What if my Wafid information is wrong?

Contact the medical center and use Wafid's official correction-request process.

## Can I book another test immediately?

Wafid currently says a new appointment can be booked two months after the existing medical report date when a valid report already exists.

## Do I need medical insurance too?

Yes. Study in Saudi separately requires medical insurance for the long-term educational visa.

## Does scholarship healthcare replace visa insurance?

Do not assume so. University healthcare and visa medical insurance are separate concepts.

## Does a fully funded scholarship pay for the medical?

Not automatically. Check your university's scholarship terms.

## Can I use any Pakistani laboratory?

Only if the official process accepts it. If Wafid is required, use the approved Wafid system.

## Should I use an agent to book medical?

It is safer to use the official Wafid system directly when Wafid is required.

## Does pregnancy automatically make a female student medically unfit?

Do not apply Wafid's work-visa pregnancy provision automatically to an educational visa; the published pregnancy condition is specifically framed in relation to work visas.
