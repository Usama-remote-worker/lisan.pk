# Saudi Scholarship Application Cost in Pakistan 2026: Translation, Documents & Full Support

How much does it cost to apply for a Saudi scholarship from Pakistan in 2026?

There is no single answer because students often combine several completely different expenses under the term **“Saudi scholarship application cost.”**

Depending on your situation, you may encounter costs related to:
* Document translation
* Document preparation
* Attestation or authentication
* English-language tests
* Medical or supporting documents
* Courier services
* Optional scholarship consultancy
* Complete application assistance

The most important distinction is this:

> **LISAN.PK's translation or consultancy fee is not an official Saudi scholarship application fee.**

Official government full scholarships cover tuition and university fees, while private translation and application assistance charges are optional private services.
