# Why Your Final Study in Saudi Review Matters

Applicants often assume: *"If I make a mistake, I can just edit it later."* That is risky.

The official Study in Saudi FAQ specifically states that **address and contact details may be edited, while other application information cannot normally be changed after submission**. Therefore, your final review is not just about making the application look professional—it is about preventing errors in information that may become locked after submission. If you run into technical issues or status errors during portal navigation, refer to our [Study in Saudi Portal Problems & Application Error Fixes Guide](/blog/study-in-saudi-portal-problems-errors).

The official platform also requires applicants to complete their personal profile before beginning the study application. Follow our step-by-step walkthrough in the [Study in Saudi Step-by-Step Portal & Document Guide](/blog/how-to-apply-for-saudi-scholarship-step-by-step-portal-document-guide).

# CHECK 1: Does Your Name Exactly Match Your Passport?

Your application name should match your passport: First name, Middle name, Family/surname, Spelling, and Order of names.

For example:
**Passport:** Muhammad Usama Khan  
**Application:** Mohammad Osama Khan

These should not be ignored simply because the names “sound the same.” Use your official document spelling. Also compare your passport with your degree, transcript, Matric/FSc certificates, and supporting documents.

---

# CHECK 2: Is Your Date of Birth Correct?

Check Day + Month + Year. Do not rely only on memory—compare the application directly with your passport. A simple mistake such as **05/08/2002** instead of **08/05/2002** can create a major identity inconsistency.

---

# CHECK 3: Is Your Nationality Correct?

Pakistani applicants should ensure that their nationality is entered correctly. Do not confuse Nationality, Country of residence, and Country of birth. Read the field label carefully.

---

# CHECK 4: Is Your Gender Correct?

This matters because Saudi scholarship programs can have Male-only, Female-only, or Co-educational eligibility. Your personal profile and selected program must be consistent.

---

# CHECK 5: Check Your Passport Number Character by Character

This is one of the highest-priority checks. Compare the application with the passport character by character. Look for mistakes involving 0 and O, 1 and I, missing digits, extra digits, old passport numbers, or typing errors. The official FAQ does not provide a general self-service method for changing submitted passport information.

---

# CHECK 6: Is Your Passport Valid?

Check your passport expiry date before submitting. Official Study in Saudi educational-visa conditions require a valid passport with sufficient validity for the intended entry period.

---

# CHECK 7: Is Your Email Address Correct and Accessible?

Your email is used for account notifications, application communication, and password recovery. Check every letter and verify you can access the inbox.

---

# CHECK 8: Is Your Mobile Number Correct?

Check country code, mobile number, and missing/extra digits. Contact details can be edited after submission, but it is much better to submit them correctly the first time.

---

# CHECK 9: Did You Select the Correct Qualification Level?

Confirm whether your previous qualification is entered as Secondary / Higher Secondary, Bachelor's, Master's, or MS/MPhil. Do not accidentally mix up qualification levels.

---

# CHECK 10: Is Your Degree Title Entered Correctly?

Use the title shown on your official academic record (e.g. *BS Computer Science* or *Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering*).

---

# CHECK 11: Is Your Institution Name Correct?

Check the exact name of your University, College, Board, or Awarding institution. Follow the portal's field requirements carefully.

---

# CHECK 12: Are Your Marks, Percentage or CGPA Correct?

Never estimate. Use your official transcript or marks sheet. If your CGPA is **3.18**, do not enter **3.8**. If your percentage is **78.4%**, do not round it to **80%** unless explicitly required.

---

# CHECK 13: Did You Enter the Correct CGPA Scale?

**3.2 / 4.0** is very different from **3.2 / 5.0**. Make sure obtained GPA, maximum GPA, percentage, and grade go into the correct fields without manual unrequested conversions.

---

# CHECK 14: Is Your Graduation Date Correct?

Check your exact academic record. Do not confuse final examination date, result date, degree issue date, or graduation date.

---

# CHECK 15: Are You Applying for the Correct Degree Level?

Confirm your target program: Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD/Doctoral study.

---

# CHECK 16: Does the Program Match Your Academic Background?

Academic compatibility is key. Stronger progressions include:
* BS Computer Science → Master's Artificial Intelligence
* BS Software Engineering → Master's Cybersecurity
* BBA → Master's Business Administration
* MS Computer Science → PhD Computing

For detailed age, marks, and CGPA requirements, see our guide on [Saudi Scholarship Eligibility for Pakistani Students 2026](/blog/saudi-scholarship-eligibility-pakistani-students).

---

# CHECK 17: Did You Select the Correct University?

Verify **Program + University + City**. Explore available options in our catalog of [Saudi Arabia Fully Funded Scholarship Programs 2026/27](/blog/saudi-fully-funded-scholarship-programs-2026).

---

# CHECK 18: Have You Checked Gender Eligibility?

Confirm whether the specific program at your chosen university is open to male or female candidates.

---

# CHECK 19: Have You Checked the Language Requirement?

Confirm instruction language, IELTS, TOEFL, STEP, or MOI guidelines. Read our guide on [Saudi Scholarship Without IELTS 2026: Universities, MOI & English Requirements](/blog/saudi-scholarship-without-ielts).

---

# CHECK 20: Did You Verify the Scholarship Type?

Ensure you select a **Full Scholarship** rather than paid study or partial options.

---

# CHECK 21: Are Your Program Preferences in the Right Order?

Rank your target universities deliberately based on your preference and career goals.

---

# CHECK 22: Did You Upload the Correct Document in Each Field?

Open each file before submitting to verify that your passport, degree, transcript, and language certificates are uploaded to their corresponding fields.

---

# CHECK 23: Are All Your Document Scans Complete and Readable?

Zoom in on uploaded PDFs. Ensure all corners are visible, text is clear, stamps are legible, and multi-page documents are complete.

---

# CHECK 24: Have You Checked Translations and File Requirements?

Compare translated documents against original certificates. Ensure names, dates, and academic details match. For accredited translations, explore our [Arabic Attestation & Certified Translation Service](/services/translation/arabic-translation).

---

# CHECK 25: Review Every Non-Editable Detail One Final Time

Perform a top-to-bottom check of Identity, Academic Details, Program Preferences, and Document Uploads. Treat the Submit button as a point of commitment.
