# Fully Funded vs Tuition-Free vs 50% Scholarship

These terms mean different things:

## Fully Funded
Can include:
- tuition,
- stipend,
- housing,
- healthcare,
- travel,
- other allowances.

## Tuition-Free
Means tuition may not be charged under that academic route. It does **not automatically prove** that housing, stipend and travel are also covered.

## 50% Scholarship
Means part of the tuition cost is subsidized. It should not be marketed as fully funded.

This distinction is particularly important when comparing Saudi technology programs.
