**Pakistani students can apply for Saudi scholarship programs that admit international applicants, provided they meet the selected university's requirements.** Saudi Ministry of Education guidance gives general age limits of **17–25 for bachelor's, up to 30 for master's and up to 35 for PhD** for its international public-university scholarship framework. **There is no single published percentage, CGPA or IELTS score that applies to every Saudi scholarship**; the individual program's conditions determine academic and language eligibility. [1–3]

**Application status:** The announced Study in Saudi application window of **16 September–6 October 2026** has closed. As of the official sources checked on **5 November 2026**, a new universal 2027 window was not confirmed. See our [official Saudi scholarship deadline tracker](/blog/fully-funded-scholarship-deadlines-saudi-universities-2026). [4]

> **Quick eligibility check:** First establish your degree level, age and nationality category. Then open the exact official program listing to confirm marks/CGPA, prior degree subjects, language test, gender, scholarship type and required attachments. Passing general eligibility does **not** guarantee admission or funding.

## Who is eligible for Saudi scholarships from Pakistan?

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education describes **external scholarships** for non-Saudi applicants living outside Saudi Arabia and **internal scholarships** for non-Saudi applicants legally residing in the Kingdom. Pakistani nationals applying from Pakistan will generally investigate programs classified for **international/external** candidates. [1]

Before applying, check these six factors:

1. **Nationality and applicant type:** Is the program open to international applicants from outside Saudi Arabia?
2. **Degree level and background:** Does your last qualification meet its academic prerequisites?
3. **Age:** Does your age fall within the applicable scholarship and program rules?
4. **Academic record:** Do your actual marks, grades or CGPA satisfy the specific listing?
5. **Language and tests:** Are IELTS, TOEFL, STEP, SAT, another test or an accepted alternative required?
6. **Documents and status:** Are the listed attachments available, and is that actual intake currently accepting applications?

Do not confuse a **program listing** with an **open scholarship application**. Study in Saudi includes full scholarships, partial scholarships and paid programs. [1–3]

## Saudi scholarship age limit: bachelor's, master's and PhD

The Saudi Ministry of Education's public-university scholarship information states the following general age conditions for non-Saudi scholarship applicants. [1]

| Intended study | General ministry guidance | What to confirm before applying |
|---|---|---|
| **Bachelor's** | **17 to 25 years** | Exact age calculation date, program-specific limits and high-school recency |
| **Master's** | **Not older than 30** | Any exceptions, required prior degree, grading and English-language evidence |
| **PhD** | **Not older than 35** | Research area, prior qualification, supervisor/program rules and specific age criteria |

**These are general public-university scholarship rules, not universal conditions for every institution, private paid program or alternative scholarship scheme.** The ministry itself tells applicants to check university-specific conditions. [1]

### Is a 26-year-old eligible for a Saudi bachelor's scholarship?

A 26-year-old is **above the general 17–25 range** stated in the ministry's public-university scholarship guidance. That does not establish that every other Saudi educational route is closed. Check whether another explicitly published program has different conditions; do not assume an exemption exists without official confirmation.

### Can students aged 31 or above apply for Saudi master's programs?

The ministry's general scholarship guidance gives **30** as the master's maximum. Students above it should seek a **specific program or funding route with explicitly different rules**, rather than relying on advice that the age limit is automatically waived.

### Why the student-visa minimum age of 16 is not a scholarship age limit

The Study in Saudi FAQ sets **16 years** as a general eligibility condition for some educational visa pathways. **That is not the same as the ministry's general age band for undergraduate scholarship admissions**. A student satisfying the visa minimum may still be ineligible for a particular bachelor's scholarship. [1–2]

## Education qualifications: Matric, FSc, A-Level, BS, MSc and MPhil

A Pakistani student's previous qualification must match the level and subjects of the chosen degree. Use this as a screening guide, **not** as an equivalence decision by a Saudi university.

| Pakistani qualification | Potential study level to investigate | Main verification |
|---|---|---|
| **FSc, FA, ICS, A-Level or equivalent high school completion** | Bachelor's | Subject prerequisites, school recognition, age, academic grades and program tests |
| **Bachelor's degree (BS, BSc, BA, BBA, BE, etc.)** | Master's | Awarded-degree level, academic equivalence, relevant major and CGPA/grade |
| **Master's, MS, MSc, MPhil or equivalent** | PhD | Research fit, prior dissertation, degree recognition and program admission requirements |
| **Foreign or non-standard qualification** | Depends on equivalence review | Ask the specific university about equivalence and required supporting evidence |

### FSc students applying for bachelor's programs

An FSc Pre-Engineering or ICS student may consider related STEM courses if the program lists the required subjects and tests. An FA or FSc Pre-Medical background will not automatically meet every engineering, computer science or healthcare program's prerequisites.

For example, an official Study in Saudi **Computer Science** bachelor's listing specifies **age 17–25**, a recognized high-school qualification, a **SAT score of at least 1010**, and a condition that no more than five years have elapsed since completing high school. These are **requirements of that specific published program**, not requirements of every Saudi bachelor's scholarship. [3]

### BS graduates applying for master's programs

A BS graduate should review whether the target master's program requires the same major or an academically related field. Some programs require a specific minimum grade, transcript format or English proficiency. There is no safe shortcut such as “all Saudi master's scholarships accept 2.5 CGPA.”

### MPhil and MS graduates applying for PhD programs

At doctoral level, applicants should examine the exact prerequisite degree, academic record, research interests, statement of purpose, research proposal, language requirements and any interview or supervisor-related instructions. **Do not assume every PhD program needs an identical research proposal or universal supervisor letter.**

## Minimum marks or CGPA for Saudi scholarships in 2026/27

**There is no one threshold for every program.** A university's admissions conditions might specify a percentage, letter grade, class classification, CGPA scale, test score or recognized-equivalency rule. Compare your real transcript with the **specific official program**, not an online blanket claim.

| Claim often seen online | Reliable approach |
|---|---|
| “60% is enough for every Saudi scholarship.” | Unverified as a universal rule; check the target program |
| “You must have 80% for all Saudi scholarships.” | Not a universal requirement |
| “Every master's program requires 3.0 CGPA.” | Compare with that program's grade and scale criteria |
| “2.5 CGPA guarantees scholarship admission.” | No eligibility number guarantees selection |
| “All PhD programs require a published research paper.” | Research requirements vary by institution and program |

### Example: a real official master's listing

The official Study in Saudi catalogue includes a **King Abdulaziz University master's listing in Information Technology** whose published conditions specify: **age not older than 30**, minimum grade **Very Good**, and an **IELTS score of 3.5 or equivalent**; the programme also lists supporting attachments. [5]

This example demonstrates **variation**, not a global minimum. Other programs and institutions may ask for higher scores, different tests or different qualifications. Verify that the listing is still relevant to the applicant's intake before relying on it.

## Can Pakistani students apply without IELTS?

**Some programs may provide alternative language-evidence routes, but there is no blanket “Saudi scholarship without IELTS” exemption.** The exact program may require IELTS, TOEFL, STEP, Cambridge English or another proof of proficiency. [5]

Even if a programme is taught in Arabic, it might apply Arabic-language requirements or other assessment criteria. A **Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter** is only a valid substitute when the receiving program explicitly accepts it; it is not automatically equivalent to an English language test.

Before paying for a language test or ordering an MOI letter, check:

- The programme's instruction language.
- The accepted test names and minimum scores.
- Whether an exemption is written in the official instructions.
- Whether the exemption depends on your previous institution or nationality.
- The validity period for tests and acceptable evidence.

See our [Saudi Scholarship Without IELTS Guide](/blog/saudi-scholarship-without-ielts).

## Are there Saudi scholarship programs for Pakistani female students?

**Yes, female international applicants can investigate programs that explicitly list female or both-gender eligibility.** The program catalogue contains both gender-specific and mixed-eligibility listings. A programme marked **Female** or **Both** is not proof that every university or field has the same admissions category. [2–3]

When screening a program, read its actual gender eligibility and residence/location conditions. Never assume that every program is available to everyone merely because it appears in a general scholarship list.

For a detailed guide, see [Saudi Scholarships for Female International Students](/blog/saudi-scholarships-female-international-students).

## Do Saudi scholarships cover all study expenses?

**Not every program is fully funded.** The Saudi Ministry of Education recognizes full, partial and paid scholarship/study categories. Study in Saudi program pages describe funding type and, for certain full-scholarship listings, specific benefits such as a tuition waiver and accommodation. [1–3,5]

| Funding label | What it indicates | What the student must check |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Scholarship** | Program advertises comprehensive study benefits | Exactly which benefits and eligibility conditions apply |
| **Partial Scholarship** | Only some expenses or benefits may be covered | Remaining fees and living expenses |
| **Paid Study** | Student pays the advertised fees | Costs, admission rules and whether any aid is available |

Do not assume a specific stipend amount, flight ticket or dependent allowance unless it is included in that program's authoritative offer.

## Documents Pakistani applicants should prepare

The exact attachment list depends on the university and study level. A **practical preparation checklist**—not a universal compulsory upload list—is below:

- Valid passport with names and birth date consistent across documents.
- Latest qualification certificate (Matric/Intermediate, bachelor's or master's as applicable).
- Complete academic transcript/result sheets.
- Any degree recognition/equivalence evidence specifically requested.
- Language test score or accepted equivalent **where the program requires it**.
- SAT/other examination result **where specifically required**.
- Statement of purpose, CV, recommendation letters or research documents **only when required by the particular programme**.
- Medical and character/police documentation at the appropriate stage **if the authority requests it**.
- Correctly formatted photographs and any portal-specific forms.
- Certified translations of documents **only in the language and format the receiver requires**.

The Ministry of Education notes attestation and medical-related requirements in its general scholarship guidance; a university can specify the required authority and stage. [1]

### Do Pakistani students need Arabic translations of degrees and transcripts?

**Do not assume every document needs Arabic.** An English-language program may accept English originals or request specified translations; other programs may require Arabic. If a transcript is in Urdu or is partly handwritten, check the receiving university's actual instructions before ordering translations.

## How to check eligibility on Study in Saudi: step by step

1. Open the **official [Study in Saudi programs directory](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs)**.
2. Filter by study level (Bachelor, Master, PhD), the program's language and other relevant categories.
3. Confirm the listing says **International** if you are applying from Pakistan.
4. Confirm whether the offer says **Full Scholarship**, **Partial Scholarship** or **Paid Study**.
5. Open the exact program details and read all **Conditions** and **Required Attachments**.
6. Match your age and academic qualifications to those conditions.
7. Check the language test requirement and any program-specific exceptions.
8. Confirm the institution's actual opening/closing dates and whether the application route is open.
9. Prepare accurate documents and apply through the official system according to its instructions.
10. Follow the application status in your own official account after submission.

Our [Saudi Scholarship Program Finder](/saudi-scholarships/program-finder) can help organize a shortlist; it is not a substitute for the official program's final conditions.
