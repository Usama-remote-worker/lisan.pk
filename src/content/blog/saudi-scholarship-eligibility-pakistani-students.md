# Saudi Scholarship Eligibility for Pakistani Students 2026: Age, Marks, CGPA & Requirements

Can Pakistani students apply for fully funded scholarships in Saudi Arabia in 2026?

**Yes. Pakistani students can apply for Saudi scholarship programs available to international students**, provided they meet the eligibility requirements of the selected university and program.

But being a Pakistani student, having good marks or meeting the general age requirement does **not automatically make you eligible for every Saudi scholarship**.

Your eligibility can depend on:

* Age
* Previous qualification
* Academic subjects
* Marks or CGPA
* Intended degree level
* Previous field of study
* Language requirements
* Passport
* Medical requirements
* Supporting documents
* University-specific requirements
* Individual program requirements

The Saudi Ministry of Education's general guidance for international scholarship students sets broad admission conditions, while individual universities may apply additional requirements to particular programs.

Students should verify their eligibility, prepare certified Arabic translations, and complete attestations in advance for upcoming cycles.

If you are planning to apply, the best time to check your eligibility and prepare your documents is **before 16 September**, not during the final days of the application period. # Saudi Scholarship Age Limit 2026

Age is one of the most important **Saudi scholarship eligibility criteria** and one of the areas where students frequently receive conflicting information.

The Saudi Ministry of Education's general admission guidance for non-Saudi international scholarship students gives the following age conditions:

| Degree Level | General Age Requirement |
| ------------ | ----------------------: |
| Bachelor’s   |             17–25 years |
| Master’s     |        Maximum 30 years |
| PhD          |        Maximum 35 years |

The Ministry states that an undergraduate scholarship student should generally be at least 17 and no more than 25, while the maximum is 30 for Master's and 35 for doctoral study.

However, applicants should still check the **specific university and program conditions** before applying.

---

# Saudi Scholarship Age Limit for Bachelor’s

For Bachelor’s scholarships, the Ministry's general rule is:

**Minimum age: 17 years**  
**Maximum age: 25 years**

For example:
* Age 18 → within the general age range
* Age 21 → within the general age range
* Age 24 → within the general age range
* Age 26 → outside the general undergraduate scholarship range

Being within this range does not automatically guarantee eligibility. You must still meet academic and program requirements. # Saudi Scholarship Age Limit for Master’s

The general maximum age stated for Master's scholarship applicants is **30 years**.

A 27-year-old Pakistani BS graduate may fall within the general age requirement, but the university will still evaluate Bachelor’s degree relevance, CGPA, and prerequisites. # Saudi Scholarship Age Limit for PhD

The general maximum age stated for doctoral scholarship applicants is **35 years**. PhD applicants should expect emphasis on research fit and supervisor availability.

# Minimum Percentage & CGPA for Saudi Scholarship 2026

There is **no single universal percentage or CGPA** for every Saudi scholarship. Statements claiming a strict 60%, 70%, 80%, or 3.0 CGPA apply across all programs are overly generalized.

Check individual university criteria. For Master’s and PhD applicants, academic compatibility matters as much as your marks.

---

# Saudi Scholarship Requirements for Pakistani Bachelor’s Students

Pakistani students applying for Bachelor's programs may hold FSc Pre-Engineering, FSc Pre-Medical, ICS, FA, A-Level, or equivalent qualifications.

* **FSc Pre-Engineering:** Potentially eligible for Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Engineering, Math, and Computer Science.
* **ICS:** Eligible for Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering, and Data Analytics.
* **FA / Arts:** Eligible for English, Arabic, Translation, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Islamic Studies.
* **FSc Pre-Medical:** Note that health/medical clinical degrees (MBBS/Dentistry) generally have distinct admission rules under public scholarship routes.

# Do Pakistani Students Need IELTS or Arabic?

Language requirements vary by program. English-taught degrees in Engineering, AI, Computer Science, and Business may require IELTS or TOEFL proof, while Arabic-medium degrees in Islamic Studies or Literature require Arabic proficiency. # Saudi Scholarship Passport & Medical Requirements

* **Passport:** Must have at least 6 months validity from the intended date of entry. Ensure spelling matches across academic certificates.
* **Medical Report:** Freedom from infectious diseases certified by an authorized medical facility.
* **Police Clearance Certificate:** Required to demonstrate absence of criminal precedents.

Yes. Academic documents must be authenticated by competent authorities (HEC, IBCC, MOFA) and translated into Arabic by certified translators.
