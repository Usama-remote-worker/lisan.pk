Applying for a Saudi scholarship is not one application followed by one acceptance email.

For an international student, the complete journey can involve:

**Eligibility** → **Program selection** → **Document preparation** → **Translation** → **Study in Saudi registration** → **Profile completion** → **University preferences** → **Application submission** → **University review** → **Ministry of Education processing** → **Final acceptance** → **Educational visa information** → **Visa requirements** → **Medical and insurance requirements** → **Visa issuance** → **Travel arrangements** → **Arrival in Saudi Arabia** → **University registration** → **Student and residence formalities** → **Beginning your degree**

Understanding this sequence before you begin is important because many students make mistakes by doing the **right task at the wrong stage**.

For example:
* A student may translate every document before selecting a program.
* Another may attest documents before knowing what the university actually requires.
* Someone else may arrange a medical examination immediately after submitting an application.
* Another student may receive university approval and immediately purchase a flight ticket even though Ministry and visa stages still remain.

This guide explains the Saudi scholarship journey **from the first eligibility check to arrival at the university**, including what happens at each stage, who controls it, and when you should move forward.

It is written primarily for international students applying from outside Saudi Arabia for **Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD study**, including applicants from Pakistan.

The Study in Saudi student manual confirms that the platform covers account registration, profile completion, long academic-program applications, educational-visa information and application tracking.

## Important: This Is a Roadmap, Not a Fixed Timeline

Not every applicant will experience every post-acceptance step in exactly the same order.

The precise sequence for medical examinations, document authentication, insurance, visa processing, consular procedures, and travel arrangements can depend on the student's university, nationality, country of application and the instructions issued for that particular case.

Therefore, use this guide to understand **the overall process**, but follow the live instructions from the university and Saudi authorities when your application reaches each stage.

# PHASE 1 — UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU ARE APPLYING FOR

# Step 1: Understand the Type of Study Opportunity

Before thinking about documents or universities, determine what kind of opportunity you want. Saudi official guidance distinguishes among **Full scholarships**, **Partial scholarships**, and **Paid study opportunities**.

Full scholarships cover study costs and university services while also providing financial support (housing, healthcare, arrival allowance, annual travel tickets). Always verify whether a program is fully funded or paid.

Saudi Ministry guidance also distinguishes between:
* **Internal scholarships** — for eligible non-Saudi students residing legally inside Saudi Arabia.
* **External scholarships** — for non-Saudi students applying from outside the Kingdom.

For most applicants living outside Saudi Arabia, the relevant route is an external/international scholarship.

---

# Step 2: Check Your Eligibility Before Spending Money

Do not begin by translating documents, paying for attestation, or choosing a famous university. Begin with one question: **Am I eligible?**

Check nationality, age, current qualification, academic field, marks/CGPA, language ability, gender eligibility, and passport validity.

Saudi Ministry general scholarship age parameters are:
* **Bachelor’s: 17–25**
* **Master’s: Maximum 30**
* **PhD: Maximum 35**

Note that long-term educational visa guidelines state applicants must generally be at least **16 years old**. Visa eligibility does not equal university admission eligibility.

Read our complete [Saudi Scholarship Eligibility Guide for Pakistani Students 2026](/blog/saudi-scholarship-eligibility-pakistani-students).

---

# Step 3: Choose the Correct Degree Level

Identify whether you are applying for **Bachelor’s** (Intermediate/FSc/A-Levels), **Master’s** (relevant undergraduate degree), or **PhD** (relevant postgraduate degree).

For postgraduate study, **academic field compatibility is extremely important** (e.g. BS Computer Science → MS Artificial Intelligence).

# PHASE 2 — FIND THE RIGHT PROGRAM

# Step 4: Start Program Hunting

Begin your search by filtering for programs that match your academic background, degree level, language proficiency, and career goals.

Explore our full directory: [Saudi Arabia Fully Funded Scholarship Programs 2026/27 Catalog](/blog/saudi-fully-funded-scholarship-programs-2026).

---

# Step 5: Verify Every Program Individually

Verify each program's previous academic background requirements, minimum CGPA/marks, language of instruction (Arabic vs English), test requirements (IELTS, TOEFL, STEP, MOI), gender eligibility, and scholarship type.

---

# Step 6: Check IELTS, MOI and Language Requirements

Language conditions vary by department. Some programs accept Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificates, while others strictly require IELTS, TOEFL, or STEP.

Check exact rules in our guide: [Saudi Scholarship Without IELTS 2026: Universities, MOI & English Requirements](/blog/saudi-scholarship-without-ielts).

# PHASE 3 — PREPARE YOUR DOCUMENTS

# Step 7: Build Your Application Document Folder

Organize your passport, secondary-school certificates, degree certificates, transcripts, recommendation letters, research statements, and language certificates. Check the full list in our [Required Documents for Saudi Scholarships 2026 Guide](/blog/required-documents-saudi-scholarship-2026).

---

# Step 8: Check Your Documents for Inconsistencies

Compare your passport, degree, transcript, and CNIC character-by-character to resolve spelling or birthdate differences before submitting.

---

# Step 9: Translate Only the Documents That Need Translation

Follow the sequence: **Find Program → Check Requirements → Identify Documents → Translate**.

For certified translations, visit our [Arabic Attestation & Certified Translation Service](/services/translation/arabic-translation).

---

# Step 10: Understand Translation vs Attestation

Translation converts language; Attestation confirms official authenticity. Authenticate certificates according to the authorities specified by your chosen university.

---

# Step 11: Prepare Research Documents Only When Required

Postgraduate applicants should prepare research proposals, writing samples, or portfolios only when explicitly requested by their target department.

---

# PHASE 4 — CREATE YOUR STUDY IN SAUDI ACCOUNT

# Step 12: Register on Study in Saudi

Register on the official Study in Saudi portal using a personal, active email address. Verify your email and set your security password.

---

# Step 13: Complete Your Personal Profile

Navigate to **My Services → My Profile** and complete all personal, biographical, and educational background sections accurately before starting an application.

---

# PHASE 5 — COMPLETE THE ACADEMIC APPLICATION

# Step 14: Start a Long Academic Program Application

Select **Apply to Study Now**, accept terms, select your degree level, and open a long academic application request. Follow our walkthrough in the [Study in Saudi Step-by-Step Portal & Document Guide](/blog/how-to-apply-for-saudi-scholarship-step-by-step-portal-document-guide).

---

# Step 15: Select Universities and Preferences Carefully

Rank your target universities strategically based on academic match, gender eligibility, and program availability.

---

# Step 16: Review Everything Before Submission

Submitted non-contact information cannot normally be edited. Perform a quality-control check using our [Saudi Scholarship Application Checklist (25 Checks Before You Submit)](/blog/saudi-scholarship-application-review-checklist).

---

# Step 17: Submit Your Application

Submit the request and immediately save your application number, submission date, screenshots, and uploaded file copies.

---

# PHASE 6 — UNIVERSITY REVIEW & TRACKING

# Step 18: Track Your Application

Track progress through **My Applications / My Requests** on the portal. Read our complete guide: [Study in Saudi Application Status 2026: Under Process, Returned, Rejected & Accepted](/blog/study-in-saudi-application-status-under-process).

---

# Step 19: University Review First

Applications are first evaluated by the chosen university. If approved by the university, the application moves to Ministry of Education processing.

---

# Step 20: Understand "Under Process"

**Under Process** is a neutral status indicating that your application is currently under review by the university or Ministry.

---

# Step 21: If Your Application Is Returned

Check the return comment, correct only the requested field or document, and resubmit. If locked, refer to our [Study in Saudi Portal Problems & Application Error Fixes Guide](/blog/study-in-saudi-portal-problems-errors).

---

# Step 22: If Your Application Is Rejected

Review the stated reason, evaluate academic compatibility, and explore alternative universities.

---

# PHASE 7 — UNIVERSITY APPROVAL AND MINISTRY PROCESSING

# Step 23: Understand Post-University Approval Processing

Once approved by the university, your application is processed by the Ministry of Education before final acceptance is issued.

---

# Step 24: Wait for Final Acceptance & Visa Stage

Upon final acceptance, international applicants receive prompts to submit educational-visa information in the portal.

---

# PHASE 8 — EDUCATIONAL VISA INFORMATION

# Step 25: Complete the Visa Information Form

Navigate to **My Services → My Applications → Application Number → Visa Information** and submit required biographical, contact, and security disclosure data.

---

# Step 26: Long-Term Educational Visa Requirements

Prepare valid passport (minimum 6-month validity), medical clearance report, health insurance, and guardian consent (if under 18).

---

# PHASE 9 — MEDICAL, INSURANCE AND VISA COMPLETION

# Step 27: Complete Medical Examination at the Visa Stage

Undergo medical testing only when instructed by the visa workflow or designated medical panel.

---

# Step 28: Complete Final Document Authentication & Insurance

Fulfill any remaining embassy, consulate, or university attestation requirements.

---

# Step 29: Complete Country-Specific Visa Issuance

Submit your passport and visa file through the official Saudi consular or visa center route applicable to your country.

---

# Step 30: Visa Issued

Verify all details on your educational visa stamp upon receipt.

---

# PHASE 10 — PREPARE FOR TRAVEL

# Step 31: Confirm University Reporting Date

Coordinate reporting dates, orientation schedules, and accommodation arrival details with the international student office.

---

# Step 32: Confirm Flight Ticket Arrangements

Verify whether your university issues flight tickets directly or reimburses travel costs under your scholarship package.

---

# Step 33: Travel Document Folder

Pack original degrees, transcripts, passport, visa, acceptance letters, medical reports, and translations in hand luggage.

---

# PHASE 11 — ARRIVAL & UNIVERSITY ENROLLMENT

# Step 34: Travel to Saudi Arabia

Arrive in Saudi Arabia within the designated reporting window.

---

# Step 35: Report to University & Verify Documents

Present original academic certificates and passport at the university International Students Office.

---

# Step 36: Complete Residency (Iqama) & University Onboarding

Complete student ID issuance, university email setup, hostel allotment, stipend bank account setup, and residency (Iqama) processing.

---

# Step 37: Begin Your Studies!

Enroll in classes and begin your academic journey as an international scholarship student in Saudi Arabia.
