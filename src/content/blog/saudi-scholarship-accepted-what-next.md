# Saudi Scholarship Accepted: What Happens Next? Visa, Medical, Ticket & Arrival

**If your Saudi scholarship application is accepted, first check whether the portal shows preliminary/university acceptance or final acceptance.** If you have only been “accepted in principle,” further approval may still be pending. Once final acceptance is reached, applicable international students outside Saudi Arabia move into the educational-visa stage through Study in Saudi.

The general post-acceptance journey is:

# Acceptance

↓

# Confirm Whether It Is Final

↓

# Complete Educational Visa Information

↓

# University / Official Visa Processing

↓

# Medical + Insurance + Required Documents

↓

# Visa Issued

↓

# Confirm Reporting Date & Ticket

↓

# Travel to Saudi Arabia

↓

# Report to University

↓

# Residence / University Registration

↓

# Begin Study

Do **not** immediately:

- buy a flight,

- take a random medical,

- resign from your job,

- pay an agent for “visa approval,”

- or assume the process is finished.

Acceptance begins the **post-admission phase**. It does not automatically mean your visa has been issued.

# Step 1: Determine What “Accepted” Actually Means

## Direct Answer

**Not every acceptance status means the entire Saudi scholarship process is finished.**

The Study in Saudi student manual shows an example where the student is:

# **“accepted in principle successfully”**

but the application still says:

# **“Waiting for final acceptance into the program.”**

Therefore, first identify the exact wording in your application.

---

# Preliminary / Initial Acceptance

This generally means the university has taken a positive action, but another stage may remain.

The official manual example shows:

**Accepted in Principle**

while the request remains:

**In Progress**

and waits for:

**Final Acceptance.**

At this stage:

### Do

- keep checking Study in Saudi,

- monitor your email,

- keep passport ready,

- prepare documents.

### Do Not

- buy a non-refundable ticket,

- assume your visa is approved,

- complete random medical procedures,

- stop monitoring the portal.

# Final Acceptance

## Direct Answer

**Final acceptance is the stage that can trigger the educational-visa process for eligible applicants outside Saudi Arabia.**

The official Study in Saudi student manual states that, upon final acceptance, non-GCC students applying from outside Saudi Arabia complete educational-visa information.

This is the transition from:

# Academic Admission

to:

# Immigration / Visa Processing

# Step 6: Check Your Passport Immediately

## Direct Answer

**Your passport should have at least six months of validity remaining for the long-term Saudi educational visa.**

The current official Study in Saudi FAQ lists:

- age 16 or older,

- valid passport with at least six months remaining,

- admission from a licensed/accredited institution,

- financial ability or financial guarantee

among educational-visa requirements.

- name,

- passport number,

- nationality,

- date of birth,

- expiry date.

# What If My Passport Has Changed?

Do not ignore the difference.

The current Study in Saudi FAQ says that once an application has already been submitted, basic information such as the passport number generally cannot simply be edited through the normal profile process.

If your passport changed:

- keep the old passport,

- keep the new passport,

- save the application number,

- contact the official support route if necessary.

Do not create false data to bypass the mismatch.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## My Saudi scholarship is accepted. What should I do first?

**Check whether your acceptance is preliminary or final.** Preliminary acceptance can still be waiting for final approval.

## What does “accepted in principle” mean?

The Study in Saudi student manual shows it as a positive university-stage result while the applicant is still waiting for final acceptance.

## Does university acceptance mean my visa is approved?

No.

## When does the educational visa stage begin?

The official Study in Saudi manual states that applicable international students complete educational-visa information upon final acceptance.

## Where do I complete the visa information?

The manual shows:

**My Services → My Applications → Application Number → Visa Information.**

## What does “Complete Visa Application” mean?

It indicates that visa information needs to be completed as part of the post-acceptance process. The official manual shows this as a specific application status.

## Does completing the visa form mean my visa is issued?

No. It means the educational-visa information has been submitted for further processing.

## Do I need a medical after acceptance?

A medical report proving freedom from infectious diseases is part of the current study requirements. Complete it when instructed at the correct visa stage.

## Do I need medical insurance?

Follow the long-term educational-visa requirements and your university's instructions.

## How much passport validity do I need?

At least six months under the current educational-visa requirements.

## Should I buy my ticket immediately after acceptance?

No. Wait until visa issuance and university reporting/ticket instructions.

## Does a full Saudi scholarship include flight tickets?

Saudi Ministry guidance lists annual travel tickets among external scholarship benefits, subject to applicable regulations. Confirm the exact arrangement with your university.

## Does the university provide accommodation?

Housing is one of the benefits identified for external scholarship students under Saudi Ministry guidance, subject to the applicable scholarship/regulations.

## Will I receive an arrival allowance?

Saudi Ministry guidance lists a financial reward on arrival among possible external scholarship benefits. Implementation depends on the university/regulations.

## Do I get healthcare?

Healthcare is listed among external scholarship benefits under the applicable rules.

## What happens after I land in Saudi Arabia?

The official Study in Saudi process includes arrival, university-coordinated reception where applicable, induction, receiving the study schedule and beginning studies.

## Do scholarship students receive an Iqama?

Saudi Ministry guidance says scholarship recipients may obtain a free residence permit for their study duration after arrival, based on a university letter and generally processed through the university's passport department.

## Is the visa the same as Iqama?

No. The educational visa is used for entry/study processing; the residence permit is handled after arrival under the applicable process.

## Can a consultant guarantee what happens after acceptance?

No. Consultants can assist with documents and understanding the process, but cannot guarantee Ministry decisions, visa issuance or university actions.

# Final Answer: What Happens After Saudi Scholarship Acceptance?

**First determine whether the acceptance is preliminary or final. If it is only “accepted in principle,” continue waiting for final acceptance. Once final acceptance is reached, applicable international students move into the Study in Saudi educational-visa process.**

The correct order is:

# **Final Acceptance**

→ **Educational Visa Information**

→ **Official Visa Processing**

→ **Medical / Insurance / Required Documents**

→ **Visa Issued**

→ **Reporting Date & Ticket**

→ **Travel**

→ **University Registration**

→ **Residence Formalities**

→ **Begin Study**

The three most important rules are:

> **Acceptance is not the same as visa approval.**

> **Do not complete expensive post-acceptance steps before they are actually required.**

> **Do not book travel until your visa and university arrival instructions are clear.**

After arrival, Saudi Ministry guidance says scholarship recipients may obtain their residence permit for the study period through the university process, while the Study in Saudi manual places university reception, induction, study schedule and commencement of study at the end of the journey.
