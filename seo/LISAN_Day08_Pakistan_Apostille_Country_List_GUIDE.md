# Pakistan Apostille Countries List (2026): Accepted, Objected and Non-Member Countries

**A Pakistani apostille can replace routine embassy legalisation only when the Hague Apostille Convention applies between Pakistan and the country where the document will be used.** It generally applies to countries such as **Saudi Arabia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada and Italy**; it **does not apply between Pakistan and Germany, Austria, the Netherlands or five other objecting countries**. The **UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are not parties to the Apostille Convention** and usually require a different attestation route.

**Last checked: 5 November 2026.** Convention relationships can change. Confirm the latest HCCH [official status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41), [Pakistan-specific objection notices](https://www.hcch.net/de/instruments/conventions/status-table/notifications/?csid=1466&disp=type), and [Pakistan MOFA destination directory](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/countries) **before paying**. A receiving university, employer, embassy or registrar can also specify additional document and translation requirements.

### Does Germany accept a Pakistani apostille?

**No, not as an apostille under the Convention between Germany and Pakistan.** Germany formally objected to Pakistan's accession. For many Pakistani civil-status documents, the [German Missions in Pakistan](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/legal-uess-1680522) expressly explain that they **cannot attest or legalise** Pakistani birth or marriage certificates. German officials may instead request verification through a designated procedure. Therefore, an instruction saying simply “get a Pakistani apostille, then visit the German Embassy for a stamp” is misleading.

**What to do:** Ask the **specific German authority** (university, Standesamt, visa section or other office) what it requires for your certificate. Then follow the German mission's published document-verification guidance where applicable. Translation requirements must also be checked separately.

### Does Austria accept a Pakistani apostille?

**No — Austria's objection means the Convention does not operate between Austria and Pakistan.** Ask the Austrian authority or mission for the correct route for your degree, birth record, marriage certificate or police certificate. Do not buy an apostille solely because Austria is a Hague Convention party in general.

### Can I use a Pakistan apostille in the Netherlands?

**No.** The Dutch government's [country list](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/legalisation/countries-apostille) explicitly warns that a document apostilled in Pakistan **cannot be used in the Netherlands on that basis**. Its [Pakistan-specific legalisation page](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/legalisation/foreign-documents/pakistan) instructs applicants to have Pakistani documents legalised by Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, with further steps depending on the document and destination use. Follow that official country guide rather than general apostille advice.

### What about Finland, Denmark, Poland, Greece and Czechia?

These five countries also objected. Do not treat them as normal apostille destinations for documents issued in Pakistan. The **receiving authority's specific authentication process** controls the next steps. The same principle applies even if a non-governmental country list or a general apostille portal includes the country name.

## UAE, Qatar and Kuwait: why are they not Pakistan apostille destinations?

**They are not currently parties to the 1961 Apostille Convention**, according to the HCCH status table checked on 5 November 2026. They are in a different category from Germany and Austria: the latter **are parties but objected specifically to Pakistan's accession**; the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait are **not parties at all**.

### Does the UAE accept a Pakistani apostille?

**Not as a Hague Apostille Convention substitute for legalisation.** For Pakistan-issued documents intended for the UAE, refer to the [UAE Embassy in Islamabad's attestation guidance](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Missions/Islamabad/Services/Services-for-Individuals/Attestation-Information). Its service describes attestation of documents issued outside the UAE and may include digital processing while still requiring an original physical document. **Digital attestation is not the same as UAE joining the Apostille Convention.** Verify Pakistan MOFA prerequisites, UAE embassy/consulate steps and the receiving UAE department's instructions for your document.

### What is the Qatar embassy attestation route from Pakistan?

Qatar MOFA offers [attestation for foreign-issued documents](https://mofa.gov.qa/en/eservices/attestation/other-documents). Its official guidance refers to recognition of relevant official seals and signatures, including Qatari missions abroad or recognised foreign missions, and an online service for completing foreign attestation. The order depends on document type, prior attestations and the receiving authority. **Do not represent a Pakistani apostille as replacing these requirements.**

### Does Kuwait require embassy attestation?

**Kuwait is not listed as a party to the Apostille Convention.** Its [Ministry of Foreign Affairs consular services](https://www.mofa.gov.kw/en/pages/consular-services/) includes certification/verification of official documents. The specific embassy and ministry steps should be confirmed for your document, and may differ for academic, civil and commercial records. A blanket three-stamp chain for every Kuwaiti application would be unreliable.

### Why does the MOFA portal not show UAE, Germany or Austria under apostille?

They are **not eligible for the same reason**: UAE is a **non-party**; Germany and Austria are **party countries that objected to Pakistan's accession**. Pakistan MOFA's [country directory](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/countries) distinguishes apostille from general attestation, while the HCCH records the underlying treaty relationships. If a portal, checklist or old article conflicts with these records, verify the *current* country relationship before ordering a service.

## How to decide: apostille, general attestation or document verification

| Your situation | Safer decision process |
|---|---|
| Destination is an HCCH party and did not object to Pakistan | Ask whether the recipient requires an **apostille for this public document**, and whether translation is needed. |
| Destination is one of the eight countries objecting to Pakistan | Ask for that country's **Pakistan-specific procedure**; it may be legalisation or separate verification, not necessarily an embassy stamp. |
| Destination is not an Apostille Convention party | Check **Pakistan MOFA general attestation** and the receiving country's embassy/mission legalisation rules. |
| Destination is not clear or submission is to a private entity | Ask for written requirements from the actual receiving authority before paying. |
| Certificate is already apostilled but has no translation | Determine separately whether the recipient wants a **certified translation** and whether that translation itself needs any authentication. |

**Practical example 1 — Pakistani marriage record for Germany:** The HCCH objection rules out reliance on a Pakistan apostille. Follow the German authority and German Missions' **document verification** guidance where applicable. An English or German translation is a separate question.

**Practical example 2 — Pakistani degree for Saudi Arabia:** The Convention applies between Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, but a Saudi university, employer, professional authority or Saudi government unit may still require degree verification or translation. Consult their *document-specific* checklist.

**Practical example 3 — Pakistani birth certificate for the UAE:** Follow the UAE attestation service's requirements for Pakistan-issued certificates, not an HCCH apostille checklist.

**Practical example 4 — Pakistani Nikah Nama for the Netherlands:** Follow the Netherlands' Pakistan-specific **legalisation** guidance and confirm whether the original Urdu Nikah Nama and/or computerized MRC needs translation.

## How to check the latest country status yourself (four steps)

1. **Identify the country where the document was issued** (Pakistan in this guide) and **the country where it will be presented**. A document issued in Saudi Arabia and used in Pakistan is a different case.
2. Open the HCCH [Apostille status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) and confirm whether the destination is a party with the Convention **in force**.
3. Check the [HCCH Pakistan-specific objections](https://www.hcch.net/de/instruments/conventions/status-table/notifications/?csid=1466&disp=type). Do **not** skip this step just because the destination appears on a generic convention list.
4. Check the [Pakistan MOFA destination directory](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/countries) and then the **actual receiving authority** for document, translation and further verification requirements.

## Apostille does not certify the content or translate the document

An apostille verifies the origin of a public document (such as the capacity of the signing official and the seal or stamp). It does **not** prove that every factual statement inside the document is true, and it does **not** translate Urdu, Arabic or English into another language. A certified translation addresses the accuracy of the language conversion. The recipient may need **both**, or only one. See the [HCCH Convention text](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=41) and our [translation-versus-apostille guide](/guides/certified-translation-vs-apostille-pakistan).

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. Which countries accept Pakistan apostille in 2026?

The Apostille Convention can apply between Pakistan and other contracting parties where it is in force **and no relevant objection prevents bilateral application**. Common examples include Saudi Arabia, the UK, the US, Canada, Italy, Spain, Türkiye, Oman and Bahrain. See the tables above and check current HCCH records before submission.

### 2. How many countries objected to Pakistan's accession?

The checked HCCH Pakistan-specific notice lists **eight**: Germany, Czechia, Poland, Austria, Finland, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece. A later withdrawal would change the applicable relationship, so verify the live notice.

### 3. Is Germany an apostille country for Pakistan documents?

**No, not under the bilateral Convention relationship.** Germany objected to Pakistan's accession. German authorities may require document verification, and the German Embassy does not offer routine legalisation for many Pakistani civil records.

### 4. Can I apostille a Pakistani degree for Austria?

You may be able to have an eligible Pakistani public document apostilled for use in *other* destinations, but **the apostille is not effective under the Convention between Pakistan and Austria**. Ask the Austrian authority for the correct requirements.

### 5. Can I use an apostilled Pakistani marriage certificate in the Netherlands?

**No, not on the basis of a Pakistani apostille.** Follow the Netherlands' dedicated Pakistan document-legalisation instructions and confirm translation requirements.

### 6. Is Saudi Arabia on the Pakistan apostille accepted list?

**Yes, the Convention applies between Pakistan and Saudi Arabia.** However, the receiving Saudi institution can impose additional document-specific verification or translation requirements.

### 7. Is the UAE part of the Hague Apostille Convention?

**No, as of this guide's fact-check date.** Consult UAE MOFA's embassy attestation service for Pakistan-issued documents.

### 8. Is Qatar an apostille country for Pakistani documents?

**No, as of this fact-check.** Use Qatar's documented consular/foreign-document attestation guidance rather than assuming an apostille replaces it.

### 9. Does Kuwait recognise a Pakistani apostille instead of embassy authentication?

The Apostille Convention does not currently apply between Pakistan and Kuwait. Ask the Kuwaiti recipient or mission for applicable attestation steps.

### 10. Why is Finland on some generic apostille-country lists?

Finland is a **Contracting Party generally** but is also one of the countries that objected to **Pakistan's accession**. A generic country list that does not check the bilateral relationship is incomplete.

### 11. Is a Pakistan-issued apostille mandatory for UK or US visa applications?

**Not automatically.** The relevant visa programme sets its evidence rules. For example, some processes focus on certified translations of non-English documents rather than apostille.

### 12. What if an apostille country is absent from the current MOFA portal?

Recheck the official HCCH entry-into-force date, objection notices and Pakistan MOFA directory. Contact the portal or competent authority if the eligibility appears inconsistent. Do not pay a third party to “force” a country selection.

### 13. Is Thailand currently accepting Pakistani apostilles?

HCCH lists **28 February 2027** as Thailand's planned entry-into-force date. In **November 2026**, the Convention is **not yet in force for Thailand**; check again when it enters into force and verify any applicable objections.

### 14. Does Pakistan accept the Convention route for India?

**No.** Pakistan's HCCH declaration says the Convention shall **not apply to India**. A generic list showing India as an Apostille Convention party does not override that Pakistan-specific declaration.

### 15. Are all Pakistani documents eligible for an apostille?

No. Eligibility depends on the nature of the public document and Pakistan MOFA's rules. Some records require prior issuer verification or other preparation. Check the official [MOFA apostille service](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/) for the specific document.

### 16. Can LISAN.PK issue an apostille?

**No.** Apostilles are issued by designated competent public authorities, not by private translation agencies. LISAN.PK can discuss translation requirements and provide a translation quotation where appropriate; official attestation remains with the competent government authorities.

### 17. Does my apostille cover my Arabic or English translation?

Not automatically. An apostille on a source document does not certify a separate translation. Check whether the recipient needs a signed translator's certificate, additional notarisation or separate authentication.
