# How to Edit a Study in Saudi Application After Submission: Passport, Qualifications and Returned Applications

**You generally cannot change your passport number after submitting a Study in Saudi application.** The official portal FAQ says a passport number can be changed only on a new account **before any application has been submitted**. Some academic qualification information and other specifically permitted fields can be updated, including when an institution returns an application, but the actual controls depend on your record and portal status. [1]

**Verified: 5 November 2026.** The official Study in Saudi FAQ contains two answers that may look contradictory: one states that only contact information can be updated after submission, while another allows changes to qualification information and certain specified fields. **Do not assume a field is editable merely because it appears in a generic blog or tutorial.** Check the exact action available in your own account and request guidance from official support when a critical field is locked. [1]

## Study in Saudi editability: field-by-field answer

| Field or situation | What official guidance supports | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| **Passport number after application submitted** | **Cannot normally be changed** | Do not create a conflicting second identity; contact official support for any exceptional correction route |
| Passport number on new account with **no submitted applications** | Can be changed | Correct it before first submission |
| Nationality, gender, residence/Gulf ID | Identified as restricted basic fields | Review before submitting; consult official support if incorrect |
| Qualifications / academic information | May be updatable under specified conditions | Open account controls; check whether 'Update Data' or an editable form is shown |
| Telephone or other contact information | Official FAQ states contact details may be updated | Update through the profile if permitted |
| Registered email | Generally cannot be edited for accounts registered by email; residents in KSA have a specified profile exception | Follow account-specific instructions; use password recovery rather than opening duplicates |
| Application returned by university | Certain qualification data and specified fields may be updated | Read the return reason and use permitted controls, then verify any resubmission requirement |
| Application already Under Process | University is reviewing it; cancellation limited after action taken | Avoid unnecessary cancellations; follow your status and official guidance |

**Source:** [Study in Saudi — official frequently asked questions](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Faq). This table is a practical summary of its published answers, **not** a guarantee that an edit button will appear in every account. [1]

## Why are there two different answers on the official FAQ?

The official FAQ includes both:

- A general statement that contact information can be updated, while other submitted data cannot be changed.
- A more specific answer stating qualification data and certain allowed fields can be updated, with passport number, nationality, gender and other identity fields excluded.

The reasonable operational interpretation is **field-specific and status-dependent editing**, but the public FAQ does not document a complete technical permission matrix for every student, university and stage. Treat any blanket claim such as “all details can be edited” or “nothing can ever be corrected” as unreliable. [1]

### What if your profile says Under Process?

The portal describes Under Process as an application being reviewed by the relevant educational institution. It is not a rejection or a final offer. The application may be at a stage where certain changes are locked because university action has started. The FAQ says cancellation is available only while **no action has yet been taken by the educational institution**. [1]

Read our fuller [Study in Saudi application status guide](/blog/study-in-saudi-application-status-under-process) for the meanings of Under Process, returned, rejected and other labels.

## How to correct a passport number mistake

**Do not assume a submitted passport number can be manually overwritten.** The official FAQ says this is not possible after submitting an application. Your safest path is to document the issue and ask the official portal to explain whether any formal remedy applies to your specific record. [1]

1. Sign in directly at [Study in Saudi](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en).
2. Check whether any application has been submitted under the current profile.
3. If no application has ever been submitted, inspect the profile's passport fields and correct them before applying.
4. If an application has been submitted, **do not submit a different passport number through another identity/profile to bypass restrictions**.
5. Use the portal's official help/support route. Describe the mismatch clearly and provide only the information the official support channel requests.
6. Keep a record of your support ticket and instructions. Never share your password, SMS verification code or email OTP with a consultant or public support group.

**Renewed passport vs incorrect passport number:** A new passport issued after submission creates a different scenario from a typing error. The public FAQ still says submitted passport numbers cannot be modified. Ask official support how renewal information should be handled; no universal self-service solution is published in the reviewed FAQ.

## Can you update qualifications after submitting?

**Sometimes, yes.** The published FAQ says qualification data and certain other specified fields can be updated. It does **not** guarantee that every GPA, grade, graduation date, qualification type or document-upload field will remain open throughout the review process. [1]

Use this safe procedure:

1. Sign in and open your actual application and profile, not a shared screenshot from another student.
2. Look for the university's returned-action notice, an 'Update Data' control or another permitted edit interface.
3. Identify exactly which qualification is incorrect and which evidence supports the correct information.
4. Update only the editable fields and upload any required document, if the portal asks for it.
5. Save changes and check whether the application requires an additional **resubmit** or **confirm** action.
6. Confirm the updated record is visible; keep an anonymized screenshot for your records.
7. If the field is locked, use official support rather than manipulating the portal or creating conflicting submissions.

**Do not invent a new CGPA or alter an academic transcript.** Corrections must reflect authentic documents issued by your school, board or university.

## What does Returned mean on Study in Saudi?

A **returned application** is not necessarily the same as a final rejection. In ordinary application workflows it often means the institution requires an applicant-side action, but **the exact meaning and available corrections are determined by the notice shown in your own account**. The reviewed official FAQ explicitly discusses editing after a university returns an application, but does not promise that all returned applications are recoverable. [1]

### Returned application: 7-step correction checklist

1. **Read the return notice carefully.** Record the field, missing document or requirement mentioned.
2. **Check the submission status and any visible response deadline.** Do not assume a universal number of correction days.
3. **Compare the entered data with original documents.** Check passport name, date of birth, degree, marks, grading scale, translation and institution details.
4. **Correct only permitted fields.** University qualifications may be editable; identity and passport fields are specifically restricted.
5. **Replace poor-quality uploads only if the interface permits it.** Use complete, legible scans rather than edited/fabricated records.
6. **Resubmit or confirm only when explicitly required by the portal.** Saving a field may not equal resubmitting the application.
7. **Monitor your status.** The next step depends on the institution's review; there is no guaranteed acceptance after a correction.

**Example:** If the university flags an incomplete transcript, check whether your account offers document replacement. If the problem is a wrong passport number, do not assume the same document-update control can fix it. Seek official instructions.

## Common mistakes: what to do and what not to do

| Situation | Safer response | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Misspelled passport name | Compare account and valid passport; ask support if locked | Creating false supporting documents |
| New passport issued after submission | Ask support for the formal renewal procedure | Altering previous passport scans |
| Missing academic marks | Check qualifications editing rights and accurate board/university record | Substituting an invented percentage |
| Document uploaded upside-down or cropped | Replace it if the application permits | Assuming a submitted upload can always be replaced |
| Account locked / forgotten password | Official recovery function | Sharing login or OTP with strangers |
| Rejected status | Read program requirements and official outcome | Treating rejection as an automatic editable return |
| Under Process for several weeks | Monitor portal and university communications | Trusting fixed result dates without official confirmation |

## Can you delete and recreate a Study in Saudi account?

The portal FAQ says students can request **profile deletion by support ticket**. It does **not** state that deleting an account will undo an institutional review, restore eligibility or bypass the restriction on passport changes. Do not delete a submitted account as a shortcut without official instructions, particularly if you need to retain existing application records. [1]

## What if the translation contains a mistake?

A certified translation and a portal identity record are different things. If a document translation contains a genuine spelling or date error, the responsible translator can review and correct the translation so it faithfully matches the original. But **correcting a PDF does not automatically update a locked Study in Saudi field**. Verify that the portal permits replacing the file or ask the university/support desk.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my Study in Saudi passport number after submit?

**Normally no.** The official FAQ says the passport number can be changed only if the account is new and no application has been submitted. [1]

### Can I change my Study in Saudi mobile number?

The FAQ allows contact details to be updated. Check the options in your profile. [1]

### Can I change nationality after submission?

The official FAQ lists nationality among basic identity fields that cannot be updated. [1]

### Can I edit my qualification or CGPA?

Qualification data may be editable under specified conditions; use the controls visible for your application or contact support. [1]

### Can I change the registered email address?

The FAQ says email-registered users generally cannot edit their registered email, with a profile-update exception described for residents within Saudi Arabia. [1]

### What does Under Process mean?

It means the application is under institutional review, not that the student has been accepted or rejected. [1]

### Can I cancel an Under Process application?

Only if the institution has not yet taken action on it, according to the portal FAQ. [1]

### Is Returned the same as Rejected?

Do not treat them as identical. Check the exact notice, permitted actions and the university's response. A returned record may allow corrections, while rejection means an admission requirement was not met. [1]

### Do returned applications allow passport edits?

The FAQ still lists passport number among restricted basic fields, even while describing certain editable fields on returned applications. [1]

### How long do I have to fix a returned application?

No universal correction period was established by the official FAQ reviewed. Follow any deadline in your own notice or request guidance through official support.
