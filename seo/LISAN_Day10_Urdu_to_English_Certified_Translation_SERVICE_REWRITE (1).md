## Certified Urdu translation for USCIS (United States)

**For documents submitted to USCIS, U.S. regulations require a full English translation accompanied by a translator's certification of completeness, accuracy and competence.** See [8 CFR § 103.2(b)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-103/section-103.2). The current [Form I-130 instructions](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/forms/i-130instr.pdf) also specify translator identifying and contact information for documents filed with that form.

A complete service request should identify the exact form or case stage. A translation suitable for a USCIS petition is not automatically the same as an NVC interview-document submission.

### What should the USCIS translation include?

- Every legible material statement from the original, including relevant stamps and annotations.
- Faithful names, dates, numbers and references.
- A clear source-to-English correspondence, especially for forms and tables.
- A signed certification that the translation is complete and accurate and that the translator is competent to translate Urdu into English.
- Other identifying information required by the form-specific instructions.

**No claim of universal USCIS approval:** USCIS decides whether submitted evidence meets the application's legal requirements. A translator's certificate addresses the translation, not the truth of the underlying document or the merits of a petition.

## Certified Urdu-to-English translation for UKVI

For a **UK visitor visa**, [GOV.UK's official supporting-documents guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visitor-visa-guide-to-supporting-documents/guide-to-supporting-documents-visiting-the-uk) says a document not in English or Welsh must have a full translation that the Home Office can independently verify. The guide lists confirmation of accuracy, translation date, translator's full name/signature and contact details.

For UK student, family and other visa routes, check the current route-specific instructions. Not every supporting document must be supplied in every case; sending a perfectly translated but irrelevant record does not improve eligibility automatically.

**Example:** An Urdu Fard offered as supporting evidence in a visitor application should be translated if submitted, but a Fard is **not a mandatory document in every visitor application**. See our [Fard translation guide](/blog/urdu-to-english-fard-translation-in-lahore).

## Urdu translations for Canadian immigration and other destinations

Canadian immigration generally requires documents not in English or French to be accompanied by an appropriate translation. **IRCC distinguishes translations by certified translators from translations that need an affidavit**, and supporting-document instructions differ by application type. Applicants should read the applicable [IRCC help centre](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=018&top=29) and the exact program guide before ordering.

For Australia, New Zealand, universities, private employers and individual courts, requirements can differ substantially. We confirm the requested destination and documentation before quoting a certificate or notarisation service.

**The important difference:** A document may be translated accurately yet still need additional authentication, an affidavit, notarisation, official issuance or a different certified-translator credential for its destination. These are separate questions.

## How we check names, dates and handwritten entries

We use a consistent quality-control approach:

1. **Read the complete original** and confirm the language pair.
2. **Preserve original spellings and numbers**; do not silently replace inaccurate source data.
3. **Record established English spellings** supplied by the client (for comparison only), especially for names.
4. **Translate legible stamps, footnotes and handwritten annotations** where relevant.
5. **Mark unreadable portions** instead of guessing or inventing missing words.
6. **Review tables, dates and fractions** a second time.
7. **Prepare the requested translator statement** reflecting the real translator's identity and competence.

For official records, accurately reporting a mismatch is more defensible than masking it. Only the document-issuing authority can correct the underlying record.

## What is a certified translation versus a notarized translation?

**Certified translation:** An English translation with a translator declaration concerning its accuracy, completeness and competence, as required by a particular receiving authority.

**Notarization:** A separate notarial action. It is not synonymous with translation certification and is not universally required by USCIS.

**Attestation or apostille:** Authentication processes affecting designated public documents under applicable procedures. They do not turn incorrect translations into correct ones.

If an authority asks for a specific type of translator, notarial act or authentication, share the written requirement so we can determine whether it is within the agreed service scope.

### Does USCIS require a notarized Urdu translation?

Its general rule is a **complete English translation with translator certification**, not automatic notarization of every translated record. Check the form instructions and specific requests.

### What details must a UK visitor-visa translation certificate contain?

GOV.UK lists confirmation of accuracy, translation date, translator's full name/signature, and contact details for the visitor supporting-document context.

### Can family members translate documents for Canadian immigration?

IRCC may restrict who can translate and may require affidavits or certified-translator credentials depending on the process. Check the current IRCC program instructions; do not assume informal family translation is acceptable.
