### Which English documents need Turkish translation for university admission?

**The university decides.** Different Turkish universities publish different document requirements, and admission-stage rules may differ from final enrolment. For example:

- **Boğaziçi University**'s 2026–27 international undergraduate registration information says translated versions are needed when required documents are **not in Turkish or English**; it says translation-agency translations are acceptable for that stated process.

This is why a statement such as “all Turkish universities require notarised Turkish translation” is incorrect. **Check the specific university, degree level and application stage.**

## Sworn Turkish Translation (*Yeminli Tercüme*) vs Certified Translation

These terms are often confused. They refer to **different documentary and institutional requirements**.

| Service or document | What it generally means | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| **Professional translation** | Accurate rendering of source content in the target language | Whether the receiving body accepts an ordinary translation. |
| **Certified translation** | Translation with a signed statement of accuracy/competence from the responsible translator | Exact wording, signature and identification demanded by the authority. |
| **Turkish sworn translation (*yeminli tercüme*)** | Translation completed by a person who has the required sworn-translator status for the applicable Turkish notarial/consular procedure | Translator's actual notary/consulate relationship and whether that person can sign for this use. |
| **Notarised translation (*noter tasdikli tercüme*)** | Translation additionally approved through the relevant notarial procedure | Whether notarisation must be completed **in Türkiye** and which notary will accept it. |
| **Apostille / legalisation** | Authentication of a public document's official origin as applicable | Whether the source or a separate notarial act needs authentication; an apostille is **not** proof of linguistic accuracy. |

A translator based in Pakistan may be able to translate between English and Turkish without being a **Turkish sworn translator registered for a specific notarial function**. LISAN.PK must **not represent its translation as Turkish-notarised or Turkish-sworn** unless the requested status and responsible translator's credentials have actually been checked and arranged.

For official procedures, Turkish government guidance and individual university notices show situations where a **sworn Turkish translation plus notary approval** is specifically required. See [Türkiye Presidency of Migration Management — Residence Permit](https://en.goc.gov.tr/residence-) and [Kayseri University registration instructions](https://intoffice.kayseri.edu.tr/en/c/2131-1/registration-dates-required-documents-for-application-and-language-of-instruction).

## Tapu, Property and Legal-Record Translation

A Turkish *tapu senedi* is a land-title document. Its fields can include **province, district, neighbourhood, block/parcel identifiers, property nature, owner and share information**. We translate the visible wording and numbers without making a title search, certifying legal ownership or claiming that a foreign land registry will accept the result.

For **English → Turkish** property matters, a Pakistani sale deed, Fard-e-Malkiat, inheritance certificate or power of attorney may be relevant, but whether a Turkish court, land office, bank or notary will accept its translation is determined by that institution. Some documents will require a separate **apostille/legalisation** or **Turkish notarial** procedure. A translator cannot replace a Turkish lawyer, land-registry officer or notary.

## Name Spellings, Turkish Characters and Document Formatting

Turkish uses the Latin alphabet with letters such as **Ç, Ğ, I, İ, Ö, Ş and Ü**. English-language names and Urdu names transliterated through English may not map perfectly to Turkish forms. We should preserve or carefully annotate the spelling used by the **passport and source document** when the receiving institution expects consistency.

Before finalising a document, we review:

- Passport and certificate names, including distinct **I/İ/ı/i** characters.
- Birth and issue dates; do not silently convert between date formats.
- Degree level, institution names and faculty names.
- Grading scale, ECTS and hours on Turkish transcripts.
- Legal terms such as *ikamet*, *tapu*, *vekâletname* and *mahkeme kararı*.
- Page numbering, stamps, signatures and reverse-side text.

A translation **does not correct the source record**. If a university certificate or passport contains a factual error, resolve it with its issuing body rather than introducing an unapproved new spelling or date in translation.

## FAQs: English ↔ Turkish Translation

### 1. Can I translate English documents into Turkish in Pakistan?

Yes. We can review an English-to-Turkish document translation request. If the receiving authority requires a Turkish sworn or notarised translation, that specific credential and formalisation route must be confirmed separately.

### 2. Can you translate Turkish documents into English?

Yes. We can quote Turkish-to-English translations of degrees, transcripts, residence records, company documents and other legible material, subject to scope review.

### 3. Does a Turkish university require all documents in Turkish?

Not universally. Some universities accept documents in English; others ask for sworn and notarised Turkish translations. Check the university's current program- and registration-stage instructions.

### 4. What is *yeminli tercüme*?

It means **sworn translation** in the Turkish context. Whether a specific translation must come from a sworn translator, and who is authorised to sign it, depends on the relevant notarial or official procedure.

### 5. Is a Pakistani certified translation automatically valid before a Turkish notary?

No. A Turkish notary's requirements and applicable translator relationship must be checked separately. A Pakistani certificate of translation accuracy does not by itself create Turkish notarial status.

### 6. Can a Turkish transcript be translated into English with grades intact?

Yes. A proper translation should retain all course titles, grading symbols, credit/ECTS information and explanatory legends as printed; we do not convert grades unless that is expressly part of an approved, separate analysis.

### 7. Do I need Turkish translation for a residence permit?

Some foreign documents require sworn and notarised Turkish translations in specified residence-permit procedures. Use the current category-specific instructions from the Presidency of Migration Management.

### 8. Can you translate a *tapu* land title into English?

Yes, subject to the document's legibility and scope. The translation reports the text; it does not certify land ownership, encumbrances or legal validity.

### 9. Is apostille the same as notarisation or translation?

No. Apostille authenticates a public document's official origin for applicable international use; notarisation is a distinct notarial act; translation renders the text into another language.

### 10. What if my source is Urdu rather than English?

State **Urdu → Turkish** specifically when requesting a quote. A bilingual source may also include English and Urdu; we must review the actual pages before defining the translation direction.

### 11. Do you guarantee that the Turkish Embassy will accept my document?

No. Acceptance belongs to the relevant authority. We can review its written checklist and provide a translation within the confirmed scope.

### 12. Can you translate Turkish court orders and powers of attorney?

We can assess legal-document translation requests, but may need additional time and specialist review. Translation is not a substitute for legal advice, notarial execution or court authentication.

### 13. How fast is Turkish translation delivery?

We agree a realistic completion date after seeing the documents, language direction and any formal certification requirements. We do not promise universal same-day delivery.

### 14. Do I need the original document before requesting a quote?

A clear scan is usually enough to assess a translation quotation, but the receiving institution or notary may require the physical original later. Follow that institution's instructions.

### 15. Can you translate Pakistani MRC, FRC or birth certificates into Turkish?

We can review the request. First identify the receiving institution's required form of translation and any sworn/notarial requirements. The source documents remain issued by the competent Pakistani authority.

### 16. What should I check before paying for a notarised Turkish translation?

Ask which **notary or consular office** will perform the act, which authorised translator will sign, which documents must be presented in original, and which fees are included in the written quotation.
