# King Saud University Scholarship for Pakistani Students: 2026/27 Admissions, SAT and Eligibility

**King Saud University (KSU) offers external scholarships to eligible non-Saudi students living outside Saudi Arabia.** Its current official international-admissions page lists a **minimum SAT score of 1010** and an **age range of 17–25** for the external scholarship bachelor's route, alongside other conditions. The university has **not confirmed a new general 2027 external-scholarship application deadline** in the official sources reviewed here. [1][2]

**Checked: 5 November 2026.** King Saud University (KSU) is in Riyadh and must not be confused with **KAUST** (King Abdullah University of Science and Technology) or **KAU** (King Abdulaziz University). The [KAUST Fall 2027 application deadline](/scholarships/kaust) of 3 January 2027 **does not apply to KSU**.

| Scholarship route | External scholarship for non-resident international applicants; a separate internal route exists for Saudi residents |

## KSU external scholarship eligibility for Pakistan

For the **external undergraduate scholarship** described on KSU's current admission page, the university lists several requirements. Pakistani applicants should check all of them rather than focus only on marks:

1. Hold a recognized high-school diploma/equivalent accepted for the chosen program.
2. Present a **valid SAT score of at least 1010**, as specified for this track.
3. Be aged **17–25**.
4. Have completed the high-school qualification within the **last five years**.
5. Meet any home-country approval requirement where applicable.
6. Not hold another scholarship at an educational institution in Saudi Arabia.
7. Have properly attested supporting documents as required by the university.
8. Have no disqualifying expulsion or current university enrollment under the listed conditions.
9. Not previously hold a bachelor's degree for this bachelor-entry track.
10. Complete required medical checks, and any interview or written assessment for certain colleges. [1]

**Important:** These are the published conditions for the external **bachelor's** pathway. Do **not** state that every KSU master's or PhD scholarship applicant needs SAT 1010 or is limited to age 25. Read the relevant graduate department's rules first.

### KSU scholarship vs fee-based health-sciences admission

KSU also describes a separate non-resident **fee-based health-sciences** route. For its Medicine and Surgery track, the page states a higher **SAT 1200** threshold and further conditions. **Fee-based admission is not the same as a fully funded scholarship.** The program finder must label such routes accurately and not tag every KSU result “fully funded.” [1]

## Can female students apply to King Saud University?

Eligibility depends on the **actual academic program and track**. KSU's published fee-based health programs include male/female program rows and certain female-only offerings. Those tables do not establish eligibility for every external scholarship major. Use the current [Study in Saudi program listing](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs), select the university and degree, then check the exact gender eligibility, residency category, scholarship type and language on the individual program page. [1][3]

## King Saud University master's and PhD admissions

KSU's International Students Department describes master's and doctoral opportunities for eligible international students, including external applicants. It does not publish one universal master's admission rule applicable to every department. [2]

For a postgraduate shortlist:

- Identify the **exact program** and faculty.
- Verify whether that intake accepts international applicants living outside Saudi Arabia.
- Confirm the required degree and academic grade.
- Check IELTS, TOEFL, STEP, Arabic-language or departmental language rules if specified.
- Check research proposal, reference-letter, interview and test requirements.
- Verify whether any offered place is fully funded, partially supported or fee-based.

**Do not use bachelor's SAT 1010 or age 17–25 rules as a blanket graduate requirement.** A scholarship award remains competitive and depends on the academic program and official admissions decision.

## KSU scholarship 2027 application deadline: what is confirmed?

**No new university-wide KSU 2027 external scholarship deadline was independently confirmed in the official pages reviewed on 5 November 2026.** The previously announced Study in Saudi period of **16 September–6 October 2026** has ended. A KSU semester start date, a paid-program announcement or KAUST's separate deadline is not evidence that KSU's external scholarships are open. [3][4]

For a reliable next-intake check, visit the [KSU international admissions page](https://dar.ksu.edu.sa/en/overseas), review current [Study in Saudi listings](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs), and follow institution-specific announcements.

## How to apply for KSU from Pakistan

1. Check the [KSU official international admissions page](https://dar.ksu.edu.sa/en/overseas) for your correct route.
2. Open the [Study in Saudi program catalogue](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs); verify the exact KSU course and its application availability.
3. Confirm the study level, scholarship category, residency eligibility, gender conditions and language requirements on the individual listing.
4. Verify relevant tests—SAT for the published external bachelor's route; graduate testing based on the actual postgraduate program.
5. Prepare accurate records and required translations/attestations.
6. Apply through the officially linked system while the applicable window is open.
7. Monitor the submitted application through the official account; **Under Process** is not an acceptance. [1][3][5]

### What does KSU scholarship status “Under Process” mean?

The Ministry's Study in Saudi FAQ says **Under Process** means institutional review. It does not promise selection or a fixed result date. Read our [application status explanation](/blog/study-in-saudi-application-status-under-process) and [post-submission correction guide](/guides/study-in-saudi-edit-application-after-submission). [5]

## FAQs

### What is the King Saud University scholarship deadline for 2027?

No new general external KSU deadline was confirmed in the reviewed official material. Check KSU and Study in Saudi rather than borrowing KAUST dates. [1][4]

### Is SAT compulsory for KSU international scholarships?

KSU's current **external undergraduate** scholarship requirements specify at least **1010**. Rules for graduate programs and other tracks may differ. [1]

### Can FSc students from Pakistan apply to KSU?

Applicants with qualifying high-school education may be eligible for the relevant route, subject to KSU's acceptance, age, recency, SAT and other conditions. [1]

### Is IELTS mandatory for all KSU students?

No universal IELTS requirement for every KSU program is established by the sources reviewed. Check the program-specific language rules.

### Are KSU scholarships automatically fully funded?

KSU distinguishes external scholarships from fee-based admissions. Check the funding category shown on the specific listing or official offer. [1]

### Does KSU offer master's scholarships?

KSU describes postgraduate opportunities for eligible international students. Eligibility and funding depend on the chosen program and track. [2]

### Can I use a KAUST application account for KSU?

No. These are different universities with different systems and requirements.
