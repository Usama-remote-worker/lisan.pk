# Study in Saudi Scholarship Deadline 2026/27: Official Dates, Closed Intake and Next Updates

**The announced Study in Saudi application window for non-Saudi applicants ran from 16 September to 6 October 2026 and is now closed.** King Abdulaziz University published these dates in its official September 2026 admission notice. **A new universal 2027/28 scholarship application window has not been verified in the official sources reviewed for this article**, so students should not rely on unofficial reopening dates. [1–3]

**Short answer for Pakistani students:** If you submitted before **6 October 2026**, monitor your existing application on the [official Study in Saudi portal](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Home/Index?lang=en). If you did not apply, use the [official program catalogue](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs) to shortlist programs and verify the next opening directly; do not treat a university's semester-start date as an application deadline.

## Is Study in Saudi open today?

**The published 16 September–6 October 2026 window is closed.** The public homepage may still display the previous dates; that is not evidence of an active new intake. Applications, scholarships, paid programs and short courses can have different availability conditions.

Before treating a button as an open scholarship opportunity, confirm all of the following:

- The program page is accepting applications, not merely advertising a program.
- The applicant category is **International** if applying from Pakistan.
- The scholarship type really is **Full Scholarship**, not Paid Study or a resident-only place.
- The program admits your degree level, gender and nationality under its current conditions.
- The relevant university has published a live intake/closing date for your route.

The [official Study in Saudi Programs catalogue](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs) includes paid and scholarship options, so **a program listed on the portal is not automatically a fully funded scholarship currently open for applications**. [3]

## When will Study in Saudi reopen in 2027?

**No new universal opening date should be published until an official authority announces it.** Repeating the 2026 September dates as “September 2027” would be speculation, not a verified deadline.

Watch these official sources:

1. [Study in Saudi — official homepage](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Home/Index?lang=en) for the platform's intake notices.
2. [Study in Saudi — program listings](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs) for program-level availability and conditions.

A social media post, third-party scholarship directory or repeated date from last year should be treated as **unverified until supported by the official program or university notice**.

That verified 2026 deadline is **not automatically** the deadline for an unrelated 2027/28 admission cycle. Students targeting a specific KAU program should cross-check its own details, scholarship classification, required attachments and test scores.

## King Saud University (KSU): don't confuse semester dates with scholarship deadlines

An academic calendar may show when a term starts, but **semester commencement is not an international scholarship application deadline**. KSU program requirements and admission routes may vary.

Use the [KSU university guide](/scholarships/king-saud-university) as a starting point, then check the university's official admissions information and the specific listing on Study in Saudi. If a next-intake date is not explicitly announced, publish **“not yet confirmed,”** not an estimated date.

The same rule applies to the Islamic University of Madinah, KFUPM, Princess Nourah University and other institutions: **the portal is not a substitute for program-specific admissions conditions**.

## Can Pakistani students apply for bachelor's, master's and PhD scholarships?

**Yes, Pakistani nationals can be considered for eligible international scholarship programs**, subject to the actual institutional and program requirements. The Saudi Ministry of Education distinguishes external scholarships (for non-Saudi applicants outside Saudi Arabia) from internal scholarships (for non-Saudis legally residing in the Kingdom). [4]

The ministry's public-university guidance gives general age limits of **17–25 for bachelor's, not older than 30 for master's, and not older than 35 for PhD** in the international public-university scholarship context. **These are general conditions, not a guarantee that every university or route uses precisely the same eligibility test**. [4]

## What to do if you already applied

The official portal says applications go through institutional review and then further processing. Its FAQ explains that **“Under Process”** indicates an application under review; it does not itself mean approved or rejected. [2]

### Step 1 — Log in using the account that submitted the application

Use the official Study in Saudi website, not an unofficial status-check website. Avoid sharing your portal password or one-time codes with an intermediary.

### Step 2 — Read the exact status and any notice

A status label may be paired with a university message, request for correction or final decision. Do not infer a decision from elapsed time alone.

### Step 3 — Check which changes the platform actually permits

Official Study in Saudi pages are not perfectly aligned: one page states that qualifications and some fields can be updated, while the homepage uses more restrictive wording about post-submission changes. The detailed FAQ specifically says passport information generally **cannot** be updated after an application has been submitted. [2–3]

**Follow the controls and instructions visible in your account**; if the platform blocks a required change, use its official support channel.

### Step 4 — Save correspondence and keep documents ready

Save the official submission confirmation and correspondence. Keep the original documents and any necessary translations accessible, but do not pay for an unnecessary translation or attestation without checking the receiving program's instructions.

For detailed status definitions, see [Study in Saudi Application Status — Under Process and Results](/blog/study-in-saudi-application-status-under-process). For technical submission problems, see [Study in Saudi Portal Problems and Errors](/blog/study-in-saudi-portal-problems-errors).

## What to prepare if you missed the October 2026 intake

Use the waiting period strategically:

1. **Check passport details** against your academic records, especially names and dates of birth.
2. **Identify degree level** and the qualification you will use: FSc/FA/ICS/A-Level for many bachelor routes; a bachelor's degree for many master's routes; an appropriate master's or equivalent for PhD.
3. **Choose programs before ordering translations.** Language and supporting-document rules differ between programs.
4. **Read the program's age, marks/CGPA and test requirements.** Do not assume a universal 60%, 70% or 3.0 CGPA cutoff.
5. **Prepare readable scans** of certificates, transcripts, passport and any program-specific documents.
6. **Check attestation steps** with the institution's current requirements; do not confuse apostille with translation certification.
7. **Review scholarship funding type.** Full Scholarship, Partial Scholarship and Paid Study are not equivalent.
8. **Monitor official announcements** rather than a recycled third-party countdown.

## Are all Saudi scholarship programs fully funded?

**No.** The Saudi Ministry of Education distinguishes full scholarships, partial scholarships and paid programs; the official Study in Saudi program catalogue visibly contains Paid Study listings alongside scholarship offerings. [4–5]

| Listing | Interpretation | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Full Scholarship | Program displays a full scholarship option | Specific benefits, eligibility and selection conditions |
| Partial Scholarship | Some costs or benefits may be covered | What remains payable, including living costs |
| Paid Study | Applicant bears the relevant study costs | Tuition and other published charges |
| Program listing only | Program exists in the catalogue | Whether applications are open for your applicant category |

Do not claim a tuition waiver, monthly stipend, flight ticket or accommodation for a particular program unless its official listing confirms the benefit.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. What is the Study in Saudi application deadline for 2026?

The officially announced international/non-Saudi window was **16 September through 6 October 2026**. It is closed. [1]

### 2. Is the Saudi scholarship portal open for new applications now?

The previously announced 2026 unified intake is closed. Check the live official program page and intake notice for any distinct new opportunity before submitting.

### 3. What is the Study in Saudi 2027 application deadline?

A new overall 2027/28 deadline was **not confirmed** by the official sources reviewed on 5 November 2026. Do not trust unverified reopening predictions.

### 4. Do Saudi scholarships always open in September?

No universal annual opening rule should be assumed. Historical dates do not establish future deadlines for every university or program.

### 5. Did King Abdulaziz University confirm October 6?

Yes. KAU's official 8 September 2026 announcement specifies **6 October 2026** as the close of its announced non-Saudi admissions period via Study in Saudi. [1]

### 6. Is the KSU semester-start date the scholarship deadline?

No. Semester-start dates and scholarship application deadlines are different. Use an actual admissions announcement for the applicable applicant category.

### 7. Can I still edit my application after submission?

Some fields have restrictions. Official portal FAQ pages differ in wording, and the detailed FAQ says submitted passport numbers generally cannot be changed. Follow your portal account and official support guidance. [2–3]

### 8. What does Under Process mean?

The official FAQ says the application is being reviewed by the relevant educational institution and processed according to procedures. It does not imply acceptance. [2]

### 9. Can I apply for a new university after the intake closes?

Not through an expired application window. Investigate separate currently open university routes only where the institution explicitly announces them.

### 10. Can a Pakistani student apply without IELTS?

Some programs may have different proof-of-language requirements. Others expressly request IELTS or an equivalent test. Check the chosen program's conditions; do not treat “without IELTS” as a universal exemption. [5]

### 11. Is Arabic translation mandatory for every Saudi scholarship?

No uniform mandatory-Arabic rule for every program is established. Follow the program's official language and attachment instructions.

### 12. What if my scholarship application is rejected?

Read the actual decision and applicable official guidance. The portal FAQ says rejection can occur where the applicant does not meet one or more university/program requirements. [2]

### 13. Do I need a consultant to apply through Study in Saudi?

No. Applicants can use the official portal themselves. Private services can assist with document review or translation but cannot promise admission or influence a government decision.

### 14. Can I apply for a paid program through Study in Saudi?

The official programme directory includes paid offerings. Review the conditions, fees, intake and applicant classification separately. [5]

### 15. Will every university announce results on the same date?

No single confirmed result date was identified across all universities. Monitor your account and the selected university's official notifications.

### 16. How do I get notified of a new intake?

Monitor the official Study in Saudi portal and the admissions pages of the universities you select. Do not send passwords to third-party pages advertising “priority registration.”
