## NADRA FRC Translation: What Does an FRC Show?

The **Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** is a NADRA-issued record of a registered person's family composition. Depending on the requested category, it may show **parents and siblings (by birth)**, **spouse and children (by marriage)** or family information relevant to a registered guardian relationship. NADRA's record depends on family members being correctly registered in its database; missing or inconsistent records should be corrected through NADRA, not adjusted by a translator.

People search for *family registration certificate translation* when applying for a spouse visa, child-related application, family reunion, civil-record verification or family-status procedure overseas. The translation must reproduce the relationship details actually printed on the FRC. It must not invent an omitted relative, change the legal category of a relationship or reinterpret an ID number.

**What we review on an FRC translation:**

- Applicant and relatives' names as written on the source record.
- Parent, spouse and child relationship labels; family grouping and sequence.
- Identity or registration numbers, including leading zeros and punctuation.
- Dates of birth and other dates in their printed format.
- Issuer, verification text, QR/code references and visible official marks.
- Any mismatched spelling between the FRC, passport, CNIC/NICOP, CRC and marriage records, flagged for the client rather than silently corrected.

**Check the source:** [NADRA Family Registration Certificate](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/identityDocument/certificates/frc) and the [U.S. Department of State's Pakistan civil-document guidance](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Pakistan.html).

## FRC vs CRC/B-Form vs Birth Certificate vs MRC: Which Document Do You Need?

These records serve different purposes. Choosing the wrong one may delay an application even when its translation is perfect.

| Document | Meaning and issuing context | Usually used to demonstrate | Translation consideration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FRC** | NADRA Family Registration Certificate | Composition of a registered family group | Translate all listed relatives and the correct *by birth* or *by marriage* category. |
| **CRC / B-Form** | NADRA Child Registration Certificate for a child under 18 | Child identity and registered family relationship | Keep child and parent details exact; some checklists expressly require an English translation. |
| **Birth Registration Certificate** | Civil birth record issued through the competent local authority, often in a computerised format linked to NADRA | Birth, parentage, date and place of birth | Verify issuing authority and whether source is already in the destination's accepted language. |
| **MRC** | Marriage Registration Certificate issued by the competent civil-registration authority | Registered marriage | Not identical to the full Nikah Nama marriage contract. |
| **Nikah Nama** | Original marriage contract / registration record | Contractual details of the marriage | Handwritten Urdu and names may require careful reconciliation; translate the complete record when required. |
| **Death Certificate** | Civil death registration from competent local authority | Registered death | Do not confuse death-registration evidence with NADRA's cancellation of a deceased person's CNIC. |

**A common mistake:** A document displaying the NADRA name is not necessarily *issued by NADRA in the same legal capacity* as every other document. For birth, marriage and death registration, the [U.S. State Department's Pakistan guidance](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Pakistan.html) identifies relevant **local government issuing authorities**, while NADRA maintains linked identity and family data.

### Is an FRC accepted instead of a birth certificate?

**Sometimes—for a specific process, not everywhere.** The U.S. Department of State's Pakistan reciprocity page lists **CRC/B-Form or FRC as alternative documents in lieu of a birth registration certificate**. That statement is specific to its civil-document instructions; it is **not a universal substitution rule** for Italy, Switzerland, Saudi Arabia or every private institution. For other destinations, an official checklist may demand **both** the birth certificate and FRC.

If the receiving authority requests a *birth certificate* expressly, check its destination-specific rules before substituting an FRC. LISAN.PK can translate the documents you provide, but only the authority can confirm what it will accept. [Read the official U.S. Pakistan reciprocity table](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Pakistan.html).

### Is a Child Registration Certificate (CRC) the same as a birth certificate?

**No.** CRC—often called a *B-Form*—is a child's NADRA identity record; a **birth registration certificate** is the civil record of a birth issued by the competent registration office. A particular immigration authority may recognise CRC for an identified purpose, but this does not make the two records universally interchangeable. NADRA confirms CRC is intended for children **under 18**. [NADRA CRC guidance](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/identityDocument/juvenile).

## FRC Translation for Italy Family Reunion

**Italy family-reunion applications may require FRC, specific civil documents, Italian translation and a Pakistani apostille.** The exact bundle changes for a spouse, child or parent; do not reuse someone else's checklist without checking your own visa category and the relevant Italian mission.

The **Italian Embassy in Islamabad's family-reunion checklist** identifies:

- **Spouse:** Marriage Registration Certificate, Nikah Nama, FRC and the applicant's birth registration certificate; additional evidence where relevant.
- **Child:** Child Registration Certificate **and** birth certificate, the parents' marriage records, FRC, and other parental-consent or custody evidence where applicable.
- **Parent joining child:** The inviting child's birth registration certificate and an FRC; dependency evidence is also listed.

The checklist refers to Pakistani **apostille** for relevant documents. Official Italian consular guidance says Pakistani public documents for Italian use require an apostille and an **Italian translation** meeting its stated conformity procedure. The Embassy in Islamabad describes *“per traduzione conforme”* for the translation; the Consulate in Karachi separately describes original document + Italian translation + apostille. **The correct method of translating and validating the translation should be checked with the mission handling your case**, particularly whether a translation needs separate formal confirmation.

**Do not assume:** An English FRC, an apostille on the source certificate, or an ordinary translation stamp automatically meets Italy's Italian-translation requirements. Nor does an apostille itself certify the translation's linguistic accuracy.

**Official sources:** [Italian Embassy Islamabad — Family Reunion Checklist (PDF)](https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Family-reunion-visa-requirments-1.pdf); [Italian Embassy — translation and apostille guidance](https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-italiano/traduzione-e-legalizzazione-dei-documenti/); [Consulate General of Italy in Karachi](https://conskarachi.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-straniero/traduzione-e-legalizzazione-dei-documenti/).

### Does an FRC need an apostille for Italy?

**For the family-reunion process described in the Italian Embassy's published checklist, Pakistani supporting civil documents are submitted under its apostille requirement.** Whether your specific FRC needs a particular apostille or accompanying translation certification should be confirmed against the checklist applicable on the **day you submit**. Pakistani apostilles are obtained from the **competent Pakistani authority**, not from LISAN.PK. For a general overview, see our [Pakistan apostille guide](/guides/apostille-pakistan) and [apostille country-acceptance guide](/guides/pakistan-apostille-country-list).

## CRC/B-Form English Translation for Switzerland Family Reunion

The **Swiss Embassy in Pakistan's published family-reunion checklist** calls for a NADRA **Child Registration Certificate (CRC) with English translation** when a child is concerned. The same checklist also lists an original birth certificate, the relevant FRC category, and additional marriage or custody records depending on the family situation. An English-translated CRC alone does **not** replace the entire checklist.

In its available checklist (version **02/2025**), the Embassy distinguishes **FRC by birth** from **FRC by marriage** and specifies the information expected in each. Because checklist versions and individual cantonal requests may change, clients should always use the current document list linked through the [Embassy of Switzerland in Pakistan](https://www.schweiz-pakistan.eda.admin.ch/en/visa-entry-to-and-residence-in-switzerland).

**Source:** [Swiss Embassy Pakistan family-reunion requirements (PDF)](https://www.eda.admin.ch/content/dam/countries/countries-content/pakistan/visa-information/checklist-2025-pk_EN.pdf).

## FRC, CRC and Birth Records for U.S. Immigration / NVC

For U.S. immigrant visa cases, first check which civil documents the **National Visa Center (NVC)** and the relevant U.S. post request. The U.S. Department of State's **Pakistan reciprocity schedule** recognises FRC and CRC as possible alternatives for a birth-registration certificate, but evidence of the family relationship may still require additional records. Do not submit an FRC *instead of* an MRC or a Nikah Nama when the case specifically asks for marriage evidence.

If a foreign-language civil record is submitted to **USCIS**, the regulatory rule generally requires a **complete English translation accompanied by a translator's certification of completeness, accuracy and competence**. For the **NVC/consular** stage, follow the Department of State's document and translation instructions for your case and embassy; do not copy USCIS wording blindly into every application scenario.

**Official sources:** [U.S. Pakistan Reciprocity Schedule](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Pakistan.html); [NVC Civil Documents FAQ](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/civil-documents-faq.html); [8 CFR § 103.2(b)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-103.2).

For marriage records, see [Nikah Nama and MRC translation services](/services/translation/nikah-nama-marriage-certificate).

## Death Certificate Translation and the Role of NADRA

For a deceased family member, some visa, inheritance, pension and dependent applications require a **registered death certificate** and a translation into English, Arabic, Italian or another language. The **competent local civil authority** issues the death certificate in Pakistan; NADRA also records the death for relevant identity-document administration. These are not identical functions.

We translate the name of the deceased, date and place of death, registration numbers, relationship information, issuing office, seals and remarks as they appear. We cannot independently confirm a death, create an official death record, or change a discrepancy in the source certificate. Applicants should resolve missing registration details with the issuing authority before translation.

## MRC Translation and Marriage-Record Consistency

The **Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC)** provides registered civil evidence of marriage. The **Nikah Nama** may contain fuller contractual information, including witnesses and marriage terms. For family applications, an authority may require one or both; never assume the MRC automatically replaces the full Nikah Nama or vice versa.

For families where a spouse's surname or parent's name appears differently across the FRC, Nikah Nama, MRC, passport or CRC, we recommend checking the original issuing records first. A translator should reproduce the source accurately and highlight discrepancies; **the translation must not fabricate a corrected record**.

## Common Mistakes That Delay a Family-Reunion File

- Sending an **FRC by birth** when the authority expressly asks for **FRC by marriage**, or vice versa.
- Treating a CRC/B-Form as an automatic substitute for a local birth registration certificate.
- Assuming an Italian checklist accepts an English-only translation.
- Ordering an apostille on the source and assuming the apostille also proves the translation is accurate.
- Using a child's nickname or a guessed Latin spelling instead of verifying the passport/CRC consistency.
- Translating only the first page of a multi-page family record.
- Omitting footnotes, seals, QR codes, issuing authority or important reverse-side text.
- Claiming that a translator or a private agency can “approve” the final immigration application.

## FAQs: FRC, CRC and Family-Document Translation

### 1. Can I get my NADRA FRC translated into English?

Yes. We can translate the information on a legible NADRA FRC into English and prepare a translator declaration when requested. Check whether your receiving authority needs a translated FRC or accepts the existing language.

### 2. Can an FRC be translated into Italian?

Yes. For Italy, confirm the required translation-conformity procedure with the Italian mission handling your application; a translated document alone may not satisfy its apostille and conformity requirements.

### 3. Is FRC required for Italy family reunification?

The Italian Embassy in Islamabad's published family-reunion checklist lists FRC for spouse, child and parent categories. The remaining required documents differ by family relationship and application type. Use the latest checklist for your actual category.

### 4. Does the FRC need an apostille for Italy?

The Italian mission's published instructions require apostilled Pakistani public documents for applicable use. Verify whether your particular submission requires an apostille on that FRC, what translation conformity is required, and whether the receiving mission has additional instructions.

### 5. Does Switzerland require an English translation of the CRC?

The Swiss Embassy Pakistan family-reunion checklist version 02/2025 explicitly identifies a CRC with English translation when children are included. Verify the current checklist on application day.

### 6. Is CRC the same as a B-Form?

Yes; in Pakistan the Child Registration Certificate is commonly known as a B-Form. It is a child identity record, not the same issuing document as a local birth-registration certificate.

### 7. Can an FRC replace a birth certificate for NVC?

The U.S. Department of State's Pakistan reciprocity guidance lists FRC or CRC as alternative birth documents. That does not eliminate any other relationship or civil-document evidence required for the individual visa case.

### 8. Do I need FRC by birth or FRC by marriage?

The correct type depends on whose family relationship the authority wants to see. An FRC by birth generally presents parents and siblings; an FRC by marriage generally presents spouse and children. Follow the receiving authority's instructions.

### 9. Can you translate a NADRA family certificate into Arabic?

Yes, when Arabic is the requested language. First confirm whether the authority requires Arabic, English or another language and whether an additional official step is necessary.

### 10. Can I translate an MRC instead of the Nikah Nama?

We can translate either document. Whether one can replace the other is decided by the receiving authority, not by the translator. Some spouse-visa checklists ask for both.

### 11. Is an FRC a marriage certificate?

No. It is a family-composition record. An MRC documents registered marriage, and the Nikah Nama is the marriage-contract record.

### 12. Do you translate Pakistani death certificates?

Yes. We translate readable registered death certificates for the requested language and intended use. Obtain or correct the official civil record through its competent issuing authority.

### 13. Do I need a stamped or certified translation?

That depends on the authority. For example, USCIS has its own translator-certification rule, while Italy's mission explains a particular Italian translation-conformity process. A generic agency stamp should not be assumed sufficient everywhere.

### 14. Can I send a phone photo instead of the original document?

A clear photograph may be suitable for obtaining a translation quotation or preparing a draft. The authority receiving the final application may still require the original civil record, its apostille and/or a physical translation.

### 15. How long does FRC translation take?

The turnaround is confirmed after reviewing language, page count, scan quality and certification needs. We do not promise a fixed urgent delivery time before evaluating the actual document.

### 16. Will translation fix a missing family member or inconsistent name on the FRC?

No. The NADRA or civil-registration authority must correct the source record. We translate what is actually printed and flag inconsistencies that may affect the file.

### 17. Can I get an FRC translation without an apostille?

Often a translation can be prepared independently. Whether the **final application** needs an apostille, notarisation or other verification depends on the destination authority's instructions.

### 18. Are you the agency issuing the FRC, CRC or MRC?

No. LISAN.PK provides translation services; it does not issue NADRA identity records, local-government civil certificates, visas or embassy approvals.
