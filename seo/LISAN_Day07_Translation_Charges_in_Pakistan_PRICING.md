## How much does it cost to translate one page in Pakistan?

There is **no universal translation price per page in Pakistan**. Some providers charge per page, some per document, and some by word count or complexity. LISAN.PK's **PKR 1,200 standard rate is per qualifying document, not a blanket price for any page of any length**. A four-page handwritten marriage contract or dense technical contract is not equivalent to a short printed certificate.

Prices can change with:

- **Source and target languages:** Urdu → Arabic, English → Arabic and Arabic → English require different linguistic checks.
- **Length and density:** A short degree certificate may be simpler than a transcript with dozens of course titles.
- **Handwriting and legibility:** Handwritten Urdu, stamps, marginal notes or unclear scans need additional review.
- **Terminology:** Legal, medical, engineering and corporate records require subject-specific care.
- **Requested format:** A plain digital translation is not necessarily the same deliverable as a signed or stamped certification.
- **Time requirement:** An urgent deadline is an availability question, not an automatic promise.
- **Delivery:** Scanned delivery and physical document dispatch have different potential costs.

**Example:** If you have a short, clearly printed degree certificate, ask whether it falls under the PKR 1,200 standard rate. If you have a transcript, a multi-page contract or a handwritten Nikah Nama, send the complete pages first; do not assume a one-page advertisement applies to the full job.

## Certified translation cost in Pakistan: what is included?

A request for a **certified translation** usually means the receiving organisation expects a translation accompanied by a statement about its accuracy and the translator's responsibility. The exact format depends on the destination and purpose. A translator's certification is **not** a government-issued stamp of approval for the underlying document.

When requesting a quote, ask these five questions:

| Question to ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does the price include the entire document? | Reverse-side notes, stamps and annexes may need translation. |
| Is a signed accuracy statement available? | Some authorities require specific certification wording and contact details. |
| Is a physical signed or stamped copy included? | A digital PDF and paper copy are not interchangeable in every process. |
| Is notarisation required and priced separately? | Notarisation concerns a separate legal act; it should not be assumed. |
| Who pays courier, MOFA, apostille or other authority fees? | Translation charges and government/third-party fees are different expenses. |

### What does USCIS require for translated documents?

For documents submitted to **U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS)** that contain a foreign language, **8 CFR § 103.2(b)(3)** requires a **full English translation** and a translator's certification that the translation is complete and accurate and that the translator is competent. The rule does **not** set a Pakistani translation-business price. **There is no standard “USCIS translation fee in Pakistan” set by USCIS**; the translation provider quotes its own service charge. An Arabic translation is not a substitute for the required English translation in a USCIS filing.

**Official requirement:** [8 CFR § 103.2(b)(3), translations](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/8/103.2).

### What about UK visa translation?

For **UK Standard Visitor visa** supporting documents not in English or Welsh, GOV.UK states that a full, independently verifiable translation must contain accuracy confirmation, the translation date, the translator's full name and signature, and contact details. Requirements can vary by visa route, so check the rules applying to your application. These are **format requirements**, not a UK-government price list for translators in Pakistan.

**Official requirement:** [GOV.UK visitor visa supporting documents](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visitor-visa-guide-to-supporting-documents/guide-to-supporting-documents-visiting-the-uk).

## Translation, notarisation, HEC/MOFA attestation and apostille: different charges

These terms describe different processes. They are **not interchangeable**, and paying for one does not automatically provide the others.

| Item | What it is | How to check cost |
|---|---|---|
| Translation | Rendering source-language text into the requested language | LISAN.PK price/quote by document and scope. |
| Certified translation | Translation accompanied by an agreed certification statement or equivalent deliverable | Ask exactly what certification is included and whether it changes the quote. |
| Notarisation | Separate notarial act, if required | Ask if available and obtain a separate quote. |
| HEC degree attestation | A government process for eligible academic records | Check HEC's official portal and fee information; not included in translation by default. |
| Pakistan MOFA attestation | Government document authentication | Check the current MOFA portal and applicable category. |
| Apostille | Origin authentication of an eligible public document under the Apostille Convention | Check official MOFA fees and destination eligibility; distinct from the cost of translating the document. |
| Courier | Shipping of physical files where needed | Charged by delivery arrangement, not automatically included. |

A destination authority may accept an English original and need **no Arabic translation at all**, or may require an original document to be authenticated before a separate translation is prepared. Verify the **receiving authority's exact instructions** before ordering an unnecessary service.

## Frequently asked questions about translation charges in Pakistan

### 1. What is the cost of document translation in Pakistan in 2026?

LISAN.PK lists **PKR 1,200** for a qualifying normal document or degree translation, and **PKR 2,500** for a bachelor's transcript. These are LISAN.PK's own rates; other companies and more complex documents can cost differently.

### 2. Is PKR 1,200 a fixed price for every page?

No. It is LISAN.PK's published **standard per-document category**. Large, complex, handwritten or specialist documents require review and may be quoted differently.

### 3. How much does Arabic translation cost per page in Pakistan?

There is no single national rate. Send the full document and specify English → Arabic or Urdu → Arabic; the quote will reflect length, complexity and the required certification format.

### 4. How much does a degree translation cost?

LISAN.PK lists **PKR 1,200** for a normal degree/document translation. Confirm that your degree fits the standard scope and whether any additional certificate is required.

### 5. What is the bachelor's transcript translation price?

LISAN.PK currently lists **PKR 2,500** for a bachelor's transcript translation. Provide all transcript pages to confirm the scope.

### 6. What is the Nikah Nama translation price?

A full Nikah Nama is assessed separately, particularly if handwritten or multi-page. Request a quote rather than assuming the price of a short MRC applies to the marriage contract.

### 7. Is NADRA MRC translation the same price as Nikah Nama translation?

Not necessarily. They are different records with different content and lengths. Send the correct document and destination requirements for an exact quote.

### 8. Are certified translation and notarisation the same thing?

No. Certification concerns the translation and the certification statement provided with it; notarisation is a separate legal procedure when required.

### 9. Is MOFA attestation included in the translation price?

Not unless the written quote explicitly says so. Government fees, authentication and courier costs should be itemised separately from the translation service.

### 10. Can I obtain a certified translation for a U.S. immigration filing?

Ask for a full **English** translation with the certification required by **8 CFR § 103.2(b)(3)** and confirm the exact deliverable before ordering. The U.S. rule does not set the provider's fee.

### 11. Does the UK require a translated document for every visa application?

Requirements depend on the visa route and document. For Standard Visitor applications, GOV.UK describes independently verifiable translations for submitted documents not in English or Welsh. Check the instructions for your own route.

### 12. Is there an express or same-day translation fee?

No universal express rate is published here. Urgent availability and any surcharge must be confirmed in an itemised quote; no fixed delivery-time guarantee is implied.

### 13. Are courier fees included?

Do not assume so. Physical shipping and special delivery arrangements should be stated separately if requested.

### 14. Do I pay separately for translation of a document's reverse side?

The quote should cover **all content that needs translating**. Send both sides before receiving the price so there are no surprises about stamps, annexes or reverse-side notes.

### 15. What is the difference between scholarship translation packages and full application support?

The published **PKR 12,000 / PKR 15,000** prices relate to scholarship documents-and-translation packages. Full consultancy or application-management work is separate and quoted according to scope.

### 16. Can I get a quotation before sending original documents?

Yes—request an initial review using **clear scans or photos**. The team will tell you whether originals or physical copies are required for the agreed service.
