### Before a real translator signs

- Verify the correct document, reference and language pair.
- Ensure all source pages included in the submission were actually translated.
- Check personal names, dates, numbers, handwritten details, seals and notes.
- Replace every bracketed field with truthful information; remove unused fields where the recipient permits.
- Confirm that the translator is competent in the named language pair.
- Confirm the receiving authority's instructions for signature, dates, name/contact details, credentials, notarisation and any additional certification steps.
- Do not use a digitally generated signature in place of a real signature without confirming acceptance.
- Do not add “USCIS approved,” “embassy approved,” “government attested” or any other endorsement unless genuinely and specifically documented.

### Basis for common wording

- **USCIS:** 8 CFR 103.2(b)(3) requires a full English translation and certification that it is complete and accurate, plus the translator's competence to translate into English: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-103.2
- **UK visitor visa supporting documents:** GOV.UK lists accuracy confirmation, translation date, translator's full name/signature and contact details: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visitor-visa-guide-to-supporting-documents/guide-to-supporting-documents-visiting-the-uk
