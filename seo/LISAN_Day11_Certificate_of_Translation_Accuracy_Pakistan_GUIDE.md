# Certificate of Translation Accuracy in Pakistan: Requirements, Example and Downloadable Sample

**A Certificate of Translation Accuracy is a statement signed or otherwise genuinely issued by the translator confirming that a translation is complete and accurate and identifying the translator's competence and contact details as required by the receiving authority.** In Pakistan, a certificate may accompany an Urdu-to-English, Arabic-to-English or other professional translation, but **a certificate does not itself legalise the underlying document or guarantee acceptance by an embassy**. [1–3]

> **Quick answer:** For **USCIS**, the translator must certify that the **full English translation is complete and accurate** and that the translator is **competent to translate from the original language into English**. For many **UK visa** applications, official guidance calls for an accurate translation with the **translation date, translator's name/signature and contact details**. Requirements vary by authority, visa route and receiving institution; use the wording actually required for your application. [1–3]

## What is a Certificate of Translation Accuracy?

**It is a declaration about the translation work, not a new government-issued civil record.** The purpose is to connect a specific source document with a corresponding translation and identify the person taking responsibility for its accuracy.

The certificate may be placed on the last page of the translation or provided as an attached page. Its useful information typically includes:

- The document title, such as *Nikah Nama*, *Marriage Registration Certificate*, *Birth Certificate*, *Degree*, *Transcript* or *Fard-e-Malkiat*.
- The original language and target language.
- A statement that the translation is complete and accurate to the translator's knowledge and ability.
- The translator's name and an explicit statement of competence where relevant.
- The date the translation or declaration was completed.
- Genuine signature and contact details where required.
- A document reference or version identifier, if used by the translator's workflow.

**Do not present a certification page with no actual translation as evidence that a document has been translated.** A certification statement should be attached to the complete translation and checked against the source before use.

## Does a certificate make a translation “embassy approved”?

**No.** A translator can attest to the quality of their own translation. An embassy, immigration office, court, university or other receiving body decides what it will accept. A translation certificate cannot grant government authorisation that the business or translator does not actually hold.

A receiving body may require, depending on the case:

- A translator's signed accuracy declaration.
- An identifiable translation company or professional translator.
- A notarised translator statement or affidavit.
- Particular credentials or qualifications.
- A specified language pair or translation format.
- Additional authentication of the **original**, the **translation** or the **signatory**, under its own rules.

**Certified**, **notarised**, **attested** and **apostilled** are not interchangeable words. Learn the differences in [Certified Translation vs Notarization vs Apostille](/guides/certified-translation-vs-apostille-pakistan).

## What does USCIS require on a translation certificate?

**USCIS regulation 8 CFR 103.2(b)(3) requires a full English translation for foreign-language documents submitted to USCIS and a translator's certification of both completeness/accuracy and competence.** The regulation is about the translator's statement, not a universally mandatory embassy stamp. [1]

| Element | USCIS regulatory requirement | Practical document preparation |
|---|---|---|
| Full English-language translation | **Yes**, for foreign-language documents filed with USCIS | Translate all visible relevant text; do not omit stamps/remarks without clearly recording them. |
| Statement that translation is complete and accurate | **Yes** | Include this declaration in the translator's own certification. |
| Statement of translator's competence in language pair | **Yes** | State competence to translate from the source language into English. |
| Translator identification | Needed to make a genuine, accountable certification | Use actual name and identifiers, not a fictitious signature or fabricated company officer. |
| Notarisation for every submitted translation | **Not stated as a blanket requirement by this regulation** | Check special form/agency instructions before purchasing extra services. |
| “USCIS-approved translator” licence | **Not created by this regulation** | Do not market a translator as formally endorsed by USCIS without verifiable authorisation. |

**Example:** If you submit an Urdu Nikah Nama to USCIS, the accompanying English translation should cover the full submitted record. Its translator statement should certify completeness/accuracy and language competence. A generic company stamp without those declarations is a poor substitute for the actual USCIS wording. [1]

## What does the UK require for a certified translation?

**UK rules depend on the application type.** GOV.UK's guidance for a visitor-visa supporting document written in a language other than English or Welsh calls for a full translation that can be independently verified, with an accuracy confirmation, the translation date, translator's full name and signature, and contact details. [2]

The general GOV.UK page on certifying a document similarly asks the translator to confirm a true and accurate translation, include the date and provide their full name and contact details. [3]

| Field | GOV.UK visitor visa guidance | GOV.UK general certification guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Accurate translation statement | Yes | Yes |
| Translation date | Yes | Yes |
| Full translator name | Yes | Yes |
| Translator signature | **Yes, explicitly** | The general page lists name/contact details; check receiving authority for signature specifics |
| Contact details | Yes | Yes |
| Extra wording or credentials | May vary by visa route | Depends on recipient's instructions |

**For a UK visa, never assume that a U.S.-focused template satisfies every Home Office rule.** Review the current instructions for the particular visa route. [2–3]

**This sample is an unsigned educational template. It is not a certified translation until a real, competent translator checks an actual source document and issues the statement truthfully.** Do not copy a fictional name, date, signature or company seal into a client's submission.

### Example: Urdu-to-English certificate of translation accuracy

> **CERTIFICATE OF TRANSLATION ACCURACY — SAMPLE / NOT VALID UNTIL COMPLETED AND SIGNED**
>
> Document: [Exact title printed on the source document]
>
> Source language: Urdu
>
> Target language: English
>
> Document reference (if applicable): [Reference]
>
> I, [Translator's full legal name], certify that I am competent to translate from Urdu into English and that the attached English translation of the identified document is complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge and ability.
>
> Translator's name: [Full name]
>
> Translator / translation business: [Accurate legal or trading name, if applicable]
>
> Contact details: [Genuine business email / phone / address as required]
>
> Date of translation: [Actual date]
>
> Signature: [Actual signature of the person making the declaration]
>
> Translation page count: [Number of translated pages]

**Why this sample includes competence wording:** USCIS explicitly requires the translator to certify competence and accuracy/completeness. Other authorities may prescribe different formats or additional fields. Do not remove mandatory fields simply to shorten the certificate. [1]

## Do I need the translator's signature and stamp?

**A genuine signature is important where the recipient expressly requires one.** UK visitor-visa guidance expressly calls for the translator's signature. USCIS's regulation requires the translator's certification; verify the actual filing instructions and certificate format used in your case. [1–2]

A company stamp can help identify the issuer but **is not a substitute** for missing declarations, a competent translator or a genuinely made certification. Also, a stamp labelled “embassy approved” cannot create an endorsement the embassy did not issue.

### Can a translation service sign the certificate?

An authorised individual may sign on behalf of a translation business where the receiving authority accepts that arrangement and the declaration accurately identifies the responsible translator or authorised signer. **Do not assume a corporate logo alone meets a translator-certification rule.**

### Can I sign my own translation certificate?

This is **recipient-specific**. Some authorities assess competence and conflicts differently. For immigration matters, check the particular authority's instructions rather than relying on an internet claim that self-certification is always accepted or always forbidden. A statement must never claim qualifications the signer lacks.

## Which Pakistani documents commonly need certified translation?

A translation certificate can accompany many different records; **the reason for translating them determines the rule**.

| Original Pakistani document | Common translation direction | Where a certificate might be requested | Important distinction |

## Do I need notarisation or an apostille as well?

**Not automatically.** A signed accuracy certificate is about the translation; notarisation generally concerns the notarial act and/or signature; an apostille authenticates the origin of a public document under the applicable international regime. None replaces the others for every destination.

A useful sequence for planning is:

1. Identify the **receiving authority** and the exact document it requests.
2. Check the required **language** and whether a certified translation is necessary.
3. Ask if the **original**, the translation, or a separate declaration needs notarisation, attestation or apostille.
4. Obtain the translation and authentic certification in the order the recipient requires.
5. Preserve both the source record and the completed certified translation.

## Frequently asked questions about translation accuracy certificates

### 1. What is a certificate of translation accuracy?

It is a responsible translator's declaration that a specific translation is complete and accurate, with any additional information required by the receiving authority.

### 2. Is a certificate of translation accuracy compulsory for USCIS?

For foreign-language documents submitted to USCIS, **8 CFR 103.2(b)(3)** requires a full English translation and the translator's certification of accuracy/completeness and competence. [1]

### 3. Is a translator's signature required for UKVI?

The GOV.UK **visitor visa supporting documents guidance** specifically lists the translator's full name and signature, along with an accuracy statement, date and contact details. Always check the visa route. [2]

### 4. Is an accuracy certificate the same as a notarised translation?

No. They are different acts, and one does not automatically substitute for the other.

### 5. Does Pakistan issue a universal government certificate making every translation embassy approved?

Do not assume that. A translator's own statement of accuracy is not blanket government endorsement. Destination authorities set their own requirements.

### 6. Can a translation be certified without an ink stamp?

Requirements depend on the receiving authority and format. A decorative stamp cannot replace required accuracy or competence statements.

### 7. Is a blank downloaded certificate legally valid?

No. It becomes a genuine declaration only when an identifiable, competent person has translated and reviewed the specific document and truthfully completed the certificate.

### 8. Can I reuse the same certificate for different documents?

A certificate should identify the **actual document and translation** it covers. Do not reuse a signed certificate to imply it covers unrelated documents.

### 9. Can the certificate cover several translated pages?

It can refer to an identifiable attached translation set where the recipient permits, but ensure all covered pages are clearly identified and complete.

### 10. Should I translate the document's stamps and handwritten notes?

A full translation should account for the source content. If handwriting is unreadable, the translator should flag it accurately rather than inventing missing words.

### 11. Do I need the original document translated if it is already in English?

Not necessarily. Confirm the language and document-form requirements of the receiving authority; avoid unnecessary translation fees.

### 12. Does a translation certificate prove my Nikah Nama is authentic?

No. It attests to the translation, not the legal authenticity or civil registration status of the source.

### 13. Can a certificate guarantee visa approval?

No. Only the competent authority can decide an application.
