# Saudi University Program Finder for Pakistani Students

**Compare Saudi university programs by qualification level, institution, language, scholarship type and any published gender restrictions.** LISAN.PK's Program Finder is a research and shortlisting tool, **not** the official application system: a matching result does not prove that applications are currently open, that funding is guaranteed, or that a particular applicant is eligible. Confirm every result on the [official Study in Saudi program catalogue](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs) or the relevant university website. [1]

**Information checked: 5 November 2026.** University programs and intake conditions may change without advance notice. The announced Study in Saudi window of **16 September–6 October 2026** is closed; separate university admission cycles may differ. KAUST Fall 2027 graduate applications operate through **KAUST's own portal**, with a different published deadline. [2][3]

## What is the difference between scholarship and paid study?

A **scholarship** may cover some or all study and living costs, depending on the actual award. **Paid study** requires the student to cover specified costs, and other programs may have funding information that is incomplete or pending. The official Study in Saudi catalogue includes study categories that must not be shown as universally fully funded. A page that simply lists 'available' programs may also include courses whose current application window has ended. [1]

| Label in results | What it should mean |
|---|---|
| Scholarship | Official institution or program evidence indicates a scholarship route |
| Paid study | Official program record identifies a fee-funded/paid option |
| Funding not confirmed | Available evidence is insufficient to classify this program |
| Intake closed | Verified announced application window has ended |
| Check intake | No reliable current opening/deadline data available |

## Can female Pakistani students apply to Saudi universities?

**Yes, many institutions have programs available to female international students, but eligibility is program-specific.** Check the official program's gender, residency and study-level fields. Do not assume that a university-wide claim of admitting women makes every program available to women, or vice versa. When the underlying record does not confirm eligibility, the finder should show **Not confirmed** rather than guess. [1]

## How to verify a program before applying

1. Open the selected university's official listing or [Study in Saudi program page](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs).
2. Confirm the **exact program title**, degree level, faculty and study language.
3. Check that the applicant's **nationality/residency category**, gender and prior qualification fit the stated requirements.
4. Read the scholarship/paid-study classification, official fees (if relevant) and application dates.
5. Check English/Arabic tests, academic marks, interviews and document format requirements.
6. Recheck the information directly on the application day.
7. Use only the university's or Ministry's official application link for submission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Program Finder an official Saudi government service?

No. It is an independent LISAN.PK comparison tool. Official applications and decisions belong to the relevant universities and [Study in Saudi](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en). [1]

### Does a matched program guarantee scholarship approval?

No. Eligibility screening and scholarships are competitive and depend on the institution's official assessment.

### Are all programs free?

No. Study in Saudi lists **paid** options as well as scholarship-related opportunities. Always inspect the actual funding category. [1]

### Does 'open' mean the deadline has not passed?

Only when the institution's official current-cycle dates are verified. An available program description does not by itself prove active application intake.

### Does KAUST use the same application window as KAU and KSU?

No. KAUST graduate applicants use KAUST's own published admissions timeline and portal. [3]

### Will LISAN.PK apply on my behalf automatically?

No. The finder helps students research programs; it does not submit applications or collect official portal credentials.

## Documents and translation guidance

Depending on the university and program, applicants may need educational certificates, transcripts, passports, language scores and certified translations. **Do not assume Arabic translation is mandatory for every scholarship or that every English-language document must be translated again.** Confirm language direction, attestation and document acceptance with the university.
