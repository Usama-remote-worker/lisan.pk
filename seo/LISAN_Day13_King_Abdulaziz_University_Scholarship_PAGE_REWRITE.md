# King Abdulaziz University Scholarship for Pakistani Students: 2026/27 Deadline and Requirements

**King Abdulaziz University (KAU) announced a Study in Saudi application period of 16 September to 6 October 2026 for non-Saudi undergraduate and graduate candidates living inside or outside the Kingdom. That announced window is closed.** Pakistani applicants seeking a future KAU scholarship should monitor the official Study in Saudi portal and university announcements; a new universal 2027 deadline was not confirmed in the reviewed official sources. [1]

**Verified: 5 November 2026.** KAU is in **Jeddah**. It is not the same as **King Saud University (KSU)** or **KAUST**, whose separate Fall 2027 graduate deadline is **3 January 2027**.

**Avoid a common mistake:** A university's next semester-start date is not proof that scholarship applications are open. Nor does KAUST's January 2027 deadline transfer to King Abdulaziz University.

## Does KAU offer fully funded scholarships to Pakistani students?

KAU describes scholarships for non-Saudi students at **bachelor's, master's and PhD** levels. Its general scholarship pages mention tuition support, housing, health services and student support arrangements; a legacy international scholarship page also lists stipends, arrival assistance, accommodation subject to availability and annual travel benefits. **Your actual funding depends on the scholarship category, selected program and offer letter.** Do not assume every available university course is fully funded. [2][3]

KAU distinguishes applicants living **outside** Saudi Arabia from those who already hold a valid Saudi residence permit. A Pakistani citizen applying from Pakistan will normally investigate the **external international** route; a Pakistani resident in Saudi Arabia must check the separate local-resident criteria. [2][4]

### Who can apply for KAU external scholarships?

The university's published scholarship material describes international applicant categories, but requirements differ by academic level, residency, department and cycle. Before applying, check:

- The chosen bachelor's, master's or PhD program is actually open to non-Saudi applicants.
- The applicant is eligible under the relevant **external** or **internal** route.
- The listed gender eligibility, study language, academic background and tests match the applicant.
- The relevant degree or prior certificate is recognized.
- Any specific age, recommendation-letter, language-test or document-attestation rules are fulfilled.
- The application is **open now**, not simply shown in an older announcement archive. [2][4][5]

**Special warning:** The university's legacy external-scholarship page describes master's age **up to 30** and PhD age **up to 35**, a generally **Very Good** qualification, and two academic recommendations, alongside example IELTS/TOEFL thresholds. Because this material is a **legacy page**, those numbers must not override current program-specific conditions or be advertised as universal 2027 rules. [3]

## KAU scholarship requirements: bachelor's vs master's vs PhD

| Study level | First qualification to verify | Additional program-specific checks |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's | Accepted high-school qualification, e.g., qualifying FSc/A-Level equivalent | Age, marks, subject prerequisites, language, residency and gender rules |
| Master's | Recognized relevant bachelor's degree | CGPA/class standing, English/Arabic test, references, department-specific requirements |
| PhD | Recognized relevant postgraduate qualification, where required | Research area, academic record, proposal/interview, supervisor/department requirements |

**No single CGPA, IELTS score or “without IELTS” promise applies to every KAU scholarship.** Degree/program restrictions can be narrower than a general university admission page. A graduate program shown on a university service page may also have a different application channel or funding status from the international Study in Saudi scholarship stream. [4][5]

## Is KAU open to female international students?

KAU's scholarship information describes international seats for **male and female** students, but **individual program availability must be checked** rather than assumed. The official Study in Saudi catalogue presents program-level attributes such as gender and study category. An overall university listing does not guarantee every program accepts every applicant. [3][5]

## Which documents are needed for King Abdulaziz University scholarship applications?

Prepare a working file before a relevant intake opens:

1. **Passport** — valid identity details consistent with the application.
2. **Educational certificates** — Matric/FSc or relevant BS/MS degrees depending on level.
3. **Transcripts / mark sheets** — complete academic records and degree classification.
4. **English or Arabic test evidence** — only if specified for your selected program.
5. **References, CV or statement** — based on the current program checklist.
6. **Medical or background documents** — only when officially required for your stage.
7. **Translation and attestation** — follow the receiving institution's actual published requirement; avoid blanket claims that every certificate requires Arabic translation.

### Do Pakistani documents have to be translated into Arabic?

**Not necessarily.** Some programs accept English originals; others request Arabic versions or particular certification. The official Study in Saudi FAQ itself tells students to follow their institution's requirements for attestations and documents. Avoid paying for unnecessary translation before checking the target program. [6]

## How to apply to King Abdulaziz University from Pakistan

1. Read the [official KAU scholarship information](https://kau.edu.sa/en/page/scholarship) and its latest announcements.
2. Visit the [Study in Saudi program search](https://studyinsaudi.sa/en/Programs).
3. Select **King Abdulaziz University**, if listed, and inspect the exact program.
4. Verify the program's **study level, location, language, gender, scholarship type and residency category**.
5. Check whether applications are open for the relevant cycle; the 16 September–6 October **2026** period is over.
6. Prepare required original records and any institution-requested certified translations.
7. Submit through the **officially designated** application platform only when a new window opens.
8. Track your status via the official portal and comply with any actual university requests.

**Important platform distinction:** KAU also operates a graduate admission e-service described on its website. That existence alone does not prove a specific international graduate **scholarship** intake is open or that the e-service replaces Study in Saudi for every foreign applicant. Follow the instructions linked from the current scholarship announcement. [1][7]

## After submission: Under Process, Returned or Rejected

The Ministry's Study in Saudi FAQ states that applications pass through institution review and may involve further Ministry/university processing. **Under Process means the application is being reviewed**, not that final admission is confirmed. Returned applications may permit some qualification-field corrections, but core identity information—including passport number—may be locked. [6]

## Frequently asked questions

### Is King Abdulaziz University scholarship open in November 2026?

The **announced 16 September–6 October 2026 window is closed**. Verify whether a distinct program or later intake has been officially opened; no new general 2027 window was confirmed in this review. [1]

### What is the King Abdulaziz University scholarship deadline 2027?

No new universal 2027 deadline was verified. Do not reuse 6 October 2026 or the separate KAUST deadline as a 2027 KAU date. [1]

### Does KAU offer master's and PhD scholarships?

KAU describes international postgraduate scholarship opportunities, subject to program and admission conditions. [2][3]

### Can Pakistani female students apply?

The university describes international opportunities for both male and female applicants, but eligibility depends on the specific course and track. [3][5]

### Does KAU require IELTS 5?

A legacy scholarship page lists IELTS 5 among example criteria, but it should **not** be presented as a current universal test threshold across all departments. Check the active program. [3]

No. Admission is competitive and program-specific. The reviewed sources do not support any guaranteed-admission CGPA.

### Does a Pakistani FSc certificate need Arabic translation?

Only when the receiving program or university requires that language. English-language originals are not automatically required to be translated into Arabic. [6]
