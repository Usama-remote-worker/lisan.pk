# HEC Degree Attestation Online in Pakistan (2026): Full DAS Application Guide

**HEC degree attestation is now an online, paperless process.** Under the new Degree Attestation System (DAS), effective **11 May 2026**, applicants submit scanned academic records, pay the government fee online, and receive an **HEC e-Attestation Certificate** after HEC scrutiny and verification by the awarding institution. HEC's current published fee is **PKR 3,000 per document**. Apply only through [HEC e-Services](https://eservices.hec.gov.pk). [Source: HEC official procedure](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx).

> **Quick answer:** You do **not** need to send your original degree to HEC or attend an HEC office for the new paperless attestation application. If you need to use your degree in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, or elsewhere, check the **receiving university, employer, visa authority and destination-country authentication rules separately**. An HEC e-Attestation Certificate does not by itself establish that every foreign authority has approved your document.

**Official process:** Online application → HEC scrutiny → university/HEI verification → HEC e-Attestation Certificate.

**Last checked:** 5 November 2026. This guide explains the current new process; older HEC help pages describing walk-in visits and courier-based paper stamping can still appear in search results.

## What changed in HEC attestation on 11 May 2026?

The Higher Education Commission launched the updated, fully online degree-attestation process on **11 May 2026**. The key change is not merely that the form moved online. Under the current model, the awarding university or higher-education institution participates electronically in verification, and HEC issues a downloadable **e-Attestation Certificate** after its final review. [HEC procedure](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx); [Associated Press of Pakistan, 11 May 2026](https://www.app.com.pk/national/hec-launches-fully-online-paperless-degree-attestation-system/).

| Question | New HEC DAS (2026) |
|---|---|
| Where do I apply? | [eservices.hec.gov.pk](https://eservices.hec.gov.pk) |
| Must I visit an HEC office? | **No**, for the new standard paperless DAS process |
| Must I send my original degree to HEC? | **No**, the new process uses electronic document submission and institutional verification |
| Who confirms the degree? | The awarding university/HEI provides verification to HEC |
| What does the applicant receive? | Downloadable HEC **e-Attestation Certificate** when approved |
| What is the fee? | **PKR 3,000 per selected document**, as currently published by HEC |
| Is it the same as MOFA or an apostille? | **No.** Foreign-use authentication is a separate question |

**Avoid outdated instructions:** If a third-party article tells you that every applicant must book an HEC walk-in appointment, send original certificates to a representative, or buy an agent's mandatory service, verify that claim against the **new** HEC system page first.

## How to apply for HEC degree attestation online: step by step

The sequence below follows HEC's published new DAS instructions. Minor dashboard labels can change as the portal is updated.

### Step 1: Register or sign in at the official portal

Open **[https://eservices.hec.gov.pk](https://eservices.hec.gov.pk)**. Create an account if you are a first-time user; otherwise sign in. Choose **Degree Attestation** from the dashboard menu.

Use your own email address and phone number. Do not share your password or one-time passcode with a third party. HEC specifically warns applicants about fraudulent portals and people pretending to offer official attestation services. [HEC scam alert](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Scam.aspx).

### Step 2: Check personal information against your documents

Enter your name, father's name, birth date and other personal details accurately. HEC checks the consistency of the application against CNIC/NADRA information and academic documents. A mismatch can prevent the process from progressing.

**Before paying, compare:**

- Name and father's name on CNIC, degree and transcript.
- Date of birth, where stated.
- University, campus/affiliated college and degree title.
- Registration, roll or student number.
- Study dates/session and program details.

Do not change the spelling on your application simply to match a future Arabic transliteration. Any actual discrepancy in a source document should be addressed with the issuing authority where required.

### Step 3: Enter your degree details

Select the correct qualification level, degree, awarding university/HEI, campus and academic session. HEC warns that similar degree titles may appear as separate options, so select the entry that matches your qualification.

Only request attestation of documents you genuinely need. Select the corresponding degree program before choosing its document types.

### Step 4: Choose the documents for attestation

HEC's current online system permits selection of:

1. **Degree**.
2. **Transcript** (including result card/detailed marks documentation where applicable).
3. **Provisional Certificate**, where issued by the university's examination office.
4. **HEC Equivalence Letter**.

HEC states that **attestation of photocopies is no longer offered** under the new procedure. It also permits qualifying complete or incomplete transcripts, subject to its specific conditions. [HEC official guidance](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx).

### Step 5: Upload clear scans

Upload readable scanned copies of the **front and back of your CNIC** and the academic records selected for attestation. Use the **Other Documents** area for any additional material relevant to your case, such as a professional registration credential if HEC asks for it. Follow the portal's accepted file type/size requirements.

Keep your scans straight, complete and legible. Do not crop out official text, registration numbers or stamps.

### Step 6: Review the undertaking and fee selection

Check every selected document and the calculated total before agreeing to the applicant undertaking. HEC states that fees caused by incorrect document selection are **not refundable, adjustable or transferable**.

### Step 7: Pay through 1-Link / 1-Bill and verify the payment

Click **Proceed for Payment** to generate the Consumer Number. Pay through a supported online banking/payment channel, then return to your HEC account and click **Verify Payment**.

**Crucial:** Making a payment alone does not necessarily mean your application has been submitted. HEC says the application is submitted automatically **after the payment is successfully verified**. Check that your dashboard shows the correct status. [HEC payment instructions](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx).

### Step 8: Track scrutiny and university verification

HEC checks your personal information, uploaded records, degree program and institution. If something is missing, the portal can send the application back with a revision request.

Open **Task Assigned to Me** or the relevant revision area, read the remarks, correct the actual deficiency, and resubmit. A complete application then goes to your university/HEI for confirmation against its records.

### Step 9: Download the e-Attestation Certificate

After university verification and HEC's final review, an approved applicant receives an issuance notification by email/SMS and can **download the e-Attestation Certificate from the HEC account**. Retain the original digital file; do not modify an issued certificate or fabricate its details. [HEC certificate issuance](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx).

## HEC attestation fee in 2026

**The current official HEC fee is PKR 3,000 for each document requested for attestation.** This is a government charge, not a translation fee or an LISAN.PK service fee.

| Documents requested | HEC fee at current published rate |
|---|---:|
| One degree | PKR 3,000 |
| One transcript | PKR 3,000 |
| Degree + transcript | PKR 6,000 |
| Degree + transcript + eligible provisional certificate | PKR 9,000 |
| One HEC equivalence letter | PKR 3,000 |

*Examples assume one chargeable document per selected category. Your portal's generated total is controlling. Extra services, translations, MOFA/Apostille or destination-country processes are **not** included.*

**Source:** [HEC's current attestation instructions and fee schedule](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx), reviewed 5 November 2026.

## Documents required for HEC online attestation

Prepare this checklist before opening the application:

| Item | When needed | Practical check |
|---|---|---|
| CNIC scans — both sides | Standard applicant identification | Name details and scans must be readable |
| University degree | If requesting degree attestation | Include entire document and identifying details |
| University transcript / marks record | If requesting transcript attestation | Include the correct academic record |
| Provisional certificate | Only if requesting that category | Must meet HEC's issuer requirements |
| HEC equivalence letter | If attesting an HEC-issued equivalence | Do not mistake it for direct attestation of a foreign degree |
| Professional council registration | Where required, e.g., medical or nursing | Follow HEC's case-specific instruction |
| Additional clarification/record | Only if HEC requests it | Use the portal's task/revision workflow |

**Foreign degrees:** HEC explicitly states that it does **not** attest degrees awarded by foreign universities as ordinary Pakistani university degrees. It may issue an equivalence letter for eligible foreign qualifications, and its own equivalence letter can be attested through the current system. [Source](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/das/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx).

## How long does HEC degree attestation take?

**There is no safe, universally guaranteed completion time.** The online application is available around the clock, but actual completion depends on HEC scrutiny, the issuing institution's response and whether revisions are required. An applicant may be delayed by incorrect campus selection, name mismatches or unclear scans.

Track your case inside **HEC e-Services** instead of trusting a third-party claim of “guaranteed same-day HEC attestation.” If an employer or university has a deadline, submit early and confirm whether it accepts a pending status or requires the issued certificate.

## How to verify an HEC e-Attestation Certificate

**Check authenticity through HEC, not a random verification website.** The new certificate is issued through the applicant's official HEC account after institutional verification. A receiving organisation should follow the official verification method or direct contact route that HEC makes available for that certificate and institution.

1. Request the **unmodified HEC-issued certificate file**, not a cropped screenshot.
2. Compare the degree holder's name, degree title and other details with the underlying academic record.
3. Use only an official HEC-linked verification method shown on the certificate or advised by HEC; do not assume any QR code or third-party website is a valid universal verifier.
4. If the receiving authority cannot validate the record, contact HEC support through [onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk](https://onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk) or the official HEC website.

**Do not upload another person's degree/CNIC to an unknown verification tool.** HEC has published a specific warning about impersonation and phishing. [HEC official safety alert](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Scam.aspx).

## Are older HEC stamps and previously attested degrees still valid?

Do **not** assume an older, genuine HEC attestation became invalid merely because the new paperless system launched. Equally, do not assume that every foreign recipient will accept an old stamp indefinitely. Acceptance depends on the document, the receiving authority, and any request for an updated verification format.

The current HEC overview describes the new method but **does not establish a blanket cancellation of all older genuine attestations**. If you already have an HEC-attested degree, ask your destination institution whether it needs a new e-Attestation Certificate before paying again.

## HEC attestation for Saudi Arabia: what comes next?

**For a Pakistani university degree, HEC is the Pakistani higher-education authentication step—not automatically the end of the Saudi document process.** Check the exact university, employer or visa authority's written requirement before spending money on an apostille or Arabic translation.

A practical sequence may look like this:

1. Obtain the relevant **HEC e-Attestation Certificate** for your Pakistani degree/transcript.
2. Ask the Saudi receiving body whether it needs **Pakistan MOFA Apostille** or a different authentication procedure for that document and use case.
3. If Arabic is specifically required, obtain an accurate **English-to-Arabic translation** covering the requested academic content and any relevant certificates or annotations.
4. Ask whether the receiving authority expects certification or authentication of the **translation itself**, rather than only the original academic record.

Saudi Arabia is listed by Pakistan's MOFA as accepting apostilles for eligible documents, but receiving authorities may impose document-specific conditions. See our [Saudi Embassy Attestation vs Apostille guide](/blog/saudi-embassy-attested-document-translation-process-pakistan) and [Apostille Pakistan guide](/guides/apostille-pakistan).

**Do all Saudi scholarship applications require HEC attestation and Arabic translation?** No universal rule should be inferred. Requirements can differ between the initial application, admission, visa and enrollment stages. Use the destination university's published checklist as the primary decision source.

## HEC attestation for the UAE: what comes next?

**HEC attestation and UAE recognition are separate steps.** The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs' [official Islamabad guidance](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Missions/Islamabad/Services/Services-for-Individuals/Attestation-Information) describes a UAE digital attestation service for Pakistani-issued documents. It requires prior authentication by the **Pakistan Ministry of Foreign Affairs** for Pakistan-issued documents, subject to specified exceptions, and may involve collection and return of the physical originals even though the resulting UAE attestation is digital.

Before applying:

- Determine whether your UAE employer, university, licensing body or immigration authority needs the **degree, transcript, translation or more than one**.
- Obtain HEC authentication where applicable for the Pakistani academic record.
- Follow the current **Pakistan MOFA** authentication route requested for the document.
- Review the UAE mission/MOFA's own portal and rules for the remaining process.
- Do not assume that a Saudi document checklist also works for the UAE.

**Important distinction:** “HEC is paperless” does **not** mean that every later process in another country is paperless or waives original-document handling.

## When should you translate your degree into Arabic—before or after HEC?

The correct order depends on what must appear on the translated copy and what the recipient accepts.

| Situation | Recommended decision |
|---|---|
| The Saudi institution accepts the existing English degree | Do **not** buy an Arabic translation unless another step requires it |
| The destination requires Arabic translation of the degree itself | Confirm the requested format and arrange English → Arabic translation |
| The receiving body needs the HEC certificate translated too | First obtain the **final HEC e-Attestation Certificate**, then provide it for translation |
| A foreign authority requires apostille/authentication of the translation | Ask that authority which document and translation-certification route it accepts |
| You have not confirmed the receiving authority's rule | Request written clarification before paying for extra stamps or translation |

A translation is **not** itself HEC attestation, MOFA authentication or an apostille. These are different services with different legal functions.

## Common HEC portal errors and what to do

### “Saved” or “pending payment” after paying

Return to the account and check **Verify Payment**. HEC says fee verification is necessary for application submission. Keep a payment reference and use HEC support if the portal still does not update.

### “Revision Requested” or “Task Assigned to Me”

Read the specific deficiency and resubmit the corrected information or requested supporting file. A revision is not necessarily a final rejection.

### Wrong university campus or degree title

Recheck the university and program entry carefully. Similar program names and affiliated colleges can appear in the system.

### Mismatch between degree and CNIC

Do not conceal the difference or ask a translator to change the source record. Follow the issuing university's and HEC's case-specific correction/verification instructions.

### Degree issued abroad

Check HEC's foreign qualification **equivalence** process instead of selecting an ordinary Pakistani-degree attestation procedure.

### Portal asking for an unrecognised payment or OTP

Stop and verify the domain. The genuine site is **eservices.hec.gov.pk**. HEC does not ask applicants to send OTPs to an alleged attestation agent. [Official alert](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Scam.aspx).

## HEC attestation FAQs (2026)

### 1. Can I apply for HEC degree attestation online from Lahore, Karachi or abroad?

Yes. HEC's new DAS permits online applications through its official portal. Availability of the online application does not remove separate destination-country requirements.

### 2. Do I still have to send original documents to HEC?

Not for the new standard paperless DAS attestation process described on HEC's current official page.

### 3. Is HEC attestation Rs 3,000 in 2026?

HEC currently lists **PKR 3,000 per document**. Verify the calculated charge in the official portal before paying.

### 4. If I attest my degree and transcript, is that one fee?

No. HEC lists the degree and transcript as separate document selections; at its current rate two such documents total **PKR 6,000**.

### 5. How do I confirm my online application was submitted?

Verify the payment in your HEC dashboard, then check the application status. A saved or pending-payment record may not be submitted.

### 6. What if my university has not verified my degree yet?

Monitor the status and follow HEC's instructions; issuance depends on confirmation from the awarding university/HEI and HEC final review.

### 7. Can I attest a transcript before my final degree is issued?

HEC says qualifying transcripts may be attested even before the degree is issued, subject to its requirements for incomplete records.

### 8. Does HEC attest my matric or FSc certificate?

HEC's degree-attestation process concerns higher-education records. For Pakistan's Board-issued matric/intermediate documents, check [IBCC](https://ibcc.edu.pk) and the relevant issuing Board.

### 9. Can HEC attest a foreign university's degree?

HEC does not ordinarily attest foreign-issued degrees. It provides an equivalence process for qualifying foreign degrees and can attest its own equivalence letters under the current system.

### 10. Does HEC e-Attestation mean my degree is accepted in Saudi Arabia?

No. HEC verifies a Pakistani higher-education credential; the Saudi university, employer or authority decides the documentation it will accept.

### 11. Does the UAE require the original degree even though HEC is digital?

The **UAE attestation process** has separate rules. Its Islamabad guidance describes courier collection of physical original documents for the UAE digital attestation service.

### 12. Must my English degree be translated into Arabic?

Only if the receiving institution or authority requests it. Confirm whether it wants the degree, transcript, e-Attestation Certificate or all relevant records translated.

### 13. Are old HEC stamps automatically invalid after May 2026?

We found no blanket cancellation statement in HEC's current new-process instructions. Ask the receiving authority whether the existing genuine attestation suffices.

### 14. How quickly can HEC issue my e-Attestation Certificate?

There is no universal guarantee. HEC's scrutiny, your university's verification and any revision requests determine timing.

### 15. Can LISAN.PK get me an HEC approval faster?

No independent translation company can promise HEC approval or priority. Apply and pay through HEC directly; LISAN.PK can assist with **translation and understanding published document requirements** without claiming to be HEC or an authorised attestation agent.

### 16. Where should I report an HEC portal or verification problem?

Use [HEC's official online help system](https://onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk) and contacts published by HEC, rather than an unfamiliar private payment or verification website.
