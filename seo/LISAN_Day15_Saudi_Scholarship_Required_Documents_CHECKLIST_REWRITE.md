# Saudi Scholarship Required Documents 2026–27: Complete Checklist for Pakistani Students

**Pakistani students applying for Saudi scholarships should first prepare a valid passport and academic records appropriate to their degree level, then add language results, recommendation letters, a statement of purpose, or other files only when their chosen university or program asks for them.** A medical report and authenticated educational certificates are described in the official Study in Saudi guidance, but the **submission stage**, **translation language**, and **attestation route** depend on the receiving institution. **Do not translate or apostille every document automatically.** [1][2]

**Status update (sources checked 5 November 2026):** The Study in Saudi window announced by King Abdulaziz University for **16 September–6 October 2026** has closed. That window is not evidence of a confirmed opening date for another intake. Use this checklist to prepare for a future opening or to correct an existing application; check the university's official notice before submitting anything. [3]

> **Quick answer:** Matric/FSc records → issuing Board verification and **IBCC** if attestation is required; Pakistani university degrees/transcripts → **HEC** if attestation is required; **Pakistan MOFA Apostille** only when the receiving authority requests appropriate international authentication. Arabic translations are **not universally compulsory** for every file uploaded to Study in Saudi. [1][4][5][6]

## Which documents do Bachelor's, Master's and PhD students need?

### Bachelor's (FSc / ICS / FA / A Levels)

Start with your passport, secondary education certificate and complete school/intermediate marks. Many programs evaluate secondary education performance and age. Students holding non-Pakistani curricula such as A Levels should check whether a local equivalence certificate is requested. **Do not assume IBCC attestation and IBCC equivalence are identical:** equivalence assesses qualification comparability; attestation authenticates eligible records. [4]

A Saudi university may separately request language proficiency, a health report or civil documents. Upload the precise documents asked for. Do not add irrelevant papers in place of a missing required field.

### Master's (after BS / Bachelor's)

Prepare the undergraduate degree and official transcript. Some institutions also want earlier educational history, graduation evidence, a CV, motivation letter or referee recommendations. Language-test requirements vary: a program that accepts an institutional exemption does not establish that *every* Saudi master's program accepts a Pakistani MOI letter.

A separate [Saudi scholarship eligibility guide](/blog/saudi-scholarship-eligibility-pakistani-students) explains how to check age, qualifications and language requirements for a specific university.

### PhD (doctoral research)

Prepare the degree(s) required by the doctoral track, full transcripts, research interests, CV and any requested research proposal, publication list or academic references. Whether an MS/MPhil is necessary depends on the doctorate's entry route. **Research papers and a research proposal are not universal upload requirements**: follow the specific department's checklist, and never represent a paid assistance package as an official requirement.

## HEC vs IBCC vs MOFA: which Pakistani office handles which document?

These organisations perform **different functions**. Using the wrong one wastes time and can leave a document without the required authentication.

| Your document | Main Pakistani authority to check | Typical sequence **only if attestation is required** | What not to assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matric / SSC certificate or result | Issuing education board; **IBCC** | Board verification → IBCC attestation | HEC does not replace IBCC for SSC |
| Intermediate / HSSC / FSc certificate or result | Issuing education board; **IBCC** | Board verification → IBCC attestation | HEC does not attest ordinary HSSC certificates |
| Pakistani bachelor's degree / university transcript | **HEC** | HEC online e-attestation | IBCC is not the university-degree attestation body |
| Pakistani master's / MPhil / PhD degree / transcript | **HEC** | HEC online e-attestation | Having an HEC certificate does not automatically settle destination requirements |
| Foreign education qualification / equivalence | Competent equivalence or issuing authority | Case-specific | Equivalence and attestation are not the same process |
| Birth, marriage, police or other public records | Competent issuing authority, then as applicable Pakistan **MOFA** | Check document authenticity and whether MOFA Apostille/general attestation is requested | These are not all HEC or IBCC documents |
| Any document for international use | **MOFA**, when required | Confirm destination/document category before paying | MOFA is not the first verifier for every academic record |

**Official basis:** IBCC identifies secondary and higher-secondary board records as its responsibility and requires issuing-board verification before IBCC attestation. HEC's updated digital service covers university degrees and transcripts. [4][5]

### HEC degree attestation: new online process

HEC's current service describes a **fully online, paperless** system. Students submit through the [HEC e-Services portal](https://eservices.hec.gov.pk/), upload the necessary academic evidence, pay the displayed fee and address any revision requests through their account. The official HEC service currently lists **PKR 3,000 per document** (including eligible degree or transcript records); fees can change, so check the portal before payment. [5]

Do not follow an old guide directing everyone to send their original degree by courier or visit an HEC counter as a universal current procedure. For detailed instructions see our [HEC online degree attestation guide](/guides/hec-online-degree-attestation).

### IBCC for Matric and Intermediate

IBCC instructs applicants to obtain verification from the original board, use its online attestation application, and follow the selected submission method. **IBCC is not identical to HEC:** a BS transcript and an FSc marksheet have different competent authorities. Always check whether your Saudi institution needs attested originals, copies, electronic verification or merely a readable upload at initial application. [4][7]

### MOFA Apostille: only when the next authority requires it

Pakistan's MOFA **lists Saudi Arabia among destinations accepting Pakistani Apostilles**, subject to document category and receiving-authority requirements. An Apostille authenticates the *origin of an eligible public document*; it does not independently validate your grades, award you a scholarship or translate the document. [6][8]

**Decision rule:**

1. Check the university or scholarship letter's exact wording.
2. Identify whether the document is educational, civil or another category.
3. Complete any necessary primary verification (Board/IBCC, HEC or issuing authority).
4. If instructed to authenticate it internationally, check Pakistan MOFA's current Saudi-country guidance and supported Apostille process.
5. Check whether the receiving university additionally specifies a different document-specific authentication step.
6. Obtain a translation **only in the requested language and format**; check whether the translation itself needs separate certification.

MOFA states that Apostille services require **prior online registration**, a requirement effective **27 July 2026**. Its published processes also include appointment/document review steps: online registration should **not** be confused with an unconditional promise that every case needs no physical submission. [9][10]

## Do Saudi scholarship documents need Arabic translation?

**Not automatically.** The correct answer depends on **the language of the original document**, **the university's permitted languages**, and **the exact stage**. If a program accepts English-language certificates, an original English degree or transcript may not require Arabic translation. If the receiving institution demands Arabic, or the document is Urdu-only and no English copy is accepted, arrange the specified translation.

| Original document | Recommended first check | Possible next step |
|---|---|---|
| English HEC-attested degree or transcript | Does your target Saudi university accept English? | If yes, no automatic Arabic translation; if no, obtain the requested translation |
| Urdu-only Matric/FSc record | Are original plus English or Arabic translations allowed? | Obtain the required certified translation, including all pages |
| Bilingual Pakistani academic certificate | Does the receiving institution accept its English information? | Translate only if requested or insufficiently legible |
| Urdu Nikah Nama / civil document | Is it required at this application stage, and in which language? | Translation appropriate to the requesting authority |
| Medical or police certificate in English | What form, age, and language does the authority require? | Translate or authenticate only if specified |
| Recommendation letter or SOP | What writing language does the department request? | Author the document in that language; do not mechanically translate otherwise acceptable material |

**Avoid a common mistake:** Some older LISAN.PK articles stated that every educational or civil document must be translated into Arabic to satisfy portal screening. That is **too broad**. Official Study in Saudi guidance delegates document authentication and admission rules to the relevant educational institution; there is no single general rule that every English document must have an Arabic copy. [1][2]

Translation also **does not equal** notarisation, HEC/IBCC verification, or Apostille. See our [certified translation versus Apostille guide](/guides/certified-translation-vs-apostille-pakistan).

## Recommendation letter for a Saudi scholarship: who should write it?

For postgraduate admissions, a strong recommendation normally comes from an academic instructor, research supervisor or other referee who genuinely knows your work. If a university asks for two or three letters, or requires referees to submit links directly, follow that instruction. **There is no universal number of recommendation letters across Saudi scholarships.**

Before asking a referee, confirm the program's format and deadline. Provide the correct program name, your academic CV, relevant achievements and sufficient notice. Never create fictitious signatures, institutional letterheads or referee email accounts. A recommendation cannot be replaced by a paid translation package or generic template.

## Saudi scholarship medical certificate: when is it required?

The Study in Saudi FAQ includes a **medical report confirming freedom from infectious diseases** among general study requirements. The official long-term education visa information also mentions medical reporting. It does **not** follow that every program needs the same hospital certificate uploaded with its initial academic application. [1][11]

Check whether your university requires a report **before evaluation**, **after conditional admission**, or **during the visa stage**. Ask which form, tests, issuing facility and validity window are accepted. Avoid paying for tests too early: documents with time-sensitive validity may expire before you need them.

The same logic applies to a police character certificate. Some institutions or visa routes request it, but it should not be presented as universally mandatory at the very first Study in Saudi login.

## Passport validity and name matching

The Study in Saudi visa guidance refers to a passport with **at least six months' validity from intended entry**. That is a published visa-stage condition, **not a promise** that six months is enough for every university's admission procedures or later travel requirements. [1][11]

Use the same spelling of your full name, father's name where relevant, birth date and passport number throughout your account and supporting documents. If the source document uses an inconsistent transliteration, address the discrepancy transparently with the institution instead of altering the original certificate. After application submission, the portal FAQ describes restrictions on changing the passport number. [1]

## File format, scanning and uploading: avoid these preventable errors

There is **no safe universal rule** that all Saudi scholarship portals accept the same maximum file size. Use the permitted type and upload size shown inside the **live portal field**. A current admissions page may request JPG, PNG, PDF, a single combined file or one file per record.

For each upload:

- Capture every printed page and official stamp, including the reverse where it contains information.
- Keep names, marks, QR codes and issuing-authority signatures readable.
- Do not crop out borders, signature areas or attestation marks.
- Preserve the source document and its translation as separate files unless the university requests a combined PDF.
- Use neutral filenames such as `Passport_Applicant.pdf` or `BS_Transcript.pdf` without exposing sensitive identifiers unnecessarily.
- Verify that the correct file opens from the portal preview after upload.
- Never edit, invent or remove qualification data to make a document appear eligible.

## Common Saudi scholarship document mistakes

1. **Getting HEC for FSc or IBCC for a BS degree:** match the record to the correct authority.
2. **Paying for Apostille immediately:** check whether your institution requests it at this stage.
3. **Translating every English record into Arabic:** follow the destination's exact language rules.
4. **Assuming a police certificate is always an application-stage document:** distinguish admission from visa processing.
5. **Using an expired passport or mismatched names:** confirm information before final submission.
6. **Writing one generic SOP for every university:** answer each department's prompt and word count.
7. **Buying a recommendation letter:** use a genuine referee following the institution's method.
8. **Uploading incomplete transcripts:** include all semesters and explanatory grading pages if requested.
9. **Using old fixed file-size or test-validity advice:** review the current portal controls.
10. **Relying on consultancy guarantees:** universities and immigration authorities—not agents—decide acceptance.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. What are the required documents for a Saudi scholarship from Pakistan?

A passport and the relevant academic certificates/transcripts are the core preparation documents. Medical reports, language scores, references, statements and authenticated copies may be required according to university, program and application stage. [1][2]

### 2. Do I need both Matric and FSc certificates for Study in Saudi?

Many bachelor's programs evaluate higher-secondary qualifications and may request earlier records. Upload both only as specified by the selected university and its portal fields.

### 3. Are HEC and IBCC attestation both compulsory?

Not for the same document. IBCC handles eligible secondary/higher-secondary board records; HEC handles eligible higher-education credentials. Whether they must already be attested **at initial upload** depends on the university. [4][5]

### 4. Can I get HEC attestation online in 2026?

Yes. HEC's current system is described as fully online and paperless, with the listed PKR 3,000 fee per eligible document. Verify the current service instructions before payment. [5]

### 5. Does Saudi Arabia accept Pakistani Apostilles?

Pakistan MOFA currently lists Saudi Arabia as accepting Apostilles, subject to document category and receiving authority. That does **not** mean every scholarship file must be apostilled. [6]

### 6. Must all Saudi scholarship documents be translated into Arabic?

No universal rule supports that claim. Check the particular program's accepted document languages and its requirements for translations. English originals may already be acceptable. [1][2]

### 7. Do I need a police character certificate to submit an application?

Only when the relevant institution or stage asks for it. A later visa request does not make it universally mandatory for first-stage scholarship uploads.

### 8. Is a medical certificate compulsory for Saudi scholarships?

Official Study in Saudi guidance includes a medical report among general study requirements. The **timing, format and test details** can depend on the university and visa route. [1][11]

### 9. How many recommendation letters do Saudi universities need?

There is no universal number. Follow the department's exact requirement and use genuine academic or professional referees.

### 10. Is IELTS compulsory for every Saudi scholarship?

No single IELTS rule applies to every degree or university. Many programs have program-specific language requirements or permitted alternatives; do not assume an MOI letter is accepted unless the institution explicitly says so.

### 11. Should I translate my Urdu documents into English or Arabic?

Use the language explicitly required by the university or receiving authority. Do not order both translations unless there is a documented need.

### 12. Is HEC attestation the same as MOFA Apostille?

No. HEC authenticates eligible higher-education records; MOFA Apostille is a separate international-origin authentication step for eligible public documents. [5][8]

### 13. Can I upload a provisional degree instead of the final degree?

Only if the particular program accepts provisional or expected-graduation evidence. Do not assume provisional evidence is acceptable for final enrolment without checking.

### 14. Is a scholarship application fee or translation package mandatory?

Do not assume any private consultant or translation package is compulsory. Confirm official application costs and required paid services independently with the university. LISAN.PK offers optional translation and document-support services.

### 15. Can I change my passport after applying?

The Study in Saudi FAQ states that the passport number cannot be modified once an application has been submitted using it. Consult official portal support if you receive a new passport. [1]

### 16. Is the 2026 Saudi scholarship application window still open?

The 16 September–6 October 2026 intake announced by KAU is closed. Check the official Study in Saudi portal and the university's own admissions page for later intakes; do not rely on speculative reopening dates. [3]

### 17. What if my university requests notarised Arabic translations?

Follow the university's written instruction, and verify whether notarisation or another form of recognition must be performed in Saudi Arabia rather than Pakistan. A translator's certification alone is not the same as notarisation or Apostille.

### 18. What should I do if a document is returned for correction?

Open the returned document request, identify exactly what is wrong, and change only the fields the portal allows. The official FAQ describes restricted identity fields and permits some qualification corrections after a return. [1]
