# Apostille in Pakistan (2026): MOFA Registration, Required Documents, Fees, Couriers and Verification

**Quick answer:** To obtain an apostille in Pakistan, first check whether the destination country can accept a **Pakistani apostille**, then register online with Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), submit the required documents for review and follow the approved appointment or submission instructions. **Online registration has been mandatory since 27 July 2026.** An apostille authenticates the *origin* of an eligible public document; it does **not** translate the document or guarantee that the receiving organisation will accept it. [Sources: MOFA consular notice](https://mofa.gov.pk/consular-service-attestation-of-documents) · [Hague Conference (HCCH) — Apostille Convention](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=41).

**Last fact-checked against government and HCCH sources:** 3 November 2026. Government portal steps, fees and appointment availability may change. **LISAN.PK is a private document-translation service, not MOFA or an apostille issuing authority.**

## What is an apostille, and when do you need one in Pakistan?

An **apostille** is a certificate issued by an authorised public authority to authenticate the origin of an eligible public document for use abroad under the **Hague Apostille Convention of 5 October 1961**. It verifies matters such as an official's signature, the capacity in which that person acted and, where applicable, the document's official seal or stamp. It does **not** prove that every statement inside the document is true. [HCCH Convention text](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=41).

The Convention entered into force for **Pakistan on 9 March 2023**. Pakistan's competent authorities include designated officials at MOFA headquarters in Islamabad and Foreign Affairs Liaison Offices in Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta and Gujrat. [HCCH accession notice](https://www.hcch.net/en/news-archive/details?varevent=903) · [HCCH list of Pakistani competent authorities](https://www.hcch.net/en/states/authorities/details3/?aid=1179).

You might need an apostille when a foreign university, immigration office, employer, marriage registrar or other institution asks for authentication of a Pakistani public document. However, **the receiving country and the particular authority both matter**. Do not buy an apostille merely because a country appears on a generic list: the Convention may not operate between Pakistan and that country because of a formal objection, or the receiving institution may require a different process. Check the [HCCH status and notifications](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) and [MOFA destination directory](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/countries), then verify the receiving authority's instructions.

## How to apply for an apostille in Pakistan online: step by step

MOFA's July 2026 notice says **applications submitted without prior online registration will not be accepted** for apostille processing. The government's current application guidance also sets out document review, appointment booking and payment before submission. The exact completion method may depend on the digital service available to your application. [MOFA consular notice](https://mofa.gov.pk/consular-service-attestation-of-documents) · [MOFA application procedure](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/how-it-works).

| Step | What the applicant does | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Check your destination** | Confirm that an apostille is the appropriate route for your particular document and destination. | Check any objection affecting the Convention's application to Pakistan; also check the receiving office's own instructions. |
| **2. Create your MOFA account** | Visit the [MOFA digital attestation system](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/) and follow its applicant-registration link. MOFA's apostille-specific notice also references [apostille.mofa.gov.pk](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/). | Use official **`.mofa.gov.pk`** pages and follow the currently displayed route rather than making duplicate submissions. |
| **3. Verify your account and identity** | Complete your profile, email verification and requested identification details. | MOFA's apostille notice specifically asks applicants to attach **both sides of their CNIC**. |
| **4. Create the application** | Choose **Apostille**, select the document category and upload the required documents. | Upload legible, complete documents; include prior HEC/IBCC/issuing-authority authentication where required. |
| **5. Submit for review** | Review names, document categories, attachments and destination details, then submit. | Submission does **not** mean MOFA has approved the document. |
| **6. Await MOFA/FALO approval** | Check your email and portal for a decision or correction request. | The current public workflow says appointment booking comes **after officer approval**. |
| **7. Book appointment and pay** | Where the portal instructs, choose the available FALO appointment; obtain your payment challan and pay through the approved mechanism. | MOFA's published workflow says the challan appears **after appointment booking**. |
| **8. Complete the required handover** | Follow the instructions in your application for the booked visit, courier route, or digital completion if offered. | Never assume that a courier can replace a required personal appearance for a power of attorney or affidavit. |
| **9. Receive and verify** | Obtain the apostilled document or e-Certificate, then check it using MOFA's official verifier. | Keep the certificate number and date; compare the verification result with your document. |

**Important 2026 update:** On **12 October 2026**, MOFA announced a Digital Attestation System that provides e-Certificates by email and aims to reduce physical visits. At the same time, its published [application procedure](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/how-it-works) still describes an approval → appointment → payment → office-submission sequence. **Follow the actual instructions for your case rather than assuming every apostille can be completed entirely from home.** [MOFA digital attestation notice](https://mofa.gov.pk/consular-service-attestation-of-documents).

### Is apostille.mofa.gov.pk registration mandatory?

**Yes, MOFA announced mandatory advance online registration for apostille requests from 27 July 2026.** Its notice names `apostille.mofa.gov.pk`, while MOFA also provides a newer digital attestation interface at `attestation.mofa.gov.pk` with an applicant portal. Because official pages can display different generations of the process, start from the current [MOFA service home page](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/) or [consular notice](https://mofa.gov.pk/consular-service-attestation-of-documents) and follow the live instructions. Do not create duplicate applications just to use both interfaces.

### Can I apply for an apostille without visiting MOFA?

**Do not assume so.** The October digital notice describes remote electronic processing and e-Certificates for eligible cases, but MOFA's public procedure still directs applicants to attend the booked office in its standard flow. Physical appearance may also be specifically required for some legal instruments. If your approved application includes an appointment, comply with it or obtain written guidance from MOFA before using a courier. [MOFA consular notice](https://mofa.gov.pk/consular-service-attestation-of-documents) · [Application procedure](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/how-it-works).

## Documents required for an apostille in Pakistan

There is **no single universal checklist** for every document. The Ministry first needs an eligible authentic record, and some records require verification by the original issuing authority before MOFA can process them. Always check the [official MOFA apostille checklist](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/apostille-checklist) and [detailed attestation SOPs](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/sops).

| Document type | Common prerequisite or supporting record | Important caution |
|---|---|---|
| **University degree or transcript** | Applicable HEC verification or e-attestation, where required for the specific educational document. | Confirm the latest HEC procedure before presenting the record to MOFA. |
| **Matric / intermediate certificate** | Applicable IBCC authentication of board-issued records. | School, board and university records follow different routes. |
| **Nikah Nama / Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC)** | Original signed/stamped Nikah Nama and MRC, plus relevant CNIC/FRC or other evidence required by MOFA's SOP. | A marriage with a foreign national may require the foreign spouse's passport, Pakistani visa and entry details. |
| **Birth / death certificate and other civil records** | Authentic civil record issued by the relevant authority, with the requested identity evidence. | The correct issuer and full readable record are essential. |
| **Police clearance / character certificate** | Valid certificate from the proper police authority and any prescribed authentication. | The receiving organisation may impose its own recency requirements. |
| **Power of attorney / affidavit** | Appropriate original legal instrument, identity evidence, notarial or other authentication, and any required attendance. | MOFA's public instructions restrict routine courier processing for some of these documents. |
| **Commercial document** | Relevant company registration, tax and/or Chamber of Commerce verification, depending on the document. | Commercial documents have a different fee category and prerequisites. |

**Before uploading:** Check spellings against passports/CNICs; ensure seals, signatures and both sides of the record are readable; capture every page; and confirm that an apostille is appropriate for the **actual recipient**, not just the country.

### Apostille for Nikah Nama and NADRA Marriage Registration Certificate

MOFA's published SOP asks, for a normal Pakistani marriage, for the **original Nikah Nama signed and stamped by the Nikah Registrar**, the **Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC)** and specified identity or family-registration evidence. If a foreign national is involved, the SOP lists additional immigration and passport evidence. Because these requirements are document- and case-specific, read the relevant [MOFA marriage-document SOP](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/sops) before submitting. [Detailed guide to Nikah Nama and marriage certificate translations](/services/translation/nikah-nama-marriage-certificate).

**Do not assume the Nikah Nama and MRC are interchangeable.** The Urdu marriage contract and the computerised marriage registration record can contain different levels of detail. Ask the overseas receiver which document, original and translated versions it wants.

### Apostille for degree and transcript documents

For a university degree, the usual preparation question is whether the record needs HEC authentication first. For Matric or Intermediate documents, check the applicable IBCC procedure instead. The apostille is a separate **MOFA step**; it is not a replacement for every preceding educational verification. See the [MOFA educational-document categories](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/list-of-public-documents) and relevant official HEC/IBCC guidance. A dedicated LISAN.PK HEC guide is scheduled separately; do not rely on an unpublished page.

## How to verify a Pakistani apostille by QR code or certificate number

**A Pakistani apostille can be checked using MOFA's official verification service.** Certificates may carry a QR code, and the MOFA verification form also accepts the **apostille number and issuance date**. An apostille that is genuine does not automatically validate the content of the underlying document or replace a requested translation. [MOFA apostille verifier](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/verify-attestation) · [HCCH — Pakistan competent authority](https://www.hcch.net/en/states/authorities/details3/?aid=1179).

1. Examine the certificate for the QR code, apostille number and date.
2. Scan the QR code, checking that it leads to an authentic government domain rather than an unfamiliar commercial page.
3. If needed, open the [official apostille verification form](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/verify-attestation) manually and enter the certificate number and date.
4. Compare the returned status and identifying details with the certificate in your possession.
5. If verification fails or details do not match, contact MOFA through the contact channel on its official verifier. **Do not submit an altered certificate.**

The newer [MOFA digital verification interface](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/verify-apostille) is another official entry point. For sensitive transactions, verify through the site directly instead of trusting a screenshot forwarded by a third party.

## Apostille vs MOFA attestation vs notarisation vs certified translation

These are **different legal and administrative functions**. Buying one does not automatically satisfy a request for the others.

| Process | What it establishes or provides | What it does **not** do |
|---|---|---|
| **Certified translation** | A translation accompanied by an accuracy statement or certification according to the receiving body's requirements. | It does not authenticate the original issuing officer's signature. |
| **Notarisation** | A notarial act, such as witnessing a signature or certifying a document copy, within the notary's lawful authority. | It does not make the translated text automatically acceptable to every foreign institution. |
| **MOFA General Attestation** | Authentication under the ordinary government attestation/legalisation pathway. | It is not necessarily interchangeable with an apostille or a foreign embassy's further requirements. |
| **MOFA Apostille** | Authentication of the origin of an eligible public document under the Convention, when it applies between relevant states. | It does not certify the document's contents or translate it. |
| **Embassy legalisation** | A separate diplomatic/consular formality where applicable to the destination and document. | It is not automatically required after a valid apostille where the Convention applies. |

See our [MOFA online attestation guide](/guides/mofa-online-attestation) and [document translation and attestation service](/services/attestation). For the Convention's scope, see the [HCCH full text](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=41).

## Translate before or after an apostille? The correct order for Pakistani documents

**There is no universal "translate first" or "apostille first" rule.** The answer depends on **what the destination authority wants apostilled**: the original public document, an officially certified/notarised translation, or both. Some receivers simply want an apostilled original plus a separately certified translation; others specify a translation with an additional official certification step. Ask the receiving institution **in writing** before paying twice.

### Translation and apostille order: decision table

| Receiving authority's stated requirement | Typical sequence to discuss with MOFA/receiver | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **"Apostilled original + certified translation"** | Check original's prerequisite authentication → **apostille the original** → translate the full document, **including apostille wording if requested** → submit both. | The apostille authenticates the original's official signature/seal; translation is a separate service. |
| **"Certified or notarised translation must itself be authenticated"** | Arrange acceptable translation → obtain the prescribed notarial/official certification, if legally possible → ask the competent authority **whether that specific notarial/official act can be apostilled** → submit as instructed. | A notarised certificate attached to a translation is a different object from the original document's apostille. **Do not assume MOFA apostilles every private translator declaration.** |
| **"Original and translation both apostilled"** | Obtain written recipient instructions identifying the two certificates and eligible public acts → verify with MOFA → complete only the eligible routes. | Separate apostilles, if possible, authenticate separate official acts; they do not prove the translation's linguistic accuracy. |
| **"Translation must show the apostille stamp / QR / endorsements"** | Authenticate original first → translate original plus all relevant endorsements and apostille text → attach both versions. | Translating too early may omit later endorsements. |
| **Non-Apostille destination or Convention objection** | Follow the receiving authority's prescribed **general attestation / legalisation** and translation procedure. | An apostille may be the wrong route even if the destination is a Convention member. |

**Key distinction:** Under the Hague Convention, an apostille verifies the *origin* of the public act to which it relates—not the factual or linguistic accuracy of the underlying content. When an apostille is placed on a **notarial act attached to a translation**, it authenticates that notarial act. Read the [HCCH Apostille Convention](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=41) and check the precise recipient requirement.

**A Pakistan-specific translation-format caution:** MOFA's published attestation SOP states that the ministry attests documents in **Urdu/English**, and that translations intended for certain other languages should include **English in one column on the same page alongside the target language**. That is an MOFA processing condition reported in its SOP; it is **not** proof that every destination requires bilingual columns or that all private translations are eligible for MOFA apostilling. Check the [current MOFA SOP](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/sops) and the receiving institution's separate requirements.

### Example: Pakistani marriage document for an overseas family application

Suppose an overseas authority asks for an **apostilled Pakistani marriage record plus English translation**. First confirm whether it specifically requires the Urdu Nikah Nama, the MRC, or both. Gather the records and MOFA supporting evidence; obtain the apostille for the specified eligible original. Then prepare a certified English translation covering the information and any endorsement the receiving authority needs. **Do not assume that the translation itself needs another apostille unless the receiving authority asks for one.** [Nikah Nama and MRC translation guide](/services/translation/nikah-nama-marriage-certificate).

### Example: Pakistani degree for study or employment abroad

If an institution requests **an apostilled degree and an English translation**, confirm whether HEC authentication is required first; complete the appropriate authentication and MOFA steps, and translate the complete record in the form the institution requests. If the institution specifically asks for a **notarised translated copy**, request the exact format rather than guessing. Never state that one country's degree procedure applies to all universities, employers or visa categories.

## Which countries accept apostilles from Pakistan?

A country's membership in the Apostille Convention **does not by itself prove that the Convention operates between that country and Pakistan**. Several states filed formal objections to Pakistan's accession. The HCCH notification record includes **Germany, the Netherlands, Denmark, Finland, Austria, Poland, the Czech Republic and Greece**. An objection can affect whether a Pakistani apostille replaces diplomatic legalisation in that bilateral relationship. [HCCH objections and notifications](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/notifications/?csid=1466&disp=type).

For **your destination today**, check three sources in this order: the [HCCH Convention status/objections](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41), the [MOFA country directory](https://attestation.mofa.gov.pk/countries), and the receiving embassy, university, employer or immigration authority. A simple A–Z website list that ignores formal objections can be misleading. LISAN.PK's detailed **country-by-country apostille acceptance guide** is scheduled separately; this article deliberately does not claim every Hague country accepts Pakistani apostilles.

## Common apostille mistakes that cause avoidable delays

- **Using general attestation when an apostille was required**, or requesting an apostille where the Convention does not apply between Pakistan and the destination.
- **Skipping MOFA online registration**, which has been mandatory for apostille applications since 27 July 2026.
- **Assuming every online application is fully remote**, despite case-specific appointment and original-document requirements.
- **Uploading a degree without required prior HEC authentication** or a school certificate without applicable IBCC authentication.
- **Sending only an MRC when the recipient wants the full Urdu Nikah Nama**, or omitting MOFA's extra supporting evidence.
- **Translating an earlier version of the document and omitting later stamps**, where a complete translation is required.
- **Paying a private company without checking the official challan**, mistaking a service charge for a government fee.
- **Using an unverified QR-code screenshot instead of checking the MOFA certificate number and date.**

## Apostille Pakistan: frequently asked questions

### 1. How do I get an apostille in Pakistan in 2026?

Check the destination and eligible document, register through MOFA's official online service, upload the required records, await officer review and follow the appointment, payment and document-delivery instructions for your approved application. Verify the certificate after issuance.

### 2. Is apostille online registration compulsory in Pakistan?

Yes. MOFA announced mandatory advance online registration from **27 July 2026**. Use an official MOFA portal linked from its current consular instructions.

### 3. Can I get an apostille without an appointment?

Do not assume you can. MOFA's current application procedure describes an appointment after approval, while its newer digital-attestation notice describes additional remote services. Follow the instructions issued for your application.

### 4. What is the apostille fee in Pakistan?

The current published MOFA schedule lists **PKR 3,000** for personal, educational and experience documents; **PKR 4,500** for legal documents; and **PKR 12,000** for commercial documents, each per document. Recheck the official fee page and final portal challan.

### 5. Can an apostille be obtained for a Nikah Nama?

MOFA lists Nikah Nama and marriage registration documents, subject to its published requirements. The required supporting documents depend on the marriage record and whether a foreign spouse is involved.

### 6. Does a Pakistani degree need HEC verification before MOFA apostille?

University credentials can require prior HEC authentication; school and board credentials may require IBCC authentication. Follow the correct document-specific MOFA checklist and the current HEC/IBCC procedure.

### 7. Which couriers are authorised for apostille services?

MOFA currently lists **TCS, Gerry's International, Excellent Courier Services, Leopards Courier Service and M&P Express Logistics**. Confirm that a particular branch is accepting your document type and that its route complies with MOFA's registration and attendance instructions.

### 8. Can I verify a Pakistani apostille online?

Yes. Use the [official MOFA apostille verifier](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/verify-attestation) with the apostille number and date, or check the QR code against the genuine government site.

### 9. Should I translate my document before or after apostille?

It depends on the recipient's instructions. When it requests an **apostilled original plus a certified translation**, apostilling first may help ensure the translation includes the endorsement. If it requests authentication of a **notarised translation**, confirm that exact process with MOFA and the recipient before proceeding.

### 10. Does MOFA apostille also certify the translation?

No. The apostille authenticates the origin of the particular eligible public document or official act. It does not independently certify the linguistic accuracy of a translation.

### 11. Does an apostille guarantee embassy or university acceptance?

No. Eligibility, document content, translation format, completeness and destination-specific rules remain separate questions.

### 12. Is an apostille the same as notarisation?

No. A notary performs a notarial act; an apostille issued by a competent authority authenticates the origin of an eligible public act for use abroad where the Convention applies.

### 13. Can a courier process a power of attorney or affidavit apostille?

Do not assume so. MOFA's consular instructions describe exceptions requiring personal attendance for certain powers of attorney and affidavits. Check the latest MOFA rules for your exact instrument.

### 14. Does Germany automatically accept a Pakistani apostille?

No such assumption is safe. Germany is among the countries whose objections to Pakistan's accession are recorded by the HCCH. Check the current legal position and the German authority's requirements before selecting a legalisation route.

### 15. Are the government apostille fee and certified-translation fee the same?

No. MOFA's fee is a government authentication charge. Certified translation is a separate service, quoted according to language, document length and the requested format.

### 16. Can LISAN.PK issue or verify an apostille itself?

No. **Only MOFA's competent officials issue Pakistani apostilles**, and official authentication should be checked through MOFA. LISAN.PK can discuss document-language and certified-translation requirements; it is not a government issuing authority.
