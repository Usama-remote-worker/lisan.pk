## Which property documents do we translate?

| Source document | English translation focus | Typical document issue to check |
|---|---|---|
| **Fard / Fard-e-Malkiat (فرد / فردِ ملکیت)** | Ownership-record extract and owner/parcel particulars | Multiple owners, fractional shares and dense tables |
| **Intiqal (انتقال)** | Mutation or land-record change entry | Transfer basis, mutation number and order/entry date |
| **Jamabandi / Register Haqdaran Zamin (جمع بندی)** | Record of rights / landholders' register | Ownership and cultivation accounts spanning many lines |
| **Registry / registered deed (رجسٹری)** | Full registered instrument or deed | Recitals, consideration, schedules and official markings |
| **Bay Nama (بیع نامہ)** | Sale deed | Property description, parties, clauses and obligations |
| **Inheritance-related land documents** | Translation of the specific estate record supplied | Distinguish revenue mutation from succession orders |
| **Property-related affidavits** | Sworn declaration as actually written | Preserve the speaker's wording and notarisation marks |
| **Revenue or property correspondence** | Official notices, decisions and extracts | Issuer identity, reference numbers and time periods |

We translate the **document you provide**; we do not reclassify a mutation as a sale deed or turn a land-record extract into a certificate of unencumbered title. For a plain-English explanation, see [Fard vs Intiqal vs Jamabandi](/blog/urdu-to-english-fard-translation-in-lahore).

## Urdu-to-English Fard translation: what is included?

A complete translated Fard should make it possible for an English-speaking reviewer to match the text to the original. Depending on the source, we assess:

- Land owner and co-owner names, parentage and address fields.
- Mauza, tehsil, district and revenue estate details.
- Khewat, khatooni, khasra and mutation identifiers.
- Ownership fractions, land area, kanal/marla and other source units.
- Printed field labels, handwritten remarks and record-specific abbreviations.
- Legible stamps, seals, signatures, issue dates and marginal notes.
- Continuation pages, schedules and material endorsements.

**Precision rule:** If a source entry is genuinely illegible, it should be marked as such rather than guessed. If the source record is wrong, the issuing authority—not the translator—must correct it.

## Can you translate a 14-column Fard without losing its structure?

**Yes.** When your Fard contains 14 columns, the English document can follow that sequence and preserve the corresponding rows. We may use **landscape A4 or A3 pages**, split wider tables into clearly labelled continuations, or retain field numbers with source-to-translation cross-reference.

The exact layout is selected after reviewing the original because **not every Fard has 14 columns**. Our aim is legibility and traceability, not a misleading claim that an English PDF is identical to a government-issued original.

### Layout and accuracy checklist

1. Review all pages and count the original fields.
2. Map column headings before translating the data.
3. Preserve owner-to-plot relationships and all ownership shares.
4. Maintain the exact values for record numbers and land dimensions.
5. Explain uncommon revenue terms without changing their meaning.
6. Label each continuation page and review for row omissions.
7. Include legible source annotations, seals and stamps where relevant.
8. Attach an appropriate translation certificate if requested.

**Sample policy:** An approved before/after land-record sample may be shown after proper redaction and consent. No fabricated sample or client ownership record should appear on the page.

## Fard translation for UK visas and British legal matters

A UK visitor visa does **not** require every applicant to provide property papers. If the applicant chooses to submit a relevant Urdu Fard, the [GOV.UK visitor supporting-documents guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visitor-visa-guide-to-supporting-documents/guide-to-supporting-documents-visiting-the-uk) calls for a full English translation that can be independently verified. Its translation details include the accuracy confirmation, date, full name/signature and contact details of the translator.

A Fard is an ownership-related record—not proof of a specific property valuation, income or guaranteed immigration eligibility. For legal proceedings, follow the receiving solicitor or court's separate requirements.

## Property document translation for USCIS

**USCIS requires a full certified English translation for any foreign-language document submitted to it.** The translator must certify accuracy/completeness and competence under [8 CFR § 103.2(b)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-103/section-103.2). Whether a property document is even needed depends on the individual application or evidence request.

We can prepare the translated record and an appropriate translator statement; we do not make immigration-law decisions or promise that an officer will accept an underlying property claim.

## Property documents for inheritance, foreign courts and overseas lawyers

Pakistani inheritance files may contain a Fard, mutation entry, registered deed, family/relationship records, court documents and other evidence. The exact required documents depend on the matter and the foreign authority's instructions. **Revenue records, succession documents and private agreements are not interchangeable.**

A receiving lawyer may ask for a translation that tracks section numbers, owner names and document references. Where an authority requires an affidavit, notarization or document authentication, that must be arranged separately from plain translation and confirmed in the quote.

**We do not provide** legal title examination, land ownership searches, valuation certificates, notarial services or government land-record issuance through this translation page.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a Fard translation the same as a certified land ownership certificate?

No. We translate an existing land-record extract. An official ownership record must come from the competent issuing authority.

### Can you translate a Fard-e-Malkiat with 14 columns?

Yes, if the supplied document is legible. We preserve the column order and the corresponding ownership data in an appropriate layout.

### What is an Intiqal document in English?

It is generally a land-record **mutation** entry, documenting a recorded change. It is not automatically a registered sale deed.

### Do you translate Jamabandi and registry deeds?

Yes, the supplied records can be assessed and quoted individually. Full documents and all annexes should be provided where a complete translation is required.

### Do I need notarization as well as translation?

Only if the specific receiving authority requires it. Certified translation, notarization and document authentication serve different functions.

### Will a UK visa authority accept my translated Fard?

A translation prepared to the route's instructions may be submitted, but no private translation provider can guarantee how the authority will assess your documents or application.

### Can you provide a translation certificate for USCIS?

Where requested, we can prepare a translator certification addressing accuracy, completeness and competence. The applicant must still follow current USCIS instructions and any individual notices.

### Can you translate a Fard for a property inheritance matter?

Yes, as a translation task. We cannot determine inheritance rights or confirm title; those are matters for competent authorities or legal professionals.

### Can you get an official Fard from PLRA for me?

This page is for translation. Original Fard issuance and verification are official government functions; see the [Punjab Land Records Authority](https://www.punjab-zameen.gov.pk/).

### Is your translation service limited to Lahore?

No. Document review and delivery can be coordinated remotely with clients across Pakistan, subject to the agreed service terms. Do not infer a walk-in branch from a city's name on a website.

### Can I send only a cropped photograph of the relevant line?

For a complete translation, provide the full uncropped document and all pages. Cropping may hide headings, seals and material notes.

### Do you guarantee embassy approval or rejection refunds?

No universal government or embassy acceptance guarantee is given. Any error-correction or refund term must be stated accurately in the current written service terms.
